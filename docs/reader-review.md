# Simulated cold-reader review

These are editorial simulations, not human peer review. No real readers participated: named personas are simulated perspectives, not statements, approvals or endorsements by the people named. Each round calls for ten independent readings (five personas, one response per persona from each of two model families). The linked `.txt` files preserve the raw answer bytes; the summaries below distinguish what the models reported from revisions checked in the text. “Round” here means a reading round, not the historical proof releases.

## What the checks cover

- **Receipt and endpoint checks:** verifying the stored receipt and recomputing endpoint arithmetic (or doing that arithmetic from the printed table) checks transcription and the final numerical deduction, not the tree-to-row reduction or the local inequalities.
- **Fixtures and authored-code replay:** the 42 selected tests exercise finite fixtures, not either production snapshot. The short three-vertex replay is a different authored witness/checker from Gold2 and suffices for the upper bound below 17/30, not for the narrow interval or existence. The historical Gold2 rebuild matched the two snapshot digests, and strict replays matched the exact receipt using the **same** authored verifier. Running it from another model family is not a fresh implementation.
- **Independent work:** separately written checks and derivations covered selected rows, envelopes, the tail and physical-tree cases, plus scoped reviews of the mathematical reduction. They were not a second exhaustive implementation of the 767,588,844 finite rows per side and did not verify every theorem. See the [proof and checker map](proof-map.md) for attribution and scope.
