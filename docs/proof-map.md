# Theorem, proof and checker map

[Paper (PDF)](../paper/friend-tree-exponent.pdf) · [LaTeX](../paper/friend-tree-exponent.tex) · [Historical report](../REPORT.md)

The PDF, LaTeX and this map are from the current checkout. The archive links below point to `a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c`, which preserves the underlying proof and verification records. A proof review, an independent implementation and a replay of the author's code are not the same: a replay confirms a verdict on specified bytes, and a finite fixture does not establish a limit theorem.

## The shorter route to 17/30

The round-one upper certificate already gives $`\mu\le1133/2000=17/30-1/6000`$. It assigns weights to core motifs of at most three vertices; the [analytic argument](https://github.com/uzay-eth/friend-tree-exponent/blob/a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c/proofs/bracket-motifs.md) bounds the expected change in total weight and turns that one-step inequality into a bound on the mean core size. The count alone does not determine its drift: a vertex can create a new non-leaf only by choosing one of its leaf neighbours, so the fractions of leaf neighbours matter.

To replay just the shipped upper witness from the repository root, use Bash, GCC with OpenMP, GMP development headers, and `xz`:

```bash
(
  set -e
  out=$(mktemp -d)
  trap 'rm -rf "$out"' EXIT
  gcc -O3 -fopenmp verify/motif-bracket/cert_verify_gmp.c -lgmp -o "$out/verify"
  xz -dc verify/motif-bracket/reference/upper.bin.xz > "$out/upper.bin"
  OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 "$out/verify" "$out/upper.bin"
)
```

The last line was `=== SUMMARY: 17329108 exact GMP inequality checks, 0 failures => VERIFIED OK ===`. This replays the authored verifier on the round-one upper witness only; it does not establish existence of the exponent or reproduce the sharper enclosure. For the round-one lower bound, replace `upper` with `lower` in the last two commands; the lower replay also passed 17,329,108 checks with zero failures. These are one-sided comparisons: $`\Phi_{\rm up}\ge c_0 C`$ and $`\Phi_{\rm lo}\le C_0 C`$ for positive constants $`c_0,C_0`$, not two-sided bounds on each potential. Both start positive at the three-vertex path; the lower potential need not be positive on every tree.

## Where computation enters

- The logarithmic exponent and its almost-sure identification in Theorem 1 are analytic; the two numerical bounds in Theorem 1 and the sharper enclosure in Theorem 2 use exact computer checks.
- Theorems 3–5, 7 and 8 use the round-one lower witness, not the five-vertex enclosure: $`\mu\ge113/200>51/100`$ is needed for the renewal argument. Theorem 4 also uses $`2\mu>1`$ in its second-moment and positivity arguments.
- Theorem 9 can use the earlier sublinear almost-sure core bound ([Addario-Berry et al., Prop. 6.22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.20185)) for summability, so it needs neither drift certificate. Theorems 10–11 use separate exact rational checks; Theorem 12 uses exact finite enumeration and continuation bounds.

**Checking the enclosure.** From the repository root, run `python3 -B paper/checks/check_literals.py --enclosure` to authenticate the receipt, recompute the exact and outward-rounded endpoints, and compare them with the printed values. It does not run the row verifier. The separate [production rebuild and strict verification](https://github.com/uzay-eth/friend-tree-exponent/blob/a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c/round2/verify/gold/RESULTS.md) matched both snapshot digests and the [exact endpoint receipt](https://github.com/uzay-eth/friend-tree-exponent/blob/a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c/round2/proofs/neon2/quintet-cert/certificate_gold2.json) byte for byte. An independent sampler checked selected rows, envelopes, the tail and adversarial trees, not every production row. Use the `gold2` preset for Theorem 2; the [broader fixture suite](https://github.com/uzay-eth/friend-tree-exponent/blob/a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c/round2/verify/README.md) does not rebuild or verify either full snapshot.

**Further amplitude results.** The archived [right-tail proof](https://github.com/uzay-eth/friend-tree-exponent/blob/a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c/round2/proofs/astra-late/source/round2/open/limit-law/right/README.md) gives $`-\log\Pr(X>x)\sim I x^{1/(1-\mu)}`$ for a finite positive $`I`$ via a finite-block variational formula. It uses the amplitude, a uniform one-leaf gradient for finite-time means, the embedded martingale and right-tail order; it is not a consequence of absolute continuity. [Gemini audited the exact rate and formula under the cited inputs](https://github.com/uzay-eth/friend-tree-exponent/blob/a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c/round2/reviews/attacks/neon-on-astra-17.md); Claude checked the tail orders, not the exact rate. No numerical value of $`I`$ is certified.

**Priority and review.** Long's [23 November draft](https://github.com/long-mathematics/friend-tree-growth/tree/090bbbe5faec83ec4124bd427eb5fda32c79bef1) characterizes the exponent by discounted generation sums, using a kernel on full birth environments within the inert-parent process, and proves termination of a two-sided approximation search. Its separate refinement of Uzay’s round-one witness gives $`\mu\le0.5664485422<17/30`$. Theorem 3 uses a different, marked-fringe kernel, and Theorem 7 supplies phase-averaged tables and an explicit error rate. Long’s [24 November draft](https://github.com/long-mathematics/friend-tree-growth/tree/5bd25a7d506cb64a8444b86334ab531d295f6c78) gives constant-factor mean growth and develops his transcendence programme; Theorem 5 gives a sharp equivalent and Theorem 4 a pathwise amplitude. Evaluating Long’s signed renewal germ at one gives the scalar $`B_s(1)`$ agrees with the response $`U_s(J)`$ above the critical discount; [transcendence of that function of the discount](https://github.com/uzay-eth/friend-tree-exponent/blob/a0972085ad72ad5685ae7b424988648d1965e73c/round2/proofs/neon2/obstruction/REPORT.md) does not decide the arithmetic of the exponent.
