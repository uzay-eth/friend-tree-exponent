RESULT IN ONE SENTENCE
In the random friend tree, the number of non-leaves grows like n^mu, both in mean and on almost every tree. A computer-checked interval puts 0.565713486333 <= mu <= 0.565736986679, which rounds to Krapivsky and Redner's 0.566 and excludes 17/30.
