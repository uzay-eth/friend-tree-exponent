**(c)** Section 5:

> "Long introduced the signed scalar Bs(1) in his transcendence programme [9] … For real s > µ, use continuation along the positive real generation variable to define Bs(1) = 1 − 1/Fs(1)"

What I introduced is the germ B_s(z) = 1 − 1/F_s(z) near z = 0, with an explicit signed Taylor expansion. I studied its boundary value at s = µ as z ↑ 1 along the real axis (lim B_µ(z) = 1).
