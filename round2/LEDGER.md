# Claim ledger, 24 November 2026

The register covers 28 Astra claims, four Neon claims, 12 Neon‑2 claims and seven Fable claims. A row sometimes separates conclusions with different evidence. Carbon closed before making a numbered claim. The family in the author column is the family that supplied the proof or computation. It can differ from the lane lead.

**A** means a written or computer-assisted argument received scoped HOLDS verdicts from two families other than its author, or from one other family together with an exact independent reimplementation by another family. **B** means one other family checked the stated scope. **C** covers author-family-only checks, missing verdicts and unrepaired gaps. Unqualified mathematical statements record proofs in the source lane. Partial checks, empirical findings, withdrawals and superseded statements are identified in the row. A conditional grade applies to the implication with its stated inputs. Numerical diagnostics have no grade as proofs of an asymptotic exponent. Reproduction of original certificate bytes is recorded separately.

The attack column links all 83 individual reports. A report's **HOLDS** or **GAP** applies to its pinned revision and stated scope, not to an entire row or a later repair. Superseded reports, inherited-input checks and reports marked archival only are retained for the record, not counted as fresh verdicts. Unarchived audit mentions do not count. A second replay using the same verifier is a replay, not a reimplementation. A review from the same model family, even in a different lane, adds no outside family. Neon‑2 inherited Astra’s morning snapshot; using an inherited lemma adds no independent check of that lemma. The individual reports preserve disagreements and the distinction between proof review and finite tests. [Review archive](reviews/README.md)
