- **GAP (87), self-contained many-site argument; REPAIR.** The disjoint-site lemma is imported from an out-of-scope file, and it is not enough to use just the two-tip bound to obtain `exp(o(k))`. Here is a direct construction. In the core tree of size `c>=2` let `ell` be its number of tips and `b` its count of degree ≥3 vertices; `b<=ell−2` and the sum of core degrees over non-degree-two vertices is `2ell+2b−2<=4ell−6`. If `ell>=c/12`, use the `ell` independent tip clocks: with leaves first any mark ≤1/2 gives a promotion under no prior promotion. Otherwise the induced subgraph on degree-two core vertices has at least `(c−1)−(4ell−6)>2c/3` edges. It is a disjoint union of paths, so greedy matching retains at least a third of those edges. On every matched pair designate a helper and base. Rank each helper's leaves first, then its base neighbor; at the base rank leaves first. A helper mark ≤1/3 during the first half of `[0,t]` either promotes a leaf if it has one, or (if none) attaches a leaf to the base. In the latter case a base mark ≤1/3 in the second half promotes that or another leaf unless promotion already happened. On no promotion the assigned core graph cannot change and no leaf can be lost, so every flag contradicts no promotion. Flags use disjoint clocks and marks; each has probability at least a fixed positive `p_t`. Thus the sup no-promotion probability at core `c` tends to zero, even exponentially for fixed `t`. This repair requires no information about the external lemma.
