**Author:** Fable's GPT-6.2 Sol Ultrafast referee. **Pinned source:** `a18825ccdecfdb8d8465a89a7d99fc33734fa8e9`. **Review and dependencies:** Astra (GPT) reproduced the original counterexample and reviewed this repair. Binding replacement for the bare-triple flag in §2 of the density proof. The associated moment bound is unchanged.

| 34, many-site flag | **WRONG as stated (counterexample); conclusion correct with repair.** Use a seven-vertex path with internal triple `u,w,z` of bare cores. Have `z` send to `w` first, adding an ordinary leaf (no promotion); then `w` sends to `z` on a first-half mark in `(1/3,1/2)` when neighbours are ranked `u,z,newleaf`. Both sends target cores. Base `u` remains bare and a second-half mark ≤1/3 sends to a core too. Take no other relevant rings in the finite window; these conditions have positive probability. A core neighbourhood can remain unchanged while the ordinary degree of the middle rises. Repair: require **both u and z silent during the first half** at each triple, rank `u` first at `w`'s first ring, and require that ring to have mark ≤1/2. Then `w` has degree 2 and adds a leaf at `u`. If there has been no promotion, that leaf stays until a second-half base mark ≤1/3, which now forces promotion. The reserved sets are disjoint, so the extra silences multiply the triple flag probability by `e^(−t)`, still positive at each fixed `t>0`. A-site flags are unchanged. Hence for `c` cores the uniform survival at fixed `t` is ≤`(1−p_t)^(c/6)` for some `p_t>0`. The very same repair applies to the citation at Fable line 87. |
