## Extra first-step serial credit

At completion size `m=b+1`, the serial middle starts at degree two and tracked stock one. The Poisson-stock subharmonic recurrence charges a loss at rate `1/m`; the physical loss probability on the next jump is `1/(2m)`. An extra parent contribution to gains can only help by increasing stock. Thus the one-step drift gives an extra `H(m+1,1;x)/(2m)` beyond `H(m,1;x)`. With `q=1−x`, the normalized coefficient after dividing by `q^b` is at least

```
M (1 + q/(2m)).
```

On a threshold box use its smallest `q` for this credit. The favourable parallel bound is still computed with the unboosted postpromotion lower. The physical adverse continuation, opposite-side credits, receive corners, and chain check come from the quarter checker. Its local tariff test uses `(a, base, fee, terminal fee, transfer)=(.004,.004,.005,.009,.002)`. Box subdivision is rational and includes both endpoints of each interval. Portions of a finite box above `5/(b+1)` are automatically covered by the symbolic bound for `b≥21`; for `b=20` they lie in the separately checked unboosted box `[3/20,1/2]`.
