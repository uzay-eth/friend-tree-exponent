## Real neighbor dynamics

The descriptor is `(D,c,m,t)` with the original focal degree, and physical core degree `c` including its mother. At genuine birth `(D,c)=(2,1)`; the mother remains the same actual vertex at all ages. The mother is either tracked at exact degree or represented by a persistent lower-degree bound. In the measured run track degree 2 exactly until it increases, then use `m>=3` forever. At an exact mother of degree `m`, the focal sends to her at rate `1/D`; each of her `m-2` other slots besides her own parent and the focal can send at at most 1, and her parent sends at most `1/2`. Therefore her degree gain is in `[1/D,1/D+m-3/2]`. Her own promotions do not change the tracked degree. All cases exclude a founder mother; the number of direct founder births is at most linear in mean and their uniformly bounded discounted contribution vanishes.

A promotion of the focal still occurs at its **true** rate `b=L/D`; it advances `c` and selects the new real child at `(2,1)` as the latest child. The preceding latest child is neither removed nor reset; it becomes part of the exterior remainder. Between promotions, let the latest child's live census be `(j,i)` for total and core degree. Its gain is in `[j-i+1/D, j-i+1/D+(i-1)/2]` and its arity increases at exact rate `(j-i)/j`. At degree above the tracking ceiling it retains a live reciprocal bound `0..1/(T+1)` until the next focal birth. In the measured run `T=3`. Untracked physical children contribute a separate `[0,1/2]` apiece. Thus the known sends are `1/m` and `1/j` wherever tracked; their sum plus the exterior receive is used **both** in the reward and in focal gain `L+rho`. Reciprocal sends on tracked edges in the reverse direction use the same focal degree `D`. A transition that is not visible on these descriptors has zero increment; no Markov or independence assumption is made about hidden rates.

The robust solve checks both comparison directions. For each descriptor the holding quotient is `(F+sum rates*successor)/(discount+sum rates)`. Starting with the lower rates, order the optional rate increments by their successor value, using the successor **plus the original reward slope** for focal receive. Adding the next increment improves the quotient exactly when its value is on the favorable side of the current quotient. After the first non-improving increment, all later increments are non-improving by ordering. Integer rounding is directed, and 750 sampled multi-port corner inequalities per main solve are checked by exhaustive enumeration. The unconditioned fallback and conditional comparison price the same physical remainder and can safely be combined by `max` for a floor or `min` for a ceiling. Conditioning is used through degree 360 and `c<=18`; beyond either seam use the original unconditioned continuation at the actual census. The full lower replay retains the original degree-12000/arity-30 analytic suffix; the full upper replays the degree-6000/arity-64 supersolution and retains the same rigorous arity-overflow term `65000*S/(1+LO)^64`. The measured conditional solves visit 72,322 rows per bound/witness; their mother-only controls use 12,652.

The conditional effect reaches the dominant moderate degree band: e.g. at `(180,4)` the original lower-mode receive slope is about `+9.124e7` ticks per unit receive; upper mode it is `−9.032e7`. Lower/upper lifetime comparisons therefore benefit from keeping both directions of the possible receive changes, rather than assuming an arbitrary independent receive every time. The D=2000 analytic reward remains in the inherited continuation; it is never replaced by a new birth or multiplied by an invented arrival probability.

## True birth mixing and evidence

Partition the physical newborn law by mother degree **at birth**. Each nonfounder can contribute one degree-2 mother birth, from its newborn residence `(2,1)`: once it leaves that residence, it can never promote again at degree 2. In the residence the wanted hazard is `1/2`; the competing leaf gain hazard is in `[1,3/2]`, since it includes its own mother send. The actual discounted probability of such a birth is bounded between `(1/2)/(HI+2)` and `(1/2)/(LO+3/2)`. The physical stationary birth measure is invariant under the discount-`mu` offspring birth kernel: away from the initial founder, each actual birth has exactly one physical mother. Equivalently, count these degree-2 maternal births by compensation and divide by the total discounted births. This gives the same bounds for their physical newborn fraction, without renormalizing the class and without assuming that the virgin exit clock is independent of its exterior. The two conditional lifetime bounds are mixed over this common fraction range, then multiplied once by `LO` for the floor or `HI` for the ceiling. The upper overflow estimate is added after mixing. For a control, omitting the fraction constraint and taking worst class alone still gives `[577176.132891310,4241748.000760550]` and `[824533.688652820,4480494.373735722]` on the two witnesses.
