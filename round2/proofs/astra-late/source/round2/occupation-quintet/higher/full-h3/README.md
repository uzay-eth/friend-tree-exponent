## Physical reward and registration

`Reader.h3_physical()` uses the pinned original snapshots and production nominal discount. It returns the exact outward row intercept and uncovered gain slopes, in payload ticks. For each covered exterior incidence it keeps the centered receive term (subtracting the outgoing transfer from the original physical gain), and for each deleted tip it adds the exact incoming edge correction. A newborn extension uses the post-promotion arity and unchanged degree. The row is charged once at the middle; covered rates still use actual exterior hazards in the cohort generator. This credits only `R=[−P]₊`, so `P+R≤K` for the original nonnegative cap `K≥P` regardless of the size of `R`. Distinct H3 and P4 row rewards remain distinct; use the earlier strict-chain allocation and this lane's exclusion as written.

One length-one prefix starts at each nonfounder birth. On a promotion a prefix persists with updated arity; it also creates a longer prefix if the event is at the prescribed parent. A chain has parent sequence `(0,1)`; a crown `(0,0)` labels the children in birth order and registers at the younger child's birth. A focused upstream mode uses `(0,1,2)`, with the first vertex exterior to the rewarded H3. Every nonfounder upstream parent is unique. Unregistered core neighbors still contribute to gain; they never prevent extra promotions. In an old/young pair of passes, `--split` gates the young-target pass on old-tip degree `<19`, so the same H3 cannot pay twice. For crowns this priority is chronological, not degree ordering. Founder-containing H3s are excluded. Labels are killed at monotone arity cuts or nonpersistent degree cuts. No age is killed.

An unpriced arm's persistent overflow is `D≥L+1` with exact arity and reciprocal `[0,1/(L+1)]`. Its degree gains leave the abstract state unchanged, regardless of their rate. Its promotions advance arity with rate in `[max(0,1−c/(L+1)),1]`. Gains of its retained neighbors use the same reciprocal range; for the explicit overflowed upstream parent that rate also enters the rewarded physical mother row, with the same variable. Allowing the overflow promotion rate independently is an outer relaxation. The other H3 tip remains in the generator after overflow, but that entire interval includes out-of-mask `D≥1999` and gets zero reward. `tail_row` evaluates the exact physical expression in reciprocal interval arithmetic beyond the G2 cutoff. This interval check by itself does not certify occupation or a nonzero reward for the permanent overflow.

At exact labels the coupled solver uses one exterior rate per gain jump, shared with the reward slope; the sibling-box option splits off only that reward contribution. The minimum/maximum of the linear-fractional ratio is at half-box vertices after cutting at `P=0`. With no sign crossing the affine numerator admits the ordered threshold solve in `coupled()`. Rounded local inequalities are checked with exact rationals. With potentials scaled by `2²⁰` ticks, the floor uses `HI` discount and `LO` seed intensity; the ceiling reverses these and rounds up. Summing over registered prefixes telescopes the continuation and birth terms from the same physical events. Normalizing by core count gives the reported all-age values.

## Joint ledger with the additional-child port

For a mother-high occurrence with `n=c_mother−2≥1` other children, let `u` be her actual parent's reciprocal, `s` the sum of those children's actual reciprocals, and `b` the physical mother exterior slope. Hold every other physical coordinate fixed. Define `P_box=P−b s+max(0,b n/2)` and `R_box=[−P_box]₊`. Then `P_box≥P`, and the sibling-only increment is `Δ_sib=R−R_box≥0`. **The same actual `u` and all other rates must appear in both clips.** `--sibling-box` uses precisely `R_box` while keeping the physical sibling hazard in the transition. Explicit fourth-parent runs can improve bounds on `R_box` by using the upstream reciprocal without taking any part of `Δ_sib`.

If an upstream baseline covers only a subset `U` of the H3 support and the sibling increment covers another subset `V`, then `1_U R_box+1_V Δ_sib≤R` occurrencewise. This applies whether `U` and `V` intersect or not. The fourth-parent and three-vertex sibling-box runs here are **alternative lower bounds** on overlapping baselines, so do not add them. An alternative mixed ledger may use the full direct floor on an explicitly disjoint support and the replacement baseline plus sibling increment here. Do not add the sibling increment to a full direct price of the same support. See `../../diagnostic/H3-ORIENTATION.md` for the empirical orientation split; those measured rates were not used as hazard restrictions.

On any `c_mother≥3` occurrence the sibling replay prices only `Δ_sib`; where our `38..65` baseline also pays, the two are bounded by `R_box+Δ_sib=R`.
