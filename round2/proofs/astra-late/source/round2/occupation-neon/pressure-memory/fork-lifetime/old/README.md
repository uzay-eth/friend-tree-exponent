## True fringe geometry and the reserve

The crown topology is apex → trunk → two tips; apex has one physical
parent outside the fringe. An eligible full true planted apex fringe
has size `>20` or has size `>12` and at least five cores. From the four
tagged roles alone let `m=Σk−3`. There are exactly `m` distinct immediate
off-crown core children. A core child brings itself and either an ordinary
leaf or a further descendant, so it brings at least two vertices. Thus
`size≥Σ(D−k)+2m` and `cores≥4+m`. For a registered exterior child of
census `(d,k)`, replace that child's two-vertex minimum by `d+k` and
credit `k` more core descendants. These sets do not intersect other
immediate branches. At donor degree overflow retain its attained minimum
degree and arity for geometry; retain `[0,1/d_cross]` for its receive rate.
The mother is not a fringe descendant and adds nothing to these counts.
The fixture verifies a fringe that is certified outside **only** by the
registered donor's descendants.

A row is paid only if these lower counts prove the true predicate. The
test therefore misses true eligible crowns whose hidden descendants
would be needed for the proof. It excludes both the size-12 block and
the size-13–20 block on at most four cores. The high-parent EDGE reserve
pays `g,hC`, and `e911a1c` adds `P4,S3,Yb`; none is this `Yt` allocation.
Other exploratory Yt masks, including old-flow and the staged higher
corrector, are not asserted disjoint. Do not stack this calculation with
those or a whole-row price on the same occurrences. No endpoint is booked.

## Additive donor identity and live rates

For each original D18 witness the scanner returns
`a + Σ_port c_port Σ_donor p`, with `p=1/D_donor`. Subtract the independent
box floor `a + Σ n_port min(0,c_port/2)`, which the replay checks is
nonnegative on the queried labels. The remaining charge is **exactly**
the sum over physical donors of `c p−min(0,c/2)`, a nonnegative term.
Pay one such term in each marked incidence; the independent box floor
is not included. The mother is present from the apex's actual birth;
her send points downward to the apex, while trunk and side children
send upward to their parent. Each selected child's lifetime begins at
its own real promotion. Its send, gain and promotion are separate; its
send changes the receiver without aging its own reciprocal. After its
degree cap, its exact history is replaced by the irreversible bound.
Other donors keep live, adapted receive intervals. The mother source
uses an exact monitored degree-two bin and a physical upper mass
`(1/2)/(LOW+3/2)` for births with degree-two mother. A degree-two birth
requires the virgin census, with promotion hazard `1/2` and total
departure hazard at least `3/2`. The remaining birth mass pays the
appropriate worst live older-mother continuation, not a reset.

Each trunk is selected at birth, then its two tips in birth order.
The older selected tip registers once; the younger birth completes the
pair. If a promotion can introduce either a side donor or a crown tag,
its actual jump carries two separate registrations, each taking exactly
one role. This covers side births before, between and after the two
tip births. There is no factor of two for equal tip labels. The replay's
physical-ID fixture verifies exact incidence multiplicities and forbids
assigning one physical newborn to two labels. After completion, a tip
swap also swaps the tagged donor attachment and row slope. All internal
reciprocals remain exact; the one donor send uses the same rate in the
running reward and its receiver's gain successor and holding denominator.
The integer drift residual is checked at both adverse endpoints on each
row. Mask exits are irreversible in tagged degree and arity. Stable age
normalizes the all-time discounted sum by the common physical exponent.

## What limits this pilot

* The smaller `(8,5,4,4)/(3,3,1,1)` authenticated rerun gives only
  `9.2736060e−8 / 1.31361511e−7` in donor floors. The retained physical
  state count grows fastest for the two tip sources: `36,508` to
  `501,548` on the medium cut. The medium tip job alone used 71.62 CPU s.
* On wider tip degree caps `(14,5,7,7)/(4,3,2,2)`, the census-certified
  box demand `Σ n|c|/2` is at least `1.387862708e−6 / 1.564939249e−6`.
  On that **same census-only mask**, the free-input physical excess
  certificate is only `[1.00659881e−7,2.767644163e−6]` and
  `[5.579784e−9,2.881208998e−6]`. There is enough algebraic demand;
  it is not a lower bound on realizable excess. With tip arity one and
  donor cap two, the wider tip-degree run paid `1.12915740e−7 /
  2.26781766e−7` for the tip sources; it is a different mask and cannot
  be added to the medium run.
* A rigorous ceiling for **all eligible true geometries within the medium
  tagged caps**, even those the lower-count predicate misses, comes from
  dropping the geometry gate. Nonnegative excess is then at most
  `4.233356687e−6 / 4.301504190e−6`. This ceiling is loose and does not
  rule out the target, either within these caps or on the full complement.
  The same solve gives the larger box-demand ceilings `4.552443418e−6 /
  6.025890977e−6`. A larger recovery would need tighter live pressure at
  the trunk and tips while retaining evidence of additional descendants,
  rather than just a larger unconditioned receive box.
