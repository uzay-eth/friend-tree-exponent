## What the 19-mark rank-one formulas give

Let `J_i` have permanent mark `1/i`, `R` be the residual generator with all direct births set aside, `G_s=(s-R)^(-1)`, and `J` evaluate at all 19 newborns. Use the true rates `p=1/D` and `a_Y−p` for the two child copies (with one incoming gain in the `p` copy). No degree cap is imposed on physical send rates. Write `K_s=C_s+k_s e_2^T`, splitting out **direct** births from virgins only, and `e_2` for label 2. At criticality the positive left vector can be taken as `ell=e_2^T(I−C_mu)^(-1)`, since `ell k_mu=T(mu)=1`; the left equation follows by expanding `ell K_mu`. The occupation with trigger productions removed is `ell J G_mu`: expand `(I−C_mu)^(-1)` to sum all successive untriggered direct generations, each followed by residual occupation. The source survives trigger production; this is removal of the emitted newborn term, not killing its promoter. This resolvent occupation is not a probability without normalization. The scale of `ell` may be chosen separately, e.g. `ell 1=1`.

The [spatial first-moment limit](../../../../../astra/source/round2/fringe/spatial-limit.md) identifies the normalized fringe occupation `ν(F)=(ell J G_mu F)/(ell J G_mu 1)`. Oriented observables in this note are by reconstruction on the distinguished ancestor fringe in §3 of that proof; a one-body root test or a downward motif count is already a test of the descendant fringe. In particular `ν(b)=π(b)=mu`, by the physical cell-generator identity with the constant score. Also `ell J G_mu b=ell K_mu 1=ell 1`. Therefore `ν(F)=mu (ell J G_mu F)/(ell 1)`; this exact normalization applies to the residual occupation, while the real newborn Palm law below is an independent representation. For bounded nonnegative root-mark-blind tests one has `J G_s F(i) ≥ J G_s F(2)`: for each chronological residual path, changing the initial root mark alters only that root's death in its own residence denominators; when a copy cuts to a child, the original root disappears and the rates and marks in the copy remain the same. This proves `ν(F) ≥ LO * J G_HI F(2)` by monotonicity, where `LO` and `HI` are the incoming joint endpoints. For bounded motif counts the same argument applies including multiplicity.

The replay sums a finite positive chronological prefix (0–10 transitions, size at most eight, 2,922 terminal types) from `J_2`, rounding path contributions down. Example lower masses per core: `(D,k)=(2,0)` 0.221317004789267, `(3,0)` 0.066506043550222, `(4,0)` 0.029375567171604; the count of virgin-child edges from `(2,1)` is at least 0.031137436097949, and pairs of two virgin children at a `(3,2)` apex at least 0.000775329400648. This is a count per core, including pair multiplicity, not a Palm probability for cherries sampled from the cherry population. The renewal-only first-body rewards from this prefix are at least `1.270821798e−6` and `1.023781866e−6`. No cherry or edge slack is charged in the final calculation. The other nonnegative GMP motif rows are discarded, without spending the first-body surplus on a compensating edge floor.

## Stronger: integrate the physical root at every age

By [the birth-time identity](../../../../../astra/source/round2/degree-law.md), `π(r)=mu E_beta int exp(-mu t) r(X_t) dt`, with `beta` the physical promoter-biased newborn law in the full tree. The newborn always starts at `(D,k)=(2,0)`. For this tracked vertex, `k` is the number of core children, `L=D−k−1`, and the two jumps are `(D+1,k)` at `g=L+ρ` and `(D,k+1)` at `b=L/D`. The physical receive rate satisfies `0≤ρ≤(k+1)/2` even conditioned on the entire past. In a virgin row this immediately gives `π(D=2,k=0)≥mu/(mu+2)`. The replay keeps both jumps at all rows; it does not assume receiving is constant or independent of the descendant states.

Let `r(D,k,ρ)` be the normalized signed first-body charge from the [sparse rows](../rows/README.md). Use the exact inner/finite-tail row through degree 64; above 64 use the all-stock floors for `k=0..4` and zero elsewhere. For `D≤64` evaluate backwards in decreasing `D`, then decreasing `k`:

```
V(D,k) = min_{ρ in {0,(k+1)/2}} [r(D,k,ρ) + b V(D,k+1) + g V(D+1,k)] / (HI + b + g).
```

The expression is fractional linear in `ρ`, hence the minimum is attained at an endpoint. Minimizing the sum keeps the actual rate in the reward coupled to its occurrence in the generator. For `D≥65` take `V(D,k)=v_k` constant in `D`. Gains cancel; `v_k` is the backward minimum of `(r_k + b v_(k+1))/(HI+b)` over `1−(k+1)/65≤b≤1` for the supported bands, with `v_5=0`. This gives `(HI−A_track)V≤r` pointwise, including for adaptive exterior input, where `A_track` acts on the same root. Apply discounted Dynkin from the physical newborn, localize and let time tend to infinity; `V` is bounded and the terminal term vanishes. Multiplying the resulting occupation by `LO` gives a lower bound under `π`. Downward rounding by `10^−21` preserves these inequalities. The all-time tail and sparse rewards are on disjoint bins. A separate affine-floor plus discounted-excess replay takes `π(k)=1` from finite edge counting and uniform integrability (or the birth-rate identity), and adds the floor only once; it is weaker than the direct all-time bound.

## The founder, universal C0, and the average denominator

The founder has `h=0`. Finite trees satisfy the signed drift decomposition with the founder included; the first-body lower reward above is assigned only to `h=1`. The founder itself and the first few generations from it cost bounded-depth expected counts `O(1+t^m)` for fixed `m`, since each core promotes at rate at most one. Their proportion relative to the mean core size therefore vanishes. In particular the exceptional parent flags and missing ancestor motifs do not affect the limiting mean. The image of the founder in the generating immigration formula supplies the common leading scalar in the fringe limit; a founder visit is not a stationary newborn row. Each local score used for the potential is bounded after allocating every motif to a single vertex, as in the [Neon comparison](../../../../../../reviews/cold/astra/occupation-neon/check/neon-proof-pin.md#core-comparison-bounds-and-code-map); the apex arities are cutoff and each bottom motif has bounded multiplicity. The spatial limit then gives `E Φ(T_t)/E C(T_t) → barΦ`, in the certificate normalization. The pinned GMP comparison gives universal `C0` of `1.84124847315` and `1.95820675182`, and positive lower comparisons for both sides; see `outputs/production-final/{lo,hi}.log` in the tracked receipt archive (`round2/attacks/neon1/outputs/neon1-production-rehearsal.tar.gz`, historical reference).

The pointwise `C0` is obtained by maximizing the sum of motif charge ceilings at a vertex, so each individual grouped charge also obeys it. For seven inner nonfounder states with `k≤1` there are no apex charges; `denominator.py` obtains exact stronger bounds by maximizing the four bottom slots over all eligible tabulated states, including tail control states. The convexity of the Bernstein embeddings makes these bounds valid for actual tail neighbours, and the `Yb` charge is bounded by the maximum weighted by `k_apex−1`. Zero is included for optional motifs. The mandatory edge need not include zero for a nonfounder. Run the same physical root calculation on the nonnegative reward `C0−local ceiling` on these seven bins and zero elsewhere. This yields certified upper bounds for `barΦ` below `1.458909469275164` (lower witness) and `1.518769126087491` (upper witness). The denominator calculation uses the potential charges, not the signed drift charges.

To convert drift to endpoints, put `F(t)=E Φ(T_t)` and let `M(t)` be the physical mean core count. The mean rank-one limits give `e^(−mu t) M(t)→m>0`, `F(t)/M(t)→barΦ>0`, and the normalized mean nonnegative reward converges to `m π(r)` after multiplication by `e^(−mu t)`. Integrate the signed inequality for `F′(t)−λ F(t)` over `[t,t+1]`, divide by `M(t)`, and pass to the limit. The common positive time integral cancels and gives `sign*(mu−λ) barΦ ≥ π(r)`. The sign is `+1` at the Neon lower endpoint and `−1` at its upper endpoint. Larger certified denominators are conservative on **both** sides. Exact replay gives:

The lower Neon endpoint starts `0.000000570322` below the incoming Astra lower endpoint, so the gain against the joint bracket is smaller than the lower entry in the last column. The order-`10^−5` target is not attained by these first-body rewards.
