The exact replay checks these bills simultaneously with every other payment in the ledger, including the direct high-donor vouchers. This pays **all** suppressed chains with a low endpoint, including low–low and terminal–terminal chains.

At a retained vertex with actual neighbour list, reserve the maximum of the existing outgoing excess and the new chain bill on each physical degree-two slot; no slot is both a direct edge and a suppressed path. Check all low-degree neighbour multisets with a high symbol. Its actual reciprocal varies in `[0,1/10]`; the local credit minus the sum of these maxima is concave in the aggregate high reciprocal. The two endpoint profiles therefore suffice. The minimum leftover is `5.4786823e−6` at degree two and `.00002` at degree three. In these tests any direct degree-two terminal also gets the more conservative maximum. Degree-three/four donor receipts are added only for actual direct high slots, never across a chain.

For a chain with `k` bare degree-two vertices, let `x,y` be the reciprocal degrees of its actual retained endpoints. If `k=1` its only coefficient is `(x+y)/6`; otherwise its terminal coefficients are `(1/2+x)/6,(1/2+y)/6`, with interior coefficients `1/6`. Each vertex earns `2 G(3)` times its coefficient; bare–bare edges pay the exactly paired slope `U(3,0)−L(3)` times the absolute coefficient difference. For a low endpoint retain its actual outgoing upper, favourable tip lower and signed difference against the incident bare coefficient. For a high endpoint use joint `.012` outgoing exposure, and on the opposite orientation retain the high tip lower up to degree 30 (zero thereafter). Given the endpoint degrees and stock, optimizing the low coefficient is checking its two endpoints, since the debit is convex in that coefficient. Those endpoints use remaining reciprocal sums zero and `(c−1)/2` with the chain slot fixed at `1/2`. The high tail reciprocal `[0,1/31]` also only needs its endpoint values by convexity. These optimizations do not change real degrees or stocks in the physical charge.
