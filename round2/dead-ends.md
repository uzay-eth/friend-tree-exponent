# Routes stopped in round 2

Work stopped on 24 November. The estimates and counterexamples below concern particular methods or proposed intermediate statements.
### Astra: failed routes, corrections, and unproved extensions

- **Degree-only closures.** Selecting the recipient by degree would give the star centre probability `1/2`; the actual probability is `(n−1)/n`. Averaging the missing core-neighbour rate per vertex or per core edge gives respectively `0.57982073` and `0.56884248`, outside the proven enclosure. A related pointwise obstruction rules out an exact positive-rate eigenfunction comparable to core count on every seed using only additive vertex degrees and oriented adjacent degree pairs (`653a258`). It does not rule out the local drift inequalities used for certificates.
- **Finite spectral approximations.** At block budget `B=1` the `M=3` and `M=4` spectra lie on opposite sides of the certified interval, so they do not give monotone one-sided bounds. The marked-environment powers and direct rational renewal size cuts have slow certified errors; the small cuts do not resolve both near-critical endpoint signs. The all-label three-core positive continuation reports a row-action interval `[0.532636730440, 2.200177068224]` for mark 20 at `0.565728`, not a spectral-radius enclosure. The intrinsic depth-32 first-hit error `≤1.9307147e−9` does not bound the remaining signed continuation.

### Fable

- **D1 — extrapolating exact small means.** The available 19 exact terms do not resolve the noninteger corrections. At size 20 the local exponent is still ~0.0176 high and Neville columns wander between 0.5696 and 0.5755. An additive offset yields an `n^(-μ)` relative correction, rather than the presumed integer powers of `1/n`.
- **D2 — lumped lookup-table generator.** Roughly 57,000 cells from 10 million sampled vertices lost to the plain cross-fitted estimator. Rare neighbor multisets and discontinuous hub indicators were especially unstable; per-vertex least squares also ignored neighboring-vertex correlations. Smooth features and run-level estimating equations reduced variance without supplying a deterministic bias bound.
- **D3 — irrationality and closed form.** Rational-discount history denominators grow faster than the relevant tail decays in the raw expansions; an explicit two-step loop already fails the 3-adic term test at 13/23 and 99/175. This does not rule out regrouped identities, and functional transcendence does not determine the arithmetic of a root. The inverse-symbolic searches found no match distinguishable from chance at the tested heights. The friend-tree kernel scan is too coarse and noisy to certify digits or a pole-free strip. A denominator lower bound is only interval arithmetic, not irrationality.
- **D4 — bounded-history alternating transport.** The five-vertex broom gives a positive earlier-root shape with no comparable younger-root partner, for every fixed lookback. This rules out a conditional proof over that class of seeds, not the unconditional order (mixing histories can eliminate the obstruction).
- **D5 — co-adapted histories and first promotion.** The tested whole-history couplings that preserve stepwise adaptation or merely permute histories fail; allowing unrestricted history couplings returns the endpoint transport problem. An up-set with excess younger-label mass `1/[2(u−2)(u−1)u(u+1)]` defeats joint induction on promotion time and promotion-root shape. These are obstructions to those proof routes, not reversals of stochastic order.
- **D6 — stationary Hall scores.** Uniform absolute convergence in the cutoff does not control relative error near the endpoint, where finite-size supply is little-oh of stationary supply. The cutoff-transfer approach yielded no usable large-label threshold. The finite local criterion and Astra's separate very-late result leave an intermediate range.
