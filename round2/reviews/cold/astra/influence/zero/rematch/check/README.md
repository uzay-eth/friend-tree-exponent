# Unmarked rematching: independent physical checks

Rooted tree isomorphisms preserve the generator: each directed arrow has rate equal to the reciprocal of its source degree, the root slot stays inert, and a successful arrow appends one ordinary leaf to its target. After an adapted rematch, draw fresh conditional future arrows on the two relabelled copies. This preserves the entire unmarked marginal law; it does not require keeping an old label's sampled future attached to a new vertex.

## Optimal rematch at a single state

For each immediate-child shape, match its minimum multiplicity across the two roots (including leaves when constructing a correspondence). This maximizes the number of exact sibling agreements. For the actual instantaneous child-occupation TV, first sum the reciprocal-weighted multiplicity on each side for each shape, then cancel the smaller **aggregate mass**; one-to-one vertex matches alone can leave cancellable mass when the two reciprocals or multiplicities differ. Exact sibling matches can always be made simultaneously with alternating nesting **if nesting was possible before pinning**: the alternating containment relation composes recursively by composing the child injections (with direction reversed one level down). When equal siblings `X` on opposite sides were not matched together, reroute the old pairs through `X`: their other endpoints can be matched by transitivity. If one of the equal siblings was unmatched, move the match to the equal sibling instead. Iterate on the remaining siblings; exact matches need no further nesting choices internally.

This gives a reduced state description: a multiset of exact matched arms and the two unmatched multisets, with a matching feasibility test on the outstanding arms. Dropping the exact matched arms entirely, or retaining only counts of unmatched arms, does not determine the next projected rates: a matched child's send rate depends on its degree, and when a dirty arm is selected the possible exact cancellations depend on its shape. No sign claim for `H` follows from the instantaneous minimization.

## Simultaneous promotions and transport

- At `J+/J`, when A promotes a private leaf and B its common leaf, match the two virgin children (`J`) and leave the untouched A sibling private. The net census change is `u−1,c+1`; projected child TV after the tick is `1/6`. The physical leaf-reward gap is `1/3`.
- The same exchange works if A promotes its LC leaf and B promotes a different LL: the two virgin cores match, and the previously paired A-LL leaf takes the LC slot opposite B's old core. On the common-min root clock this requires transport rerouting, since the A-LC slot was originally paired with the send to its B core. Check the complementary transport cell as well. With `A=(L,L,J)`, `B=(L,J)` and the A `J` private, swap `1/12` of the B endpoints between (A LC promotion, B old-core send) and (A private-core send, B LL promotion). All four successor pairs admit nesting. The exchange reduces expected next instantaneous TV by `1/8`, even with **no private A leaves**.
- Keeping the original synchronized mass fixed, residual A-private-leaf and B-LL promotion classes can pair at mass `min(l/A, u(1/B−1/A))`, by the Fréchet bound and a product allocation within the two classes followed by a product of leftovers. B-LC is a **core send**, not a B promotion. If a solver also reroutes synchronized mass, this residual formula alone is no longer the optimization problem.
- For `A=(L,L,L)`, `B=(L,J)`, the exact root-event transport optimum is `43/144` expected next instantaneous child TV; the original fixed-common-min coupling with product residual is `61/144`. The optimal plan here preserves nesting. The checker minimizes over all vertex transports by successively removing a leaf edge in the support forest of a transport vertex. Each removal exhausts one remaining marginal, and both marginal distributions are checked exactly. For any proposed potential substitute the next-state potential for TV in this transport problem; the holding/base cost is independent of that transport on the common rate-one root clock. A horizon-wide guarantee still needs the statewise potential inequalities. For general generator transport, pad each marginal with a self-loop to a common uniformization rate before optimizing.

## Obstructions

Maximal agreement of broad event classes is insufficient to preserve alternating nesting. Take `A=(J,star2)`, `B=(J)` where `star2` has two leaf children. This state is reachable: from `J+/J` promote the common leaves together, pair A's next private promotion with B's inert choice, then pair A's send into its private core with B's inert choice. In the obstruction state, coupling A's send into the common virgin with B's inert selection at rate `1/3` is compatible with both exact marginals and perfect agreement of the promotion/nonpromotion classes, since neither root has leaves. But it leaves two A `star2` arms against one B `J`, and neither A arm can be nested inside that B arm. That failure cannot be repaired by relabelling. The fixture also has a TV-optimal *admissible* plan, so it is not a demonstration that unconstrained TV optimization always fails or must lose value here.

Virgin names are exchangeable among eligible siblings, not across arbitrary parent positions. A tree with a virgin child and a virgin grandchild has two equal fringe shapes at different depths; no root-preserving isomorphism swaps the two. Exact matching of their *occupation atoms* is allowed only when the observable has forgotten that context, not as a correspondence between the physical vertices. Likewise the original fixed-label coupling cannot identify the old private A leaf with the original B common leaf. In the permanent marked model even equal unmarked sibling virgins from the `J+/J` example have different immutable birth marks: `1/3` in A versus `1/2` in B. Their common unmarked child-occupation mass is `1/3`; the marked common mass is zero.

## General LC exchange and cap-eight cross-check

After filling the residual B-LL column first from A private leaves, call the remaining private-core/B-LL mass `r`. Exchange `x=min(v/A,r)` between the `(LC,LC)` and `(private core,LL)` cells, adding the same mass to `(LC,LL)` and `(private core,LC)`. It is a rectangle exchange, so both marginals remain exact for every `x` up to that limit. For `(LC,LL)`, match the two new virgins; the untouched A leaf formerly paired to B-LL now pairs to the arbitrary old B-LC core. This leaf vacuously nests under that core, with the required orientation at depth one. The projected change is only `u--,c++`. In the complementary cell, the A private core gains one leaf but stays private, and the B-LC core gains one leaf while its A partner stays a leaf. Nothing changes in the census; the generator's discounted row drops that self-transition. Neither argument needs the old B-LC or A private core to be a virgin. Optional extra exact matches can be ignored so the checked conservative census remains valid.

`lc.py` independently builds the rational transport by filling the B-LL column and distributing remaining mass by a product, then executes the rectangle exchange. It checked 640 concrete census rows with 76 nonzero LC exchanges, exact unlabelled physical marginals, nesting and marginal leaf counts. Using the independent Bellman corner replay and the same documented terminal bounds, it matched the C++ `tv lc` cap-eight output **exactly on all 27 seed rows** (maximum `20067929613` ticks at scale `10^10`). This is a small-cutoff TV arithmetic check, not a global contraction or signed-envelope result.
