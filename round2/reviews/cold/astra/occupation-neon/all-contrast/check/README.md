# Independent D18 all-contrast review fixture

**Feasibility: an exhaustive staged control scan is plausible; an exhaustive scan of physical trees or an unconditional tensor/subdivision expansion over every row is not.** This check supplies an exact independently assembled `g` oracle and a two-tail subdivision fixture. It does not certify a full eight-family envelope or an exponent improvement.

## Scale and practical gates

The archived lower production GMP log reports 436,764,314 control rows on one payload, 622.26 user CPU seconds, 14:46.59 elapsed and 3,416,064 KiB maximum RSS. Counts: `f` 5,068,364; `g` 83,370; `hV` 15,590,190; `hC` 7,798,560; `P4` 102,128,250; `Yt` 175,910,700; `Yb` 92,610,000; `S3` 37,574,880. `Yt/P4/Yb` are about 85% of that baseline. The archived rate of about 702,000 rows per CPU-second is a baseline, not a throughput measurement for the new kernel. Two mappings are 6.51 GiB total; keep output streamed and do not allocate a full rational array or repack both blobs without accounting for RSS and page-cache contention. Even 32 output bytes per archived row is about 13 GiB of output. The table counts overlap tail-control combinations already; they should not be multiplied blindly by a tensor size to estimate a new scan.

1. Benchmark **bounded slices** of `Yt`, `P4`, `Yb`, and the genuinely mixed tail paths, with the exact final kernel and both files open. Report per-family covered row counts, subcells opened, maximum depth, CPU/wall/RSS, and the time spent on the exact slow path. An archived single-witness GMP runtime is not a production budget for two signed payloads.
2. Stream exact inner rows and supported fixed-role families; keep paired intercepts and coefficients, fold each exterior rate box using the sum of positive signed coefficients (or sum of negative coefficients for a lower bound), and reduce online. A degree-seven tensor has 64 coefficients for two tail axes and 512 for three; bisecting every axis together multiplies cell count by `2^axes` per level. Use a cheap enclosure first, refine only threshold-relevant unresolved cells, and retain an exact conservative enclosure or explicit unresolved status at every exhausted depth/work limit. A physical stock cap, a sampled tail grid, or a silent discard is not exhaustive. Bins with unbounded stock/arity need a proved saturated/overflow formula using the **actual** rate range.
3. Count by actual support stratum and record omissions with a reason. Paired tail axes include two endpoints' **included** donor reciprocals as functions of their own live degrees; they are not independently adjustable external rates. Degree seam, bud/gain activation, no-exterior founder, inactive larger routes, unbounded arity, and the exact `x=0` limiting endpoint need explicit coverage. Check accumulator width before integer narrowing and retain exact arithmetic at certification. If the objective is a ratio, bounding a polynomial numerator divided by a positive denominator requires a denominator enclosure, or use the sign of `N−bound·D`; a Bernstein hull on the cleared numerator alone certifies only its sign.

### Producer snapshot

The current `scan.py` correctly pairs the signed affine coefficients before its exterior corner choice for the six supported higher families. `validate.py` passed 606 sampled higher rows, including 283 multi-tail rows; 106 tested support directions had a strict split-corner gap. This implementation is a bounded physical-label scan, not exhaustive D18 closure: `scan()` builds a raw Cartesian product before testing motif support and sibling symmetry. At degree cap 18, even `P4` visits `323 × 153 × 153 × 170 = 1,285,388,190` candidates before support pruning, compared with 102,128,250 controls in the original full `P4` proof. Build role-specific domains and enforce symmetry during enumeration, not after generating tuples. `S3` scans especially poorly if its strictly capped host still loops over all degree/arity labels. Small successful capped scans are correctness probes, not a basis for a feasible extrapolation unless the loops are restructured.

One integration defect was reproduced in the earlier snapshot and sent to the producer: the generic query accepted `g` despite only supporting the higher families. On `(D,k,h)=(64,3,1)→(37,2,1)` it produced a different lower intercept (numerator `208194364977790908482109117107213` versus the oracle's `228391634308282604037586875964078` on common denominator `46271938636845875200000000000000000000`). The live slopes agree on that example. Incoming tail `f→g` must use the prescribed transfer `ψ=c2/D²+c3/D³`, not the raw `f` gain; arity overflow does not route that transfer. A generic `f` query likewise needs the newborn `f` addition at promotion. **The current query explicitly rejects `g`**, verified on the exact previously failing input.

The raw candidate count above describes the earlier Cartesian loop. The producer subsequently moved to role-specific labels and direct symmetry enumeration; that removes the stated pre-prune bottleneck, without proving all-tail closure or providing a native-kernel throughput measurement.

## Same physical choice, separate payload algebra

For each original physical embedding, first construct **unsigned** old rows `A_i(ρ)=a_i+b_i·ρ` with that payload's own `λ_i`, `f→g` and `g→higher` transfer, and its own center. Then `δlower−θδupper=A_lower+θ A_upper` at the original pair of trials. For another linear form track its explicit signed weights; for example `A_lower−θ A_upper` is a different form, not the contrast of the two opposing production slacks. Replacing a witness's λ adds `(λ_old−λ_new)weight` to its unsigned row and leaves the recharge unchanged; original signed nonnegativity does not automatically transfer to a different λ.

Use common physical degree, arity, and receive values. In particular, with `T_i` the routed derivative and center `r̄_i`, combine `Σ w_i r̄_i T_i` in the intercept and `Σ w_i(Δ_i−T_i)` in the coefficient of the **one** live receive. For incoming transfers combine `Σ w_i(r_live−r̄_i)T_i`. Do not use one payload's center for both, or combine two already-optimized GMP mode results as though they were signed row operands. Separate extrema of the *weighted* terms can be added to give a conservative one-sided bound, but they are not the exact common-choice extreme; subtracting same-direction extrema need not even give a bound. If neighboring rows or a corrector share a physical receive, sum their affine coefficients prior to optimizing that receive as well.

The two shipped binaries happen to have elementwise identical `rpar/rch/trpar/trch`. The fixture therefore adds a synthetic distinct-center mutation (`+1/79` parent, `−1/79` child on the upper row only). The expected bias, independently assembled from incoming/outgoing terms, changes 389 of 407 tested rows and changes no live-rate slope. The stock/census and tail test set includes inner/mixed/both-tail rows, a root without an exterior parent, exact `D=17/18/19`, and arity overflow. At the original trials 52 rows have a strictly looser independent-corner upper bound; the alternate transformed sign and λ run has 298. The independent physical formula is compared by exact fractions to the existing general extractor, plus the discount perturbation evaluated directly from the corresponding witness's physical edge value.

The tail fixture uses `x=18/Dp`, `y=18/Dw`, fixed legal arities `(3,2)`, and live exterior donor sums `(1/3,7/12,8/15)`. On growth, `x` becomes `18x/(18+x)`, not `x−18/Dp²`; the same rule applies to `y`. After multiplication by the positive `(18+x)^3(18+y)^3`, the joint row is a polynomial of bidegree at most `(7,7)`: the gain difference cancels the apparent `1/x` or `1/y` in leaf gain, the sending reciprocals contribute at most one additional degree, and the remaining Bernstein/transfer profiles are cubic per axis. This bound applies to this fixed-stock-law `g` stratum, not automatically across a support/cap discontinuity. The replay computes 64 exact coefficients, tests 25 held-out rational points (including two live far-tail values), and verifies eight exact subcell evaluations after de Casteljau division. A producer may consume `--edge DP KP HP DW KW` for exact normalized unsigned/contrast affine operands in JSON, optionally with `--distinct-upper-centers`.
