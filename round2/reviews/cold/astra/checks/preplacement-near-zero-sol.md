## Physical races and the small-positive interval

* **Next-gain and last-gain race.** Given the full history at the start of any inter-gain gap, the tag's degree and candidate donor set stay fixed until a gain. A candidate initially a leaf cannot ring and still be a leaf without having caused a tag gain. After it is a core, thin the tag's channel to it at rate `1/d` until the first gain or a qualifying other leaf. From such a leaf, real births and rate-`1/2` removal proposals embed the birth–death family; continue the comparison family after the physical stop if necessary. The `7/8`, `5/8`, `1/16` floors are sequential on past histories; multiplication does not assume independence, and an earlier gain already counts as a successful stop. The stock cutoff also counts if already attained at the start. Conditional on reaching `M` other leaves before the gain, restart the first-send barrier there; for the specified donor it costs at most `2/(M+1)` that it subsequently wins. This is a bound on a *finite* gap ending with that donor. In particular, no event of no further gain, no conditioning on the future winner, and no stopping time advertised as the last gain of the entire infinite path is needed. The gain that first reaches `d` is a genuine stopping time. A finite hit after `T` has a completed gap longer than `T/d`, so the union over fewer than `d` gaps and at most `d` candidate donors per gap gives the stated `d^-6+2/d²` bound.
* **Exterior.** Every probability bound in the local races is uniform over full finite starting trees, and applies after conditioning on the history through the relevant stop. Later exterior changes do not break the gain/removal rate inequalities or the first-send barrier. This is not conditioning on a prescribed *future realized exterior trajectory*: doing that could change the local clock laws, and the argument never takes that conditional law. There is also no selection on eventual promotion or on the tag winning a future race.
* **After the hit.** Its fresh leaf starts a tracked process with live family birth rate one and removal rate at most `1/d`. For death rate `q=1/d` per member and `r=1-q`, the comparison's mass at zero at age `h` is `q(1-e^(-rh))/(1-q e^(-rh))≤q`; conditional on being positive its count is geometric on `{1,2,…}` with parameter `r e^(-rh)/(1-q e^(-rh))≤2e^(-rh)`. Thus the finite-age shortage is at most `1/d+2/d^7`. At the endpoint use the true remaining degree mass comparison with `Gamma(L,1)−Exp(1)`. The two failure events `Gamma(L,1)≤d/2` and `Exp(1)≥d/4` cost at most `4/d` apiece (stochastic gamma shape order, Chebyshev, Markov); no independence is used. On success an on-time hit gives original-clock mass strictly above `(d/4)exp(−(T+h))`. Zero mass is never charged as a finite late hit. This proves the loss bound for the closed upper end of `0<W≤exp(−(T+h))`; no continuity at a positive threshold is required.

## True size amplitude and the early seam

At fixed starting tree and size `m`, factor each transition into the `Exp(j)` wait at size `j` and the winning physical arrow. Fresh uniforms for the arrows and fresh exponentials for the waits realize independent sequences; the arrow law depends on the graph, not the wait. The true size process is therefore Yule from `m`, its normalized limit is `S~Gamma(m,1)`, and `S` is independent of the embedded-chain limit `Z` under this starting-tree conditioning. Pathwise `W=S Z`; `W` and `S` are not asserted independent. At the degree hit only the strong Markov comparison for the *remaining* `W` is used; there is no assumption that a birth-size `S` is independent of the time or outcome of a race. Applying Markov at `2(N+2)` and factoring the event there at birth yields `P(0<Z≤epsilon)<(5/256)/N²`. The argument uses `<=` in this loss event, so a hypothetical atom at `epsilon` is paid.

For `2≤n<N` the two-birth identity compares `(n+1,n+2)`, with `n(n+1)≤N²` and start size at most `N+2`. Subtract only the older label's small-positive loss from the hub gap, leaving a strict margin of at least `(8119/800000)/N²` in the general certificate range. At the seed edge (`n=2`) the exact horizon-four calculation has `p3-p4>.022645238637>5/256`. For the pair `(2,3)` restart at deterministic size three, not at a random promotion: label 2 then has degree one or two, and the exact non-enumerative early bound is `p2-p3≥127/2440>5/256`. The center/leaf mixture gives `p1=p2` at all thresholds by exchangeability of the seed labels. At `n=N` the late theorem starts at the exact next old size and compares `(N+1,N+2)`; no index is skipped. This proves strict order for every pair starting at `v≥2` on `0≤x≤epsilon`, without a gap between zero and the positive part of that window. The window does not resolve all thresholds at intermediate labels.
