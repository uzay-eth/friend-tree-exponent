In the chain-middle placement the extra leaf initially sends only into the mark. It adds one red unit, no outside channel. It matters to the old side or outside clusters only if the mark itself sends to its stock or to a core. A tag-to-stock send starts the same degree-two virgin whichever stock leaf was chosen. Neither placement has an initial virgin branch. In both, `Δ=0` until the old boundary gains or there is a tag-origin outside arrow; the combined numerator is at most three before either event. This gives an `O(ε)` early clipping error uniformly in the old shape and in the schedule. The likelihood is exactly one up to that first change. This rules out an extra early contribution from the one-unit difference in initial red mass even when `mθ` diverges arbitrarily slowly.

## Combining ranges

The compact birth-scale theorem (`open/age-order/scaled/README.md`) has a strictly positive uniform gap coefficient on any bounded `vx` interval, including zero. For any putative sequence of failures with labels diverging, either the thresholds stay above a positive constant along a subsequence (the [upper-edge theorem](fixed-upper-edge-sol.md) applies), or they tend to zero. In the latter case pass to a subsequence where `vx` stays bounded or tends to infinity, then use the compact-scale or transition result. Thus there is **one finite, non-effective cutoff** for strict adjacent order on `0≤x<1`. At `x=1` both tails vanish; there is equality there. No order for every early pair follows.
