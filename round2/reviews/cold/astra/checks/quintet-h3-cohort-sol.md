## The same original interpolated H3 cap survives clipping

Use the outward verifier sign `σ=−1` lower, `+1` upper, at each original nominal discount. Let `P` be the exact charged physical H3 row for one unordered path, allocated once to its middle. The incoming transfers are the two original edge jumps when a tip is deleted; the outgoing P4/S3 transfers retain the original center on covered ports. Birth increments use the actual post-promotion arity, with the newborn as the distinguished new tip. `Reader.h3_physical` uses the production H3 potential, not an imported numerical score or a box-worst replacement for `P`. Its reverse direction is just an orientation of the same row.

For a fixed physical middle, let `C_ij` denote the production majorant at the original G3 endpoint corners. Each physical endpoint is either an exact/overflow inner label or has weights `t=d3e/D,1−t` at its capped-arity tail knot and INF. The physical row satisfies `P <= Σ w_i*w_j*C_ij` with the **same endpoint interpolation as the production family**:

- The discount term and middle leaf-gain/centered terms are affine in the two endpoint distributions. Uncovered receive uses its original physical box; bounding the signed linear increment at each corner only increases the expectation. Exact inner-tip overflow uses the true degree and a capped arity for storage. Its promotion has zero weight jump, and the actual signed external-plus-leaf gain is within the production range `[0,(2D−K3e−2)/2]`, separate from its send from the middle.
- The two projected `env23` edge transfers each use independent donor and surviving-tip coordinates. The verified radius bounds the fine edge jump about the interpolated center; the donor reciprocal is the mean of its G3 corner reciprocals. The triangle inequality bounds the signed product by the expectation of `σ(inv−r)*center+|inv−r|*radius`. This includes an INF donor without dropping the `−r` part. The endpoint projections into G4 for newborn S3/P4 compose exactly across its earlier seam, including arity overflow and INF; covered transfers and newborn support depend on internal labels only.
- For **each** tail endpoint, its own gain contributes `t*γ*(H_inf−H_knot)` with `γ=(D−c+1/D_middle+ρ_ext)/(D+1)` in the original production interval. Its promotion has the production `β=(D−c)/D` interval, or zero increment in overflow. Both coefficients can be bounded at the tail knot, averaging over the other endpoint. The middle's gain from that tail's attached reciprocal is `t/d3e` times its physical middle jump, which itself interpolates between INF and the knot; bounding that signed jump by the larger of the two gives the original quadratic credit in `C_ij` on the tail knot. With two tail endpoints do this **separately for both sends**, averaging over the other endpoint for each; no product or cross term is discarded. This covers the knot, the degree-gain seam, and the limit at infinity, not just the two finite tips used on some paid masks.

The authenticated production corner bounds and nonnegative weights give `P<=K_m`, with `K_m` the original local signed cap or its nonnegative part. More generally for any admitted H3 payment `0<=A<=R=[−P]_+`, `P+A<=max(P,0)<=max(K_m,0)`. This inequality is applied **after** evaluating the physical interpolated row; it does not claim that clipping commutes with interpolation or that the stored `env23` arrays are a verifier for a new potential. The per-middle aggregation still uses `C(c,2)` original H3 rows and the original same-sign family caps. The global production higher cap and the reconstructed host cap both still bound the clipped family sum. Therefore their minimum still applies, and `H−h` already priced by `204990e` is unchanged. More explicitly the composition is `Σ higher physical rows <= Σ h − Σ A`, with `Σ(H−h)` and `Σ A` using different inequalities. No changes are made to weight denominators, the conditioned singleton source, or the `g18`/FY slots.

The independent checker reconstructs the pre-rounding C++ H3 corner majorant from original wire weights and envelope centers/radii, then compares it to the physical affine row at its adverse external rate. ## Paid pieces and chronological counts

Write `h→m→y` for the nonfounder chain (high mother, middle, young tip). Its actual upstream reciprocal is separate from its `n=c_h−2` extra child reciprocals. If `b` is its mother-port slope, set `z_i=max(0,b/2)−b/D_i>=0` for every extra child. With all other coordinates, including the upstream send, held equal, `P=P_box−Σz_i`, so the exact increment over the sibling-box baseline is

```
Δ = F(P_box, Σz_i),    F(B,z) = max(0,z−B) − max(0,−B).
```

`F(B,0)=0`; convexity in nonnegative `z` gives `Σ_i F(B,z_i)<=F(B,Σz_i)` by comparing each `z_i` to the common total. `F` is also nonincreasing in `B`. The distinguished-sibling floor uses the adverse remaining boxes for `B` and the lower `z_i` on the sibling's persistent reciprocal interval. These independent boxes only weaken the bound. The box baseline freezes **only** the sibling contribution to the reward; real sibling gain hazards still move the cohort. Other live reward slopes and gain hazards retain their shared coordinates. The full direct `c_h≤7` result is an alternative, not an extra floor on the sibling mask.

The paid box slice is `D_h=38..65,c_h=3..7`, the paid direct slice is `D_h=38..65,c_h=2`, and the sibling increment is `D_h=19..65,c_h=3..7`. The common remaining cuts are `D_m≤5,c_m=2..4,D_y≤18,c_y≤3`. In the `c_h=2` slice the upstream mother-of-mother is still present, but there is **no extra child**; the strict triple `(c_h,c_m,c_y)=(2,2,1)` is removed, including from this direct slice. The sibling mask cannot meet it. On the overlapping `c_h≥3` supports, box plus incremental payment is at most `R_box+Δ=R`; on a nonoverlap the same inequality holds after setting the absent summand to zero. The upstream fourth-parent alternative and earlier overlapping direct result are not in the sum.

The base chain is registered by its actual ancestral promotions. The additional-child test uses exactly `hmys`, `hmsy`, `hsmy` (with `m` preceding `y`), one for each possible chronology of a fixed distinguished sibling. Old prefixes continue at their post-event arity; insertion adds a new prefix at the actual newborn mark. Other children are not erased, and equal census labels do not merge actual siblings. A sibling entering `D≥3` keeps its identity with reciprocal in `[0,1/3]`; no new registration occurs at the seam. Terminal H3 ownership is once per unordered path; multiple ordered sibling incidences are allowed only because their rewards sum to at most the single `Δ`. These are occupation rewards over all ages, not counts divided by the number of registered trips. Summing prefix generator inequalities cancels all shorter-to-longer birth terms, leaving **one** `μ` times the length-one value per physical core birth (and a nonnegative `(HI−μ)` times the total value). Replace `μ` by `LO` once. There is no extra multiplication by birth intensity for each extension or each order. Bounded-degree core promotions and the finite role counts give the fixed-radius moment control needed for the normalized sums; founder-containing sets are unpaid.
