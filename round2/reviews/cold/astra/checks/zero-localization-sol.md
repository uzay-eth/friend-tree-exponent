## Localization: where signed expansion can and cannot stop

Write the scalar of order `k` as `(V_s C Δ)^k V_s b`. Pair the two physical histories starting from the same old tree with and without one inserted leaf. Until the observer is infected, the common observer neighbour set and root degree agree. A structural leaf could change status in just one copy only through a differential observer send; with a clean observer that cannot happen. Off the unique affected child path, future intrinsic shapes and hence all reset payoffs are identical. The same root coefficient multiplies each of those cancelled pairs; even on the affected child the root's census alone says nothing about the whole-child score.

The surviving shift is a **single** multiplication by `C Δ`, not two shifts for the two reset signs and not a physical send. Its two matched sign branches have total absolute coefficient `2p_o≤1`; the continued observer has the same original child label, with its old parent becoming inert, and the positive branch attaches one matched leaf on both sides. The connected discrepancy above the current infection front can only advance by the next directed surplus arrow on the old core path, at rate at most `1/2`. Repeated returns can increase the gap without advancing the front. Rerooting before infection preserves the parity/nesting argument and may leave an already large gap, so multiplying pristine first-hit coupons across resets would not be justified. With no shift, or on a fixed initial segment completed before the first shift, the pristine coupon does apply at successive first infections.

For distance `d`, the weighted drift is at most `(z−1) z^(−d)/2`. Before the hit the residence integral therefore costs at most `z^(−d)/λ` for `λ=s−(z−1)/2`, and a surviving shift multiplies this weight by `z`. The hit within the residence costs at most the incoming weight by the stopped supermartingale. Landing exactly on an infected child means a time-zero hit in the next block; count that shift once. At the hit the same residence restarts, **including its entire unmasked continuation**. For exactly `r` remaining shifts use `2B_r`, with `B_0=1/s` and `B_r=q^(r−1)/s²` otherwise. This bound applies to the actual conditional starting states, even if one side can now choose a child that did not exist on the other side. Before the hit, a nonfinal residence's affected-child term need not vanish; it must be followed. A final residence whose observer never hits contributes zero to the difference. Summing the finite terms and charging two unrestricted order tails gives the localization inequality. Applying it on each observer-reset marginal gives the factor two for `H` at positive depth.

The independent checker solves three signed orders directly on a labelled finite directed-arrow model, then recomputes the same three differences by splitting at the first observer hit. It rejects killing at the hit, dropping higher-order continuations at the hit, and counting a matched shift twice. This fixture is a control of the decomposition, not a surrogate for the all-shape rate proof.

## Spine: fixed original boundary and chronological time

The cut acts on **original** depth, preserved through every observer shift. A leaf at depth `D+1` still sends into its retained parent at rate one and can be promoted; in the projected tree it then occupies the same degree slot as an opaque core. Its core return and its signed occupation selection are suppressed. An outbound selection of an already opaque slot may be kept as a hidden ordinary event in a full lift, or discarded as a projected null event without reallocating its rate. The old parent of a shifted observer must stop sending. The independent directed-arrow fixture shows that a moving cutoff opens an outgoing observer shift and a core up-send at rate `1/2` after one shift; deleting the slot instead raises an actual retained-source selection from `1/2` to `1`. Both are different operators.

The time-resolved TV comparison applies on the cut as well. The cut can forbid root `C` completely at remaining depth zero. Elsewhere the root and all its immediate child types have the ordinary census; at remaining depth one, opaque descendants only alter the adapted child histories. If a paired frontier slot is opaque only on the core side, the ordinary leaf on the other side still sends into the retained parent; the surplus has the allowed parity sign. There is no shortcut under which a forbidden shift renormalizes the remaining child probabilities.

For total chronological residence time `t_0+…+t_k`, the Laplace ratio factors as `∏exp(−(s−a)t_i)`. Positive paired measures are formed on the **shape and elapsed time** of each row; equal-shape cancellation at the same age survives a cumulative-time test because the subsequent kernels and elapsed-time test agree on both masses. An event mask depending on the discarded individual histories need not survive cancellation. The checker gives a finite signed atomic counterexample in which such a mask makes a zero atom nonzero, and a separate example where every individual residence is below `T` but their sum is above `T`. The two short-contact histories instead use the raw normalized clocks, signs and finite prefactors; raw coefficients are not used for an infinite geometric sum.
