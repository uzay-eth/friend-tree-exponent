## Original-physical upper, including the zero box

Until promotion the tip stays adjacent to its original parent. A true parent gain, including the tip's own arrow at rate one, adds a fresh tracked sibling and increases parent degree by one. The `j` tracked siblings themselves give gain rate `j`; their promotions have rate `j/k`, while the tip promotion has rate `1/k`. All these channels are disjoint. An old nonleaf neighbour stays nonleaf, so `k≥j+2` at all times. Pay one at promotion and test `f(j)=1/(j+1)`. With rates `j+1`, `j/k`, `1/k`, the drift is `−1/(j+2)+1/k≤0` for `j≥1`, and is `−1/2` at zero. Any extra parent gain lowers the test. The physical probability of any promotion is at most this `f`, by finite-horizon optional stopping and monotone convergence of the promotion event. Independently the hazard bound `1/k` gives `E[e^(−pτ);τ<∞]≤1/(1+pk)` for positive `p`; each inequality remains usable at the first cell's `lo=0` (the second ceiling is then just one). There is no claim about `W^0`, including when `τ=∞`.

At promotion the affine Yule seed is `1+1/k_actual`, because the permanent parent slot contributes at most `1/k_actual`. The normalized promotion reward is at most `1+1/k` throughout the post-cutoff branch: `k_actual≥k` and `A_p(1+1/k)` increases to `A_1(1+1/k)` on this power range. Factor this deterministic bound out of the smaller of the two valid discount ceilings; no factorization by a random promotion time is made. Below cutoff the two-race cap is the positive mixture checked in the earlier fractional proof. The recurrence uses `lo` in the discount denominator, rounds upward, and takes the maximum with the gain successor. Thus extra gains do not increase the test and the stopped supersolution is valid on the actual tree. ## Shifts and serial credit

In two placements on distinct adjacent old targets, both old target degrees increase once. For a tip attached to an old core of degree `d` with `l` old leaves, the parent's new degree is `d+1`; its other sibling stock is exactly `l` when the opposing target was a core, and `l−1` when that target was an old leaf promoted by the opposite placement. On that old-leaf side the parent is now degree two with no other leaf siblings: its old neighbour is a core. On a star, the opposite promoted old leaf supplies the required permanent core slot at the centre. The serial adverse tip has `(2,0)`, since its sibling slot at the middle is the old host, now a core even if it was originally a leaf. On the edge seed both parallel coefficients cancel; serial lower without a bonus suffices. The coefficient itself is `s_v−s_u` for the test of the leaf at `u` minus that at `v`; that sign means the debit belongs to `u` only when `s_u>s_v`. The unchanged common completion factor is `Γ(p+1)/[n(n+1)(n+2)_p]`.

For the low-power host bonus, while the middle has degree two and one fresh leaf and the old host has degree `k`, its gain channels are precisely the fresh leaf and old host (`1+1/k`). Its loss is its own arrow to that fresh leaf (rate `1/2`); the other half of its own clock is **host growth**, not a second stock loss. Host growth has rate between zero and `k−1/2`. Its only effect on the race state is to increase `k`; the two focal exits pay the audited `V(3,2)` or the true last-loss return-state `V(2,0)`. At fixed row the drift is affine in the unknown growth rate, so the minimum of the two endpoint quotient bounds is safe. Taking the maximum with `M` is justified as a choice to stop and use the separately available baseline; it need not preserve the continuation drift where the baseline branch is chosen. The host started at its **new** degree `d+1`; in particular old leaves use `H(2)` and bare suppressed sites use `H(3)`. None of these bonuses is booked on favourable parallel tips.

## All-tree payment and telescoping

A direct edge has one signed difference. Its debit is bounded by one baseline `b` plus the two nonnegative directed excess allowances. For a known direct neighbour of degree `e`, receipt from the focal vertex of degree `d` implies `s_opp≥1/[de(e+1)]`, and the opposite favourable lower is `L(e+1)`. Each directed bound then subtracts the common baseline only after truncating its raw debit at zero. For unresolved slots zero is a safe lower for both quantities. At fixed direct degrees and chain-slot count the residual is concave in receive, because all debit hinges are convex and the credit is their negative plus affine terms. With no direct excess, the concavity extends across the triangular count/receive domain: the direct transfer reserve and chain bills are affine in the count. Thus extreme counts, or all counts with their receive endpoints, are sufficient. A low recipient that books a transfer uses degree cutoff 14, so its unresolved direct neighbour really has degree at least 15. That neighbour has already budgeted `.005` per direct port in those cells, including if it lies in the analytic degree tail; a chain port earns no such receipt. Discarded payments only help the inequality. Each transfer is charged at most once at either end of a physical direct edge.

For the tail, `s≤(d+l)/[2d(d+1)]`. Multiplying by the stock ceiling at `l` gives directed exposure at most `(1+1/(d+1))/(2(d+1))`, using `(d+l)/(d(l+1))≤1`. On the core side of an old-leaf edge the bound at `l−1` cancels the multiplicity `l`, so the total is at most `T`. For `x=l/d≤1/2`, stock credit minus the other direction is bounded below by `xz` and the paid port count is at least `D/2`; the minimum is no worse than the checker expression before `T`. For `x>1/2`, `g>0` gives the stated half-stock credit, the coefficient shrink bounds the other-direction debit by `U(2,0)/(4(D+1))`, and the net port allowance is nonnegative. Large stars use all their central serial weight and the same core-side total cap `T`; small stars and seed are checked separately.

For paths, both end coefficients of the actual bare chain depend on the true endpoint reciprocals. Low endpoints use the paired difference with their two receive corners. Higher ones use `max(s_chain·forward_cap, exposure_endpoint)`; beyond `K` the displayed analytic min bounds exposure by the stock and discount alternatives, each decreasing in endpoint degree. All remaining ranged debits are convex, so their maxima occur at corners. The code scales exactly with LCMs: `X` includes each reciprocal's denominator times six, each endpoint coefficient's denominator, and 12; `Y` includes each slope, credit, and fee denominator. No truncation of a signed expense is hidden in `int()`. For lengths greater than three the additional sites have identical `s=1/6`, adding positive credit and zero extra internal debit. Suppressing a path keeps the degrees of the retained cores in their induced skeleton, preserves the two terminals even for a core tree that is itself a path, and leaves a connected acyclic tree.
