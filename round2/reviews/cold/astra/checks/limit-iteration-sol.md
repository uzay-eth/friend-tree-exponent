### Can the two coefficient densities be iterated?

There is a viable *conditional* Young bootstrap if the two actual weighted side-coefficient laws have `L^r` densities. Given `P=p`, they are independent under the factorized shape gates of a single cut. For unnormalized densities `f_i∈L^r`, `1<r<2`, convolution gives `L^s` with `s=r/(2−r)`, and the norm is bounded by `||f_1||_r||f_2||_r E[P^(−μ(1−1/r))(1−P)^(−μ(1−1/r))]`. The joint Beta moment is finite for the actual `n_i≥2` and is explicitly a ratio of Beta functions; `r=2` gives bounded continuous convolution by the same translation argument. To use this on the full law one would need *quantitative* regularity for the weighted **planted** coefficient laws, size dependence of their norms at the selected physical cuts, and a way to pay for unresolved mass that preserves the gain. The free edge-seeded `L^r` theorem supplies none of these, and six-side inverse moments are not density estimates for either coefficient. Even positive inverse moments of all orders allow two deterministic positive coefficients, where the conditional angular image still has a square-root fold and may fail strong `L²`. Thus the joint-coefficient negative-moment improvement above is unconditional under the cited inputs, but an iterative Young bootstrap to still higher regularity is a separate proposal with missing inputs, not a consequence of current mesh or six-side estimates.
