## Conditioning, denominator, and the combined development

Starting at each **noninitial** newborn, select at most its first promotion, and only if all prior focal events are plain structural-leaf gains until the prescribed degree `d`. Before that promotion there are no core children and the competing outside gain is at most `1/2`. Regard those inputs as unwanted, even when they would also raise degree. At each step the wanted rate is fixed until a focal event and the total hazard is bounded by `L+L/(L+1)+1/2`. Conditional on the entire past at the step, integrate the wanted intensity against the no-focal-event survival lower bound and the discount. Iteration at the successive stopping times gives the stated product `a_d(s)`. No future exterior proposal clock is conditioned independent of success. Two distinct initial newborns cannot select the same output birth, because each output has a unique promoter. Consequently Tonelli over the actual birth times gives the discounted class count lower bound, relative to the total discounted count of noninitial births. Distinct **exact birth-degree** classes are disjoint; overlapping current-degree windows are not used in any endpoint here.

For a birth in class `d`, the parent remains a core, its degree never falls, and the entire subsequent gain interval shrinks from `[0,(k+1)/2]` to `[0,1/d+k/2]`. Use the computed baseline for all births and the computed **difference** of the class-specific bound and baseline for that class. Each difference is nonnegative in the replay. This need not assert a lower bound on the difference between two unknown true lifetime expectations. The same reasoning applies with the all-time tail unchanged: the tail optimization uses only `b`, so restricting `ρ` preserves validity. Applying that bonus to the **all-time** occupation in the stronger calculation gives the development above. It is not an addition of two lifetime bounds.

For normalization, `I(s)=∫e^(−st) E C(t)dt` diverges as `s↓μ`; the discounted physical birth count divided by `I(s)` is `s−C(0)/I(s)`. Conditional on a birth, price its life from that history, then use Tonelli. The lookup discount `HI` is conservative at `μ`, and at `s` near `μ` one can use it directly for `s≤HI`. Alternatively price at `s` and pass down by continuity; for the finite-state and five-band recurrences this includes one-sided limits if an endpoint is initially attained. Integration by parts gives `[ε(ℒΦ−λΦ)]_s=ε(s−λ)[Φ]_s−ε Φ(T_3)/I(s)`. The unnormalized boundary at infinity vanishes for `s>μ` from the mean comparison. Thus a positive price at least `LOW*v` gives `ε(μ−λ)≥LOW*v/M` for an upper bound `M` on the normalized average potential. This route does not require integrating an unbounded signed generator under the stationary environment.

For the [eight-motif potential comparison, §5](../occupation-neon/check/neon-proof-pin.md), use its **unrecharged** upper grouped cap. In the seven young states `k≤1`, no apex motif is charged there.
