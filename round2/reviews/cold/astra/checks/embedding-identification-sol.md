## 1. Tightness and the all-`J` constants

Under the physical promotion-Palm age mixture the newborn has `(D,k)=(2,0)`. Real incoming gains and the focal vertex's own initiating rings are disjoint channels. A rate-`d−1/2` upper pure-birth chain and an independent unit-rate proposal clock dominate degree and arity conditional on the birth environment. Replacing the common independent `Exp(μ)` age by the larger `Exp(s0)` preserves both increasing comparisons. If `τ` is the upper chain's hit of `J+1`, then the two dominating tails are `E exp(−s0 τ)` and `E[(τ+1/s0) exp(−s0 τ)]`. Holding-time Laplace transforms yield exactly `P_J` and `P_J H_J`, with the product ending at `J`, not `J−1`. Logarithms give `P_J=O(J^(−s0))`, `H_J=O(log J)`. Multiplication by `z` takes probability units to intensity units only once.

For an added high child, reverse to the child, whose mother and entire constrained apex path are determined. Remaining free role choices are bounded by `K^r`. For an added high mother, reverse to the mother, choose a child for the old apex in `k_mother` ways, and fill the remaining `r−1` positions at cost at most `K^(r−1)`. This uses physical incidence re-rooting; it does not use a conditional degree law of the donor. A binary canopy gives a sharp illustration of the missing multiplicity: with root level `j` of probability `2^(−j−1)`, arity two above level zero, arity zero at level zero, and degrees three and two respectively, the high root mass at `J=2` is `1/2`, whereas the mother of the observed vertex is high with probability one. Its `k`-weighted root mass is exactly one. This is a stationary incidence countermodel to replacing the maternal donor count by a root tail; it is not an alternative eigenlaw. It even violates the proposed *unweighted* `P_2` maternal budget, since `P_2<1` for the trial discount.

With `F` the complete required extension mass, the implemented full-cap row is `S_cap≤F`; its all-`J` row is `F−S_J≤ε_J`. Together they sandwich the slice `S_cap−S_J` above by `ε_J`, also paying any omitted mass. Taking limits at each *fixed* `J`, then letting `J` tend to infinity, gives the exact projective equality. Testing only at the running cap cannot do this: a single unit of extension mass at degree exactly equal to the cap passes exact cap closure for every cap while all fixed census columns converge to zero. The same reasoning applies to normalization at the root. The fixed-pattern upper bound from successive labelled insertions and the bounded reciprocal remainder give compactness and identify the port limit; a cofinal schedule must eventually **share** each such port with its one-node extension columns and send their census cutoff to infinity.

## 2. Ordered slots and the distinguished root

For a fixed apex occurrence there are `∏(k)_c` possible assignments of its displayed roles to distinct positions. The assigned positions and distinguished role determine at most one apex embedding observed from the distinguished vertex. Thus start from the uniform-core observation measure and divide labelled mass by that falling-factorial count, without dividing by an automorphism order. Re-rooting to an ancestor is not a new uniform-core draw. In particular, adding a mother with arity `k` divides the joint intensity by `k` for a **specified** spine position and sums it back over all `k` possible positions when the new position is not observed. These two operations are both necessary; the canopy fixture also concretely tests them. A specified new child position divides by `k−c`, which cancels the corresponding child projection. With the root normalized, these extensions generate the full-ball partitions by nonnegative summation, yielding compatible connected balls with finite marks and an infinite simple maternal ray. The leaf mark is exactly `L=D−k−1`, not a freely chosen decoration. Uniform ordering here is a decoration of the actual root law, not a substitution of a uniform mother for the mother of an observed child.

## 3. The time-one operator and what identifies the law

The correct space is the un-oriented rooted **core** tree with nonnegative integer ordinary leaf stock at each vertex; the full core degrees are retained in truncated balls. For a finite linear target choose exact cylinders with `L` and core degree fixed at **every** displayed vertex. There are then finitely many oriented/ordered readouts, obtained by summing disjoint choices of spine and slots. If the convention in §3's phrase “every un-oriented marked ball indicator” allows balls recording only `L` at boundary vertices, a generic such ball is a *countable* union of degree-saturated cylinders and need not itself have the exact finite readout expected by `time_rows`. Restricting the schedule to the degree-saturated family is sufficient and is consistent with the degree-fixed convention used elsewhere in the note. At radius zero, a fixed `L` alone already allows infinitely many distinct `k`; the fixture exhibits this distinction. Conversely, dropping `L` from the tests and readouts would lose information about the generator: already a two-sided core path of degree two has zero promotion at a bare vertex, and promotion rate `1/3` with one attached leaf. Do not apply the eigenmeasure theorem on a census-forgetting projection of this state space.

The old-cell error follows from time-ordered paths and `2 sqrt(2)e < 9`. A boundary slot represents only a deleted directed interaction; it stays in the ordinary degree denominator. The `j`-step uniformization kernel preserves genuine arrow rates and discards cap overflow, including the genuine transition out of a state at the cap. The lower Poisson part divides by an upper bound for `exp(cap)`; the lost mass is covered using the root-cell second moment and outward bounds on the Poisson remainder. `cap*omitted` in the upper enclosure is redundant with a full missing-path Cauchy–Schwarz estimate, but harmless. For asymptotically tight enclosures grow the `ceil_sqrt` grid as well as radius, cap, and Poisson cutoffs; the default fixed grid alone leaves a nonzero floor. The replay fixture tests a sharply saturated cap analytically against `exp(−3/2)`, and rates when a boundary has multiple retained slots.

At each fixed radius, exhausting finite disjoint input prefixes controls the untested mass by `3` times the reconstructed complement; then grow the radius and accuracy. One discount is selected per relaxation; vanishing bands ensure it is common in the limit. The limiting equality on the determining cylinders lifts to equality of positive finite measures, since `Q_1 1≤e`. No generator graph-core argument is needed. The physical uniform rank-one estimate gives a direct proof of identification: for a probability eigenmeasure `λ` at rate `e^s`, integrate the estimate at integer times with `f=1`. Then `exp((s−μ)n)→λ(h)` where `h` is bounded and pointwise strictly positive, so `0<λ(h)<∞`, `s=μ`, and `λ(h)=1`. Repeat for every degree-saturated marked cylinder to get `λ=π`. This checks positivity at the right domain and does not require that a left eigenprobability itself have full support, or that `h` have a globally positive lower bound. The global normalization rows give `z→1/μ`.

The projection construction alone does **not** give one end. The bare bi-infinite line with deterministic direction to one end, `D=2,k=1` at every vertex, has consistent slots and all incidence tails zero. It is **not** an invariant law for time one: core-to-core sends create leaves, and the original root still contributes to the cell sum. It is a countermodel only to inferring one end from projectivity. After identifying the un-oriented **marked** law with the physical `π`, the cited finite-fringe representation gives one end. The constructed mother ray must then target it. In a tree the edge to the component containing that end is the unique mother edge at every vertex, so no second orientation remains to mix. With this lift, fixed labelled mass is recovered by multiplying the decorated cylinder probability by its known finite divisor and by `z`; fixed reciprocal port limits follow from projection. No moving-column objective follows without its own uniform budget.
