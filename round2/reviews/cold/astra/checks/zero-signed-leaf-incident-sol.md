## Reachable strongly coupled row

At a joint residual root tick, A promotes its private leaf and B promotes the common leaf, with paired rate `1/(AB)=1/6`. The result in `(u,v,c,e,l,k)` order is `(0,1,0,0,0,1)`. The physical root census is `(D,L,j)=(3,1,1)` on A and `(2,0,1)` on B. Thus `b_A−b_B=+1/3`; the old reward in `envelope.cpp`, `fixture.py` and the surrogate README is `−1/2`. The error is `v(1/A+1/B)=5/6` on this row. In general the physical leaf counts are `L_A=u+v+l`, `L_B=u`. Negating the observable reverses the error, so the old interval is not a justified two-sided comparison. The fixture recreated the bad payoff despite independently listing the clocks; its old PASS does not validate the physical observable.

The geometry and root clock themselves check out. A synchronized root selection of that LC slot, at `1/3`, promotes the A leaf and grows the B core, converting `v→e` (dirty CC), not `v→c`. A residual selection of the same B core paired with the A private core, at `1/6`, leaves `v` and the census unchanged. In the uncut pair the LC slot returns at `1/2` synchronously (adds a common leaf) and at `1/2` in A alone (adds a private leaf); with the B core made opaque, the A leaf still returns at rate one and the entire gain is private. The fixture reconstructs these rates from the labelled degree laws, without importing either old surrogate implementation.

Exact finite one-sided paths from the reachable LC fixture check these orientations and bounds.
