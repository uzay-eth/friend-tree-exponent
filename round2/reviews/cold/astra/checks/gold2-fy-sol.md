## Physical occupation and absorbing donor

At a nonfounder of `(D,c)`, including its mother in `c`, exact gain rate is `D−c+Σ_core 1/D_neighbor`; exact promotion rate is `(D−c)/D`, keeping `D` and increasing `c`. Tracked adjacent degrees enter exact receives, not frozen receiver bins. Each untracked core slot contributes a reciprocal in `[0,1/2]`. The Bellman checks use the same gain rate in the total holding hazard and in the gain continuation, with the exact signed worst-corner residual after rounding. Relaxing separately boxed rates or taking a uniform lower reward over a band cannot increase the floor.

For the small payer run, states below donor degree 12 include every feasible arity; the boundary gain from `(11,c)` enters the sentinel, a promotion at degree 11 still goes to `(11,c+1)` when possible, and above the boundary the true degree is monotone. The sentinel is not a physical degree-12 vertex with zero stock. It represents all actual donor states at `D≥12`. In a registered path the donor remains a durable adjacent core and its actual receive into the host is boxed by `[0,1/12]`; the extra slot is not also included in the `[0,1/2]` count. Donor-own gains and promotions keep the projected state unchanged and contribute zero to the generator, so they are omitted from both numerator and denominator. They may still alter the invisible state and future rates within the box. In the mother–donor prefix which has not yet spawned the host, the additional births after entering the sentinel are deliberately dropped, not charged as sentinel self-loops with a birth source. A donor already registered with the host remains tracked through later host promotions; its existing continuation and any eligible new pair sources remain on the host side. The reward floor at every paying state is `M−max f_h(x)` over `x=0,1/12` and every original knot between them. Piecewise linearity places the maximum at these candidates even if neither extreme is a physical reciprocal. The below-boundary bins take minima over actual donor degrees rather than pretending they share a physical degree. The older-payer lane tracks every donor degree `2..5` at all arities, and uses the discrete reciprocal exactly.

Each host's distinct ordered neighbours are partitioned into mother-payer/child-donor, child-payer/mother-donor, and two children. In the last class choosing which older child is registered as payer or as donor produces the two ordered roles once each, at the younger child's true promotion. In the maternal classes start the prospective mother at its genuine birth, start the host at its mother's promotion, and register the pair only at the host's subsequent promotion. The mother, existing host and old child keep their evolving state throughout. Thus the birth source is appended to the same physical promotion transition, not attached to a selected eventual good history. Zeroing capped continuations discards nonnegative reward.
