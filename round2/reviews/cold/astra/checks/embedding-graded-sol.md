## Closure and graded ports

The cap at order `r` bounds **all** degrees in an order-`r` census. For an in-band target, a same-order gain predecessor only lowers a degree. An old promotion predecessor decreases arity while leaving degree fixed. A new virgin tip deletes one specified target role and decrements the mother's arity at fixed degree: the source fits the wider preceding cap, and its promotion rate is `(D−k_target)/D`. This deletion is performed once for each distinct labelled slot, even if several virgin tips are indistinguishable by census. There is no unobserved boundary influx from a larger degree; an out-of-band successor still contributes its outgoing loss. A bare target cannot promote one of its nonexistent leaves. A new **rooted-at-newborn** embedding can only have size one, since the new core initially has one ordinary leaf and no core children. Thus the stationary source is one on the virgin row after normalization, zero elsewhere. The explicit `D=5` mother to virgin edge has incoming coefficient `−4/5` on both splits of its `(5,0)` source.

When the entire base census fits the next cap `J_max`, every eligible labelled extension appears once. Using `J_max` in the port gives omitted reciprocal at most `1/(J_max+1)`. The mass-cut upper counts at `2≤J≤J_max` are separate from that reciprocal estimate: the omitted child incidence reverses to a unique mother and costs at most `K^r` times the degree tail; omitted mother incidence reverses to at most `k_mother K^(r−1)` choices and uses the **weighted** arity tail. At the top of the band the retained-mass inequality uses all represented extensions. At lower thresholds the combined high-or-missing budget is taken against the same base mass. The physical mass and flux do not acquire an automorphism divisor; the distinct slot has already been specified.

**Adverse seam.** The companion plants a degree-seven nonfounder with a degree-two mother and a degree-two core child. Its order-one census is retained, but no order-two extension exists at cap five. Both true exterior fluxes per map are `1/2`, which would violate a spurious `1/6` overflow bound. The source correctly uses `1/2` and installs no donor mass cut for that census. The same test at order two uses a degree-five-to-virgin edge with a degree-two exterior mother: `1/2>1/4` at the next cap three. The retained `D=3` base, by contrast, resolves the degree-two child slice in its `J=2` mass cut. These tests also establish that a skipped extension does not skip the underlying generator balance.

## Same physical law and the founder boundary

Let `η=π/μ`. Use `η` for each labelled mass, both directions of every receive, and total mass. Within a finite sided census, split the *same* physical occupation among the endpoint boxes for the actual child receive `u`, mother receive `v` and common discount `μ`. The affine `mass` coefficient is `b−s`, so `b−coefficient` recovers the discount coordinate for each column; the join equates its **weighted** sum with `low*m_low+high*m_high` from the embedding LP. At a degree-five census with `k=3` and one with `k=4`, the exact test assigns different mass, receive and promotion rates even though both use the same coarse arity bucket. This is an outer relaxation: it does not force one common discount across *all feasible LP solutions*. The actual physical law has a common discount and supplies a feasible lift. The inherited sided overflow and Palm class-minorant construction is independently audited in [path-fast-sol.md](path-fast-sol.md); its older discount endpoints enclose both the physical `μ` and the narrower embedding split used here.

On an oriented physical edge, exactly one child-to-parent reciprocal goes to `up` at the parent and one parent-to-child reciprocal goes to `down` at the child. The same edge-mass column supplies both. At `(18,5,3)`, every retained edge degree is in an exact low-degree band. All represented arities in each bucket are summed: the `(D_parent,D_child)=(5,5), (k_bucket,parent,k_bucket,child)=(3,3)` equality contains four separate fine edge censuses, each with its own two split masses. The exact `(D,k)` single-root joins never use that coarse arity bucket as an exact census. No equality to a partial degree bucket is needed here. This is a joint restriction on one physical law, not the intersection of independently optimized marginal prices.

The finite generator checks preserve promotions from the original founder as physical virgin sources but omit the founder's own contributions as a planted apex. A mother at small degree can be the founder, yielding the real finite-tree surplus `1/D_founder−1/(J_max+1)` when absent from planted extension masses; the companion encountered 30 positive residuals and checked that correction. As in the flat audit, capped configurations with a founder involved have uniformly bounded multiplicity at that cap. For tests anchored at a founder child while the founder grows beyond the cap, her number of promoted children has expectation at most `O(1+t)` and weighted reciprocal contributions are bounded. Her own total promotion intensity is at most one. These are negligible after normalization by exponentially growing mean core count; any initial orientation anomaly is confined to the founders. Thus the exact edge and receive joins are **stationary**, not literal finite-tree equalities with a founder excluded from only one side. The normalized birth measure is `β(f)=η(b f)` and `η(b)=1`, so the overflow conditional value in the objective is a single constant. The other terms are only the finite mother bins' differences from it, evaluated at the same normalized birth flux.
