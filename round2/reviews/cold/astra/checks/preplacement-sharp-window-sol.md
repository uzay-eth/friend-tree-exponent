## Return duration and physical clock

After a last **actual** tracked-leaf promotion the sender is the new degree-two core. Until focal gain, the focal degree `d` stays fixed and the sender's actual degree `k_phys` is at most the virtual `k`. Its incoming gain rate is at most `k_phys−1+1/d≤k−1+1/d`. Pad actual gains to the full virtual growth channel and split the outgoing physical return into a common rate `1/k` and the excess. Any excess return or other focal gain ends this stage immediately, with the same next-degree lower state and a fresh leaf; both rates in the nominal race remain valid before that exit. A virtual hit of 32 is failure even if the physical sender is still below 32. The initial favourable parallel leaf uses the same construction at `d=1`, starting from actual parent degree and discarding at or above 32. There is no restart from an arbitrary bare-core state.

The normalized nominal return success pays zero at 32 and gives the original frozen return lower. In contrast the duration transform pays **one at both exits**, including failure at 32. At row `k`, integrating the exponential holding time tilted by `θ=1/2` gives the denominator `1/k+(k−1+1/d)−θ`; each denominator is positive. The nonincreasing return row follows backwards since the numerator of `R(k)−R(k+1)` is `1/k+(θ−1/k)R(k+1)≥0` at this tilt. The nominal transform bounds the physical stopped time because earlier physical exits shorten the race, or by comparison with the accumulated exponential-clock drift. It is unconditional on success.

In positive stock the rates `j` and `j/d` are real monitored channels. All other focal gains land in the same state as a tracked birth. Taking the larger of the recurrence value and its gain successor makes the extra-gain drift nonpositive, for every outside hazard. A last loss factors the duration upper and next-degree potential by a **conditional** bound at the actual gain, not by independence. A failed return has payoff one, bounded above by that continuation potential. Each success increases focal degree, losses at fixed degree spend stock, and nominal returns have a finite cap; the bounded-state exit stage is also almost surely finite. Thus each successive bound can be used in the whole physical path, including failures. For the initial host-credit race, the fresh leaf's arrow gives hazard at least one while the stage is pending. Its exits are either host growth with the original state, a last loss, or a focal gain with one or two tracked leaves (both can track both on the two natural gain channels). The checker covers all four continuation keys and charges the associated worst value times `1/(1−θ)`. No claim of conditional deadline control given a future successful outcome is required.

## Exact exit and strict mass floor
