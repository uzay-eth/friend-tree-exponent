# Cold review: fixed thresholds through the upper edge

**Holds.** Final proof (`open/age-order/fixed-threshold/uniform/upper-edge/README.md`) Git blob `d862bf91df24c8fe1bdd3e8edf7b750ccb9ba767` (including the revised clock and weighted-integral wording). For each fixed `0<p<1`, same `κ` as the interior theorem and a relative error uniform on `[p,1)`. This gives one strict adjacent cutoff on that range; it is not a quantified cutoff.

- **Tilt and stopping.** Every rounded outside cutoff is finite under both laws: a physical all-red continuation has per-step blue hazard at least `b/j`. The upper beta comparison works on each countable stopped history with its actual posterior. The lower density is a dominated subprobability, by Fatou under the original reference; equality of infinite-path laws is unnecessary. Normalize both bounds by the rare beta survival before taking limits.
- **Uniform likelihood.** Set `u=1−θ`. The outside-index red waits satisfy `E[uG]=θ` and `Var(uG)=θ` all the way to `u=0+`. After the clipped start, use the dyadic bound on `uR_f−θf` to bound `e_f=log(uR_f/(θf))`; Abel summation against the increasing deficit costs a summable `2^(−k/8)` per dyadic scale, with both endpoint terms included. The red quadratic and sum-to-integral errors telescope against `1/R_f−1/R_(f+1)` even if `G=0` or enormous. The early interval has no deficit change except with probability `O(ε)`, uniformly. These arguments justify using the bounded `L∞∧1` below and the finite-window envelope in expectation above. They do not imply infinite-product uniform integrability.
- **Universal constant.** The old-parent term costs at most `1/2` times its first-gain probability; its rate before that gain is at most two. Add the rate-one tag-origin envelope to get `EΔ_f≤2Σ_(h<f)1/(b+h)`. For `d_f=log(1+1/f)−1/(b+f)`, `0≤d_f≤b/[f(b+f)]`. The expected cost on the first `εv` terms is `O(ε)` and its tail after `Mv` is `O((1+log M)/M)`. The intervening Riemann limit is bounded by `2∫ log(1+s)/[s(1+s)] ds=π²/3`. Jensen and the truncated lower comparison thus give `exp(−π²/3)/2` for all sufficiently large labels, independent of the initial leaf-placement shape and threshold in `[p,1)`.
- **Clock and spatial cutoff.** At each outside step the exponential wait at the adapted total rate `B_f+Δ_f` is conditionally independent of that step's winner. Red choices advance no auxiliary time. Maximal variance and the compensator error are `O_M(1/v)`, giving `log(1+f/v)` on a bounded outside-index window. Keep exterior degree slots but omit root-to-tag returns from the outside clock. Couple the independent inert old side and the rate-one immigrant driver up to the first actual tag-to-core send. Early sends cost `O(ε)`; later sends cost `O(1/v)` with fixed clipped parameters and bounded core stock, whose stop is removed using the envelope. No discrete global denominator coupling is needed. Ordered incoming proposals for the continuous-time local readout use the reversed-path bound. Away from time zero the deficit is monotone with tight variation, so a small clock displacement changes the weighted integral by a vanishing amount. This delivers the same `g` as in the interior theorem.
- **Normalization and joint limit.** If `b=v−1/2`, the fixed-size physical coefficient is `Γ(v)E[Y^(−b)]/Γ(1/2)` and the beta coefficient is `Γ(v)/[Γ(1/2)Γ(b+1)]`. Their ratio is `Γ(b+1)E[Y^(−b)]`, including the factor `b`. Uniformity in threshold permits taking the fixed-label endpoint limit first. Given `θ` and the long no-outside prefix, the first scaled red wait shifts by `uk=O(1)`. Keep `θ^k` inside the mixture, and keep the **same** `k` at the overshoot threshold; this gives product survival `e^(−z−y)`. Center by `K−v`. For the tilted amplitude, choose a diagonal sequence in a metric for weak convergence and divide out the Pareto factor, which tends to one in probability. This proves the stated exponential limit for the tilted amplitude, not for the unweighted law.
