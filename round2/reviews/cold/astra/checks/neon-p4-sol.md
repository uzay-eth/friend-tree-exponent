## Joint signed corrector without a special entrance path

The same file builds a broader, finite-to-infinite certificate on the old multicore mask: `n>=13`, at least five cores, higher rows owned by the focal apex only. It follows **every** physical crown path from the true newborn through the finite size cap. At paid states put `q(p)=a+bp=δ+_higher(p)-δ−_higher(p)/t`, summing all six higher families before optimization. Original nonnegative lower-witness charged rows make `q(p)<=δ+_higher(p)`, even when the contrast is negative. Outside the paid mask use zero reward. The binary payload row reader is inherited, with exact signed row floors tested on every supported admitted motif. The mover reconstructs the literal ordinary tree instead of treating descendant shapes as independent arms.

For each crown `z`, let `S(z)` be its nonnegative P4 seam price on the paid mask (zero off the bare face). The controller can either stop and take that certified lower continuation, or pay the signed reward until the next crown jump and continue. This is a proof device: the payoff of the chosen policy is bounded by the one actual **nonnegative** masked upper-witness lifetime integral. A stop with zero price discards the rest, and one with positive price uses only the future P4 continuation, never another joint installment. Define the backwards value by rounding down the robust continue value, then comparing with the stop value:

```text
C(z) = floor_grid min_(s,p) [q(p) + Σ_y r_z(y)V(y) + p V(z+root)]
                             / [s+n-1/D_root+p]
V(z) = max(S(z), C(z)) within the cap;
V(z) = S(z) at exit.
```

`r_z` contains all arrows whose source and target are inside the crown. The **same** external rate `p` enters the reward, successor, and holding loss. Signed correctness comes from checking the cross-multiplied inequality at both endpoints of `s,p`, not monotone discounting of the signed reward. A stop branch needs no generator inequality; its continuation is independently priced from the current state. External events only vary predictable `p` during a holding interval. Conditional stopped Dynkin on each level, then induction through the count cap and the P4 seam, bounds the genuine lifetime from every physical birth environment. This uses no full-tree sup ratio, conditional independence, or product of rare prescribed entrance events.
