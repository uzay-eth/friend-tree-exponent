# Cold referee: rematching the two newborn virgins

The joint residual promotion may rematch the two virgins **without altering either physical tree**. This is valid for the exact signed operator `T_s=Δ_o V_s C` and for its repeated applications to functions of unmarked planted shapes. It tightens the matching-dependent toll on a reachable row, but does not change the exact operator, prove a stronger uniform contraction, or certify a production table.

## Construction and preservation

Keep permanent vertex IDs separately in the two marginal trees. The cross-copy common tree is a third object: a partial rooted correspondence of those IDs. At a common source match common-target choices at their minimum rates. At the observer, with degrees `A≥B`, every residual pair of one A-private target and one B-common target (including the inert slot) has rate `1/(AB)`. Both targets undergo their actual physical update before any correspondence changes. The construction may depend on the full past, provided that the rate law of each next marginal arrow conditional on that past is still its ordinary reciprocal-degree law.

On the proposed event, A promotes a private leaf `a`, B promotes its member `b` of a shared LL slot, and A's old partner `a_old` of `b` stays a leaf. The new subtrees at `a` and `b` each consist of a virgin core and one fresh structural leaf. Remove the old link `a_old↔b`, add `a↔b`, and pair the two fresh leaves. This is an adapted change of the matching, not deletion, transfer or relabelling of physical vertices. The observer degrees need not agree. The old A partner is a genuinely private leaf thereafter, including its live rate-one return. The common tree is still connected and parent-preserving, the new pair is clean with equal neighbour counts, and the only newly unmatched neighbour at the even root lies in A as required. Other matched arms retain their links and their dirt. In `(u,v,c,e,l,k)` the successor changes `u→c` while `l` is unchanged (the identity of one private leaf changes); the fixed-matching successor instead changes `u→v` and `l→k`.

The next conditional labelled generator on each marginal is unchanged: synchronized mass plus residual mass sums to the same selection probability for every actual target, including the null inert slot. Following a rematch, freshly coupled driver increments can be drawn conditional on the current pair; no dormant scheduled clock must continue to be shared with its old partner. Nonexplosion and uniqueness of the ordinary pure-jump law give the same entire marginal law, not just the same post-tick state. No relabelled root, absolute-depth change, or extra physical jump is introduced.

## Exact reachable controls

1. **The `J+ / J` residual promotion.** The paired tick has rate `1/6` at root degrees 3 and 2. Fixed and rematched successors have identical labelled physical trees. The signed leaf reward remains `+1/3`. The exact signed occupation atom at the virgin is `1/3−1/2=−1/6` regardless of the coupling. The *correspondence-based bound* goes from `5/6` with fixed partners to `1/6` after matching the virgins. An evaluator doing full canonical-shape cancellation already sees `1/6` with the fixed pair; the benefit of rematching is for the clean/dirty comparison and its future dynamics. The preexisting A leaf, now private, can subsequently be promoted simultaneously with a B-only hit on the rematched core at rate `1/6`; the pair then really becomes dirty. Treating the clean label as permanent would be wrong.
2. **A genuinely nonidentical arm.** Start with a common root with a leaf and a virgin child, plus a private A leaf. At rate `1/12`, a residual tick promotes that private leaf while adding a second structural leaf to B's older virgin child. At rate `1/2`, the private A core sends a new private A leaf to the root. At rate `1/15`, the new private leaf and the true shared LL promote and can be safely rematched. Root degrees are then 5 and 3; the older B arm is still a two-leaf core. The exact signed measure has `+4/15` on the virgin and `−1/3` on the two-leaf core. The proper rematch keeps the older dirty arm and has a correspondence toll of `13/15`. One can even forcibly re-pair the new A virgin with the older two-leaf B arm in this fixture while retaining a rooted nested map by changing other links; retaining all physical arms and charging the resulting dirt preserves the marginal laws, but raises the correspondence toll to `19/15`. Pricing the forced nonidentical pair as clean would pay only `|1/5−1/3|=2/15` for it; the bounded indicator of the two-leaf core sees `1/3`. Deleting the older B arm loses that atom outright and changes its labelled generator.

## When labels matter, and what iteration requires

For `T_s`, a child test receives its complete unmarked rooted fringe with one inert parent slot. Equal shapes have the same `C`-selected state, the same reset options, and the same future intrinsic law. In particular, an arbitrary earlier selected root, old source ID, old matching, or the fact that two promotions arrived through different arrows does not enter the continuation. Condition at each residence or observer selection, then apply the shape-defined continuation. The positive pair kernel can cancel equal shapes at the same elapsed age for any bounded shape/age test, so total-time tilts and further `T_s` applications are admissible; there is no need to preserve a single old correspondence down the whole expansion. Even when using a clean match, cancel only the equal mass and keep the excess coefficient. Future loss of equality under exterior sends must still be paid by the current residence.

An observable that retains a source tag, birth mark, distinguished old edge, or an individual-path mask distinguishes some of these outcomes. For instance, testing whether the selected B core has the old shared vertex's label gives a signed mass `−1/2` on the first witness, not a cancellation with the new A core. The appropriate equality test must then include the retained information. In particular, this rematch cannot be substituted silently into a proof tracking a fixed old path or first infection of a named physical site. Both witness arms have the same original depth, so original depth alone does not distinguish them.

## Extensions and limits of this verdict

- For instantaneous occupation one may aggregate all equal canonical child shapes, including duplicate multiplicities, before taking absolute mass. This requires no change to the physical coupling and is never worse than correspondence-only matching at that age. The census improvement demonstrated here is different from improving the exact physical TV of `T_s`.
- A continuation rematch beyond the virgin event is valid when it pairs entire isomorphic rooted child subtrees and leaves a parent-preserving, parity-nested partial correspondence on the rest. Equal root degree, equal immediate stock or a shared creation time is not enough. A nonidentical arm may remain paired **as dirty** if the correspondence has the required nesting, or remain unmatched where the invariant allows it. Its full signed contribution still belongs in the toll.
- A new finite comparison must incorporate the joint rematch successor at its actual rate, pay all uncertainty and continuation terms under the resulting census, and validate its own exit and global degree bounds. An upper bound does not automatically improve merely because the new one-step toll decreases. Under the local switch the dirty count `v+e+l+k` does not increase on that switch (it increases by one on the equivalent fixed residual event), but this observation is not a replay of any revised solver.
