# Cold audit: moving-cutoff original low forest

**Verdict: the diagonal transfer (`open/forest-age/diagonal/README.md`) holds**, conditional on the prior physical mean/renewal estimates, `C_n/n^mu→X>0`, the one-ended oriented core law and its mass transport, and the stationary low-radius and degree-tail bounds. The large-cutoff Pareto/inert consequences additionally use the prior stationary forest-age coupling and the inert size and core limits. None of the finite fixtures independently establishes those prior results. This audit is only of Astra's diagonal, not Neon-2 Claim 5.

## Uniform cylinder estimate on the actual jump chain

For a bounded downward score, its planted fringe restores the one physical parent slot at a nonfounder. Boundary total degrees can be read from leaf counts and all incident core slots, including children just outside the nominal radius: if the cylinder records only induced edges and leaf marks at its boundary, charge one extra level. Capped renewal birth labels are auxiliary and do not replace actual degrees. Apply the weighted physical mean expansion to a bounded planted-fringe test; it is bounded in the fringe weight norm since `W≥1`. The founder contributes at most the norm of the test. The expansion is uniform in the initial finite compatible tree, not just the initial edge. Its leading count coefficient is the same as for the constant-one score; centering by `pi f` therefore kills it before dePoissonization. The downward case uses the planted remainder directly; it does not need ancestor reconstruction by a positive-generation sum. Thus the `4^k` allowance is safe, with an absolute prefactor that need not equal the one-cell `C` of the earlier audit. Use the explicit spectral margin `eta=2^-348` of [the effective-cutoff review](age-effective-sol.md) as a conditional input to the exponential remainder, not as a numerical threshold for entering the asymptotic range.

For a one-birth perturbation, use the physical dirty-pair coupling. Adding at a core dirties its two copies; promoting a leaf additionally creates a dirty core and dirties both copies of the old parent. Match births from clean pairs and leave physical degree slots in place. A dirty core initiates at rate one and can create one dirty core or infect one pair; a dirty leaf's initiation does not promote a core. Descendant reads agree after discarding each dirty vertex and at most its first `k` ancestors on each side. Clean matching preserves the ancestry of new paired cores; an old root whose truncated descendant read can differ has a dirty vertex in that read on at least one side. Thus the sum difference in conditional means at remaining time `u` is at most `6(k+1)||f|| e^(2u)`, allowing the doubled sup norm for changed matched summands. This is a count of affected ancestors, not a degree or volume moment. The same argument at lag zero bounds one genuine jump. Centering can simply double the norm. No bounded maximum degree in the physical tree is required for this estimate.

Restart from the actual `T_m`, `m=floor(n^.99)`, with a size-only Yule clock independent of its discrete arrows. At `h=log(n/m)`, the terminal size is a sum of `m` independent geometrics of parameter `m/n`. For `p∈(0,1]`, the centered geometric scaled by `p` has a uniformly bounded exponential moment in a fixed neighborhood of zero, giving a uniform quadratic log-mgf there. The clock deviation `n^.55` has Chernoff exponent `m n^1.1/n²`, of order `n^.09`. The same mgf gives the continuous-time size-stop error: apply Doob to `exp(theta e^(-s)N_s)` for small **fixed** `theta`; its terminal expectation is at most `exp(m(theta+O(theta²)))`, and the stop boundary gives `exp(2 theta m)`. The exponential is a submartingale by Jensen on the Yule size martingale.

The stopped backward forecast has squared-jump compensator at most `∫_0^h 2m e^s J_f² e^(4(h−s)) ds≤2m J_f² e^(4h)/3`. Its maximal jump is at most `J_f e^(2h)`; the terminal deviation `J_f n^.55` gives bracket-to-deviation exponent `2·.55−1.03=.07`, with the jump term of smaller order (`.55+.02<1.03`). Continuous size boundary crossing is not needed for the compensator: the rate uses the predictable size immediately before each jump. Standard exponential jump compensation gives the stated summable bound. On coupling the same discrete history at sizes `S` and `n`, the error is at most `J_f |S−n|`; no monotonicity of signed tests is used. The conditional failure bounds are uniform in `T_m` so they bound the original-chain events even if the auxiliary clock is only introduced separately for each `n` and test. Union bound over the deterministic lists, then Borel–Cantelli, then divide by `C_n`. The killed leading term has only the prefactor `e^(mu h)C_m/C_n→1`, so no rate for `X` is used. The clock and martingale bounds do not need an effective numerical spectral time to be summable.

## Census, tails, and simultaneous cutoffs

The top of a nonfounder retained core component has a high **parent**, not merely a high neighbor. Scan high parents and low core children. With core height measured in edges, reject exactly when a retained core is reached at depth `j` from the child. A retained core's real leaves always survive; an original core with all its other incident edges cut can now look like an ordinary leaf, and an original bare core can become an isolated singleton. Compute original `K` before forgetting these kinds. A degree-`≤Nmax` short branch has at most `(Nmax+1)^(j+3)=U` ordinary vertices, and the marked census is determined by depth `j+3` from the parent, with the boundary-degree convention above. For short-component survivor counts a high parent contributes at most `BU`, including when each possible distinguished core is a separate coordinate. Different occurrences of the same coordinate must keep their multiplicity.

The admitted coordinates form a deterministic finite alphabet. If its cardinality is `Tn`, the labelled encoding bound gives `log Tn≤ O(U log(U+Nmax))+log B+log U`, so `log Tn=n^{o(1)}` for fixed `alpha<1/3`. All `2^Tn` sign tests, multiplied by the number of cutoffs, still have log cardinality `n^{o(1)}`. Their sup norm is at most `BU` because they put one sign on each census atom, not because each coordinate can be separately estimated and the errors summed. Their maximum realizes the full mass norm of the signed measure. This pays simultaneously for all integer `2≤N≤Nmax` on a single event. The same event allows a sample-dependent cutoff lying in that range and diverging on the event; no measurability assertion about the artificial continuous parent approximation is needed to take this pathwise supremum. For simultaneity over all fixed `alpha<1/3` one can intersect the events for a countable cofinal sequence of rational exponents. At `alpha=1/3`, this particular bound has `j log(Nmax+1)` of order `log n log log n`, so the audit yields no such extension.

The constants in the radius budget check: `j0=3 floor(j/6)≥66 Nmax³+50 log log n−3`, hence `j0≥64 N³` for large `n` and `0.08 j0≥4 log log n`. A top-to-descendant path of length `j` makes *each* original survivor's radius at least `j/2` by the triangle inequality, so the prior radius bound charges the entire discarded survivor mass. For a short component with an over-cap parent, reverse each surviving-core-to-parent path by mass transport. There are at most `c_parent` first choices and at most `U` low-path continuations across all depths. Multiplying by `pi(c 1{D>B})≤K B^-1/8` gives `K U^-3` when `B=U^32`, also negligible at the claimed log scale. These are stationary bounds, not finite-tree radius assertions.

For the finite tail, total surviving-core density minus admitted **surviving-core** density is a nonnegative exact census identity. Apply the cylinder estimate to both terms and bound the corresponding stationary remainder by the preceding costs. The founder can account for many surviving cores even though only one component; this subtraction handles it without approximating its survivor cost by `1/C_n`. Omitted component-count mass is bounded by this survivor remainder on both sides. Consequently (3) holds in the full mass norm for the two measures, also when the survivor is distinguished. Since `BU`, `J_f/||f||`, and `4^k` are `n^{o(1)}` and `mu>.55`, the error can be absorbed into `n^-eta/200` for sufficiently large path-dependent `n`. The denominator `d_N~A N^-mu log N` dominates both errors for every diverging permitted `N`, including one diverging very slowly. Dividing the moving survivor law by `pi(D≤N)→1` is a different normalization; the normalized component law is divided by the vanishing `d_N`.

## Which marks can be compared in TV?

At each `n,N`, the empirical and stationary **exact degree** marks live on the same lattice of ratios `(N+1)/N,(N+2)/N,…`, plus the finite-tree founder symbol. The ratio and its log are equivalent encodings. Norm approximation remains true for arbitrary measurable tests there, jointly with the entire observed rooted component and original `K`, or for any projection of those marks. In contrast, for every fixed `N` the lattice has probability one for the actual parent law (include the founder symbol) and zero for the continuous Pareto target. Thus the joint distance to that continuous target is maximal in TV, even after dropping the component marks. Bounded tests Lipschitz in log relative degree and arbitrary in the discrete marks are the correct mixed comparison for the separate large-`N` stationary approximation. There is no empirical observation of the latent parent amplitude or promotion ages.

The inclusion of `K` in the target is justified only on the prior successful path coupling: the entire retained branch agrees with the inert branch, and the one omitted parent slot is restored when classifying the inert root. If there has been a further degree cut, even a nonroot original core can look like a terminal leaf in the visible shape. An exact counterexample to reconstructing `K` from the observed graph uses cutoff two and rooted parent vectors `(0,0,0,3)` and `(0,0,0,3,4,5,5)` (labels start at zero, founders first). In both, the admitted top is vertex 3, its deleted parent has degree three, and the observed top-rooted tree is the edge `3–4`. In the first, vertex 4 is an original leaf (`K=1`); in the second it is a degree-two original core with high child 5 (`K=2`). Their `(ratio,F)` atoms agree exactly and their `K` atoms do not. This is a counterexample to a stronger reconstruction rule, not to the diagonal. The proof's use of original kinds and the no-further-cut event avoids it. After forgetting the parent ratio, the prior stationary coupling gives TV for the `(F,K)` **moving** mixture. On that mixture the two size exponents use the same uniform age and are `(mu V,V)`, independent of the continuous parent mark in the weak limit.

Uniform original survivors are a core-size-biased observation; TV errors of the component sample need not survive unbounded `K` weighting. Here the survivor comparison comes from its own census. Its local limit follows by opening any fixed finite ball under `pi`, while the entire component is compared only to the moving stationary law. Uniform surviving ordinary vertices are overwhelmingly isolated original leaves since at most `(N+1)C_n=o(n)` ordinary vertices lie in core-containing pieces and all original leaves survive. The audit makes no extremal-component, sibling-tuple or faster-cutoff claim.
