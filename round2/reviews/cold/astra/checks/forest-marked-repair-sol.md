### Cardinality obstruction

For a deterministic admitted alphabet of size `T`, the exact mass norm is the maximum over `2^T` sign vectors. Its union budget in log units is `T log 2`. At `N≥5`, a full binary core skeleton of height `h=floor(2 log_2 log n)` with `1–4` leaves independently attached to each of its `L=2^h` bottom cores gives at least `4^L/2^(L−1)=2^(L+1)` admissible types at the original growing maximum cutoffs. The physical parent of the top has degree `N+1`, the maximum retained degree is five, and the type has size `O((log n)^2)`. Original core flags recover the binary skeleton; child swaps give a group of order `2^(L−1)`. Every labelled realization is reachable by successive single-vertex attachments, since every target in a nontrivial physical tree has a neighbour with positive probability of sending to it. This invalidates the asserted cardinality, independently of whether another proof of the estimate exists.

The counted-component mixing conclusion is the imported product approximation: bounded tests uniformly Lipschitz in log relative parent degree, arbitrary in whole shape and `K`; the parent factor is Pareto and the tree is the inert virgin at independent uniform age in `[0,log N]`.
