## Physical support and ownership

- Five cores do not imply size above 12. A planted host with four direct virgin cores has size 9 with no host stock, 10 with one host leaf, and 12 if one tip at the latter crown carries three leaves. All three are excluded. The next example, with size 13, passes. These are ordinary parent arrays with the exterior parent removed from the focal count.
- At a paid state the host has `k≥4` direct core children, and the lower bound `1+L_host+Σ_exact(D_i+k_i)+Σ_overflow(cap+1)` exceeds 12. An exact tip contributes itself and its `D_i−1` downward neighbors; each of its `k_i` core children contributes at least one additional, distinct downward vertex. Overflow only needs its degree bound. The broad, degree-2, large-only and tagged replays all check this gate; the tagged gate uses two vertices per untracked younger arm, not a synthetic donor in the reward. The whole physical fringe therefore has at least five cores and size at least 13. This excludes the size-12 ledger and the size-13–20 higher ledger, which requires three or four cores. The founder is omitted.
- Each unordered **physical** triple is registered at its latest promotion: choose two of the previously registered children. Three equal descriptors in one triple give one occurrence, not six. A class of `m` equal tips contains `binom(m,3)` different triples. The replay enumerates distinct physical indices before grouping and checks the closed multiplicity by falling factorials; it also checks all 720 orders of six tip promotions. The three deleted-tip transfers within one S3 charge are three incidences, including when their descriptor values agree.
- Pinned high-parent and EDGE support (`occupation-neon/high-parent/README.md#physical-charge-and-disjointness`) pays the applicable `g/hC`, first-body and frozen inner-edge rows. The `ead947b` joint memory price (`occupation-neon/interpolate/compose/residual/edge-projection/combined/FREEZE.md`) spends residual `f/g`; it does not spend an S3 row or price all siblings of a paid motif by default. [P4](p4-band-sol.md) is a down-chain owner; Yb (`pressure-memory/yb-lifetime/README.md`) has different short and middle roles and a deep tip. The checker plants a crown on which all three unpaid masks fire, with shared physical vertices and a shared P4/Yb edge. The keys `(family, labelled embedding)` are distinct. Connected-deletion transfers were already subtracted from the lower-family row and added to the higher one in the original signed decomposition, so sharing a receive or a promotion is not double payment.

The original `λ`, sign, table scales and centers are retained. All 3,360 cap-five paid charge classes per sign match the producer, with nonnegative signed row corners, plus 12 uncapped Bernstein fixtures per sign.

The independent recursive replay visits 74,612 projected states per sign for each cap-five objective (70,442 nonzero broad reward rows; 69,252 large-only). The degree-two kernel visits 209 states, 60 paid. The exact host receive enters reward and gain together. A selected tip's unknown descendant receive takes the sign-correct worst corner *in the reward*, and its conditional rate range is still used in the continuation; allowing these two controls to vary separately is a lower relaxation. For the gain-rate box the extremum of the positive-denominator ratio is attained at a vertex. For auxiliary tip losses the minimizing ratio adds rates in increasing order of destination value, stopping when the next destination cannot lower it. Birth updates arity at fixed degree and appends a real virgin. Out-of-cap tips cannot return but remain exterior donors; beyond the host domain the reward is permanently zero. Downward grid rounding preserves the discounted subsolution. The stable-age conversion uses `HI` in the positive discounted integral and `LO` for the physical multiplier. The physical mean and stable-age identities and exhaustive D18 GMP nonnegativity are inherited, not rerun.

## Fifth-donor defect and matched repair

Write `a` for the intercept with selected-tip external tolls already minimized, `r` for the signed host slope, `z` for the fourth child's reciprocal, and `w` for the host's mother plus the remaining `k−4` children. The reduced row is `a+r(z+w)`, with `0≤w≤(k−3)/2`. The **flat** worst corner over *all* these donors is `a+min(0,r)(k−2)/2`, since `z` has its own half-rate. The producer uses `(k−3)/2` in that flat baseline and keeps it when adding the fourth-donor credit `rz−min(0,r)/2`. For `r<0`, the tagged-aware score exceeds the correct one by `−r/2`, even with the true reciprocal shared in the gain. The repair is to use `(k−2)/2` in both flat baselines, or use `a+rz+min(0,r)(k−3)/2` directly for the aware reward.

The replay reproduces the original numbers but finds **105/12** flat and **144/18** aware violating corners (lower/upper). For example the physical upper crown with host `(1,4,1)`, selected tips bare/bare/virgin, mother of degree 2 and fourth tip of degree 3 has 13 planted vertices and seven cores. Its reduced slope is `−2031689/10^11`; the producer's flat and aware formulas exceed the reduced row at the actual host receive by `−r/3` and `−r/2`, respectively. This exhibits failure of the asserted worst-corner bound after tip pressures were minimized; the full raw row with those particular descendant rates may be larger. Both corrected versions pass every retained tagged corner. The corrected **differences of floors** are `646356975017116981369059/25000000000000000000000000000000000` (lower) and `7956231334368412216201857/50000000000000000000000000000000000` (upper). Even corrected, these are nested diagnostics and are not booked separately. The broad solve uses `a+r(total_receive−selected_receive)` per triple and has no such baseline error.
