## Sign: displayed relaxation and strengthened adverse cases

The displayed examples are valid for the two externally published `Ke2` intervals, the displayed scalar cone, the two numerical `C` factors in the displayed vector, and the two depth lower seeds throughout the prior. Normalize each row against `g_i=3i/(i+4)` at the model crossing; `g` is positive, so its exact eigenvalue is the spectral radius. The trial radii are `0.999999936132` and `1.000000063868`. This does not import the physical exponent interval as a numerical sign premise; its upper endpoint is used to compute uniform seed floors. I treated the printed cone and entry intervals as inputs, and did not rerun the four-core conditional lookahead or claim feasibility for unpublished individual action intervals.

One additional exact datum rejects **both original models**: `(K_1(s))_{i,2}=1/[2(s+1/i+3/2)]`. With no copy, a degree-two root can pay only during the virgin residence; a promotion leaves the degree unchanged but makes the root bare. The common `1/(1+s)` profiles do not have that holding-time transform. Merely imposing positive time weights, an upper bound on weighted moments, or a better recentering formula does not itself supply this equality or a tail anchor.

Here is a stronger obstruction with the same two crossings `s*=.565728±1e-7`:

1. Retain the **exact** two output matrices of the size-eight/four-core, at-most-one-copy killed family, at every `s`, not just their floors at `U`. Let `E_i(s)` be the Laplace transform of its weighted first-exit measure, including exits from spending the second copy and using the weight `W` at the successor. It is a finite positive renewal sum. This retains the virgin column of `K_1` exactly, since the omitted no-copy paths cannot end in a paying degree-two root.
2. Convolve that exit measure with `h(t)=exp(−.19t)(1−exp(−20t))`. Add the resulting mass in columns 2 and 20, with row multipliers `p_i,q_i≥0`, all at depth three. This last depth is an abstract relaxation: first-exit types, successor marks, and precise post-exit copy counts are not imposed. In particular the added mass does not change the first two **killed** depth matrices. Its transform is `E_i(s) H(s)`, `H(s)=1/(s+.19)−1/(s+20.19)`.
3. At `s*`, set `p_i` to the positive part of `(.205+.095*(i−2)/18)−K_kill(i,2)`, divided by `E_i H`; fill column 20 to make `(K(s*)g)_i=g_i`. Both fills are nonnegative and `max_i(p_i+q_i)<.294`. Exact comparisons verify both named column intervals, the cone, both numerical `C` contractions (even `.976433602362` at `.56564`), and strict same-direction signs in all 19 trial action ratios. The full families have positive time densities and are strictly decreasing in real discount, hence the designated crossings are genuine and unique on the prior.

This pair handles sharper positive interpolation directly. Thus any nonnegative time test after exponential tilt is bounded on these examples by the same sharper envelope: powers, centered even powers, positive interpolation remainders, and tests using both exit and post-exit times.
