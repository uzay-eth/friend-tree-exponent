## Weakest stochastic step, reconstructed (§5)

The one-leaf coupling needs both dirty copies of a hit clean recipient. A dirty source can add at most two dirty cores per ring, and a synchronized clean promotion creates a matched pair. When a clean source selects a dirty matched neighbour, it still has the same neighbour slot on both sides and no other clean invariant changes; private leaf-ring activity at dirty roots stays local. For a promoted old leaf, the larger tree has the unpaired new core and the old parent is dirty on both sides. There is no increase in dirty count from leaf rings. Hence `E D_dirty(u)≤3e^(2u)`, with true physical marginals. At fixed terminal time `b` the backward martingale has finite pathwise variation between its finitely many jumps; no continuous quadratic variation. Compensation with predictable total rate `n(T(u−))` gives exactly

```
Var_T A_k(T(b)) ≤ 3 n(T)e^(4b)(1−e^(−3b)).
```

For `g_h(T(1))`, the jump bound before normalization is `3e^(2(h+1-u))`, so after multiplying by `d_2^-1 e^(−μh)`, the variance bound keeps `e^((4−2μ)h)` (the claim uses the looser `e^(4h)`). Set `η=μ−σ>0` and `δ=2μ−1>0`. The uniform approximation to `Ψ` is `|Ψ−g_h|≤(K_2/d_2)e^(−ηh) C`; at time one use `E C_1²≤2e² C_0²`. Thus with a universal constant `K`,

```
E_T[(M_(j+1)−M_j)^2 | F_j]
 ≤ K e^(−2μj)[e^((4−2μ)h)n(T(j)) + e^(−2ηh)(1+Ψ(T(j))²)].
```

With `h=log(j+2)/η`, averaging the first term gives `K n(T)(j+2)^((4−2μ)/η)e^(−δj)` and the second gives `K(j+2)^−2 (e^(−2μj)+E M_j²)`; the author's looser exponent works as well. If `a_j=E M_j²`, iteration of `a_(j+1)≤(1+b_j)a_j+c_j` yields `a_j≤exp(Σb_i)(a_0+Σc_i)` for nonnegative summable `b_i,c_i`, precisely with constants independent of the deterministic restart seed. Jensen bounds every time between integers. Thus the continuous limit is closed in `L²` and retains its conditional mean.

For strict positivity, from the earlier pathwise log exponent at the Yule epochs, and `τ_n=log n+O(1)` almost surely, `C(T(j))=exp((μ+o(1))j)` and `n(T(j))=exp((1+o(1))j)`. The reverse comparison gives `n(T(j))/Ψ(T(j))²≤exp(−(δ+o(1))j)` (up to an immaterial fixed prefactor). At deterministic `j` the conditional law of `e^(μj)M_∞` is that of the restarted limiting martingale. Conditional Cauchy–Schwarz gives `P(M_∞>0|F_j)≥Ψ(T(j))²/[K_{10}(Ψ(T(j))²+n(T(j))+1)]`. This is why merely establishing a positive initial mean would not suffice and why using the earlier logarithmic, rather than the desired normalized, limit is not circular.
