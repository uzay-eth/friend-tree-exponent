## Projection and all-time justification

* **Rates and reward use the same pressure.** A gain at path slot `i` has leaf pressure `L_i`, the indicated path neighbor reciprocals, plus exactly one external mother reciprocal at `A` or the hidden side sum at `B`. The same exterior pressure appears in the affine running reward for a complete path, and is the same variable in numerator and denominator. The code's transition credit is an algebraic way to include this rate-linear instantaneous reward. A send from mother to `A`, or side child to `B`, changes only the actual recipient's degree. In particular it does not make the sending donor older in the projected degree.
* **Mother cap.** Given current exact mother degree `m`, her gain has lower rate `1/D_A` and upper `1/D_A + m − 3/2`: among her `m−1` other neighbors, her own parent is a core contributing at most `1/2`, and every other neighbor contributes at most 1. This use requires a nonfounder mother; founder/direct-child contributions vanish in the stationary law. Her own promotions can change future hidden pressure but do not change the retained degree. For an exact side child `(d,k)`, its gain lies in `[L+1/D_B,L+1/D_B+k/2]`; its promotion rate is `L/d`. A donor exiting a cap never reenters, and its real reciprocal stays in `(0,1/(cap+1)]` (cap zero uses `1/2`). The tails need no fictitious resetting age.
* **Multi-child phase creation.** On a tip promotion the old prefix continues with one more core child, and the new prefix extends to the promoted virgin. These are two additive values at **one** physical rate `L/D`. At `B`, the new child is a side donor in the old `AB` prefix, but is the path tip in the emitted `ABC`, where only older siblings are side donors. Earlier `ABC`/`ABCD` tags independently keep the later child as an additional side donor when `B` promotes it. Summing their additive conditional values does not require independence. Keeping only the newly emitted cohort would miss physically valid older paths.
* **Irreversibility and all ages.** Overshooting a prescribed degree or arity cannot be undone. A nonfull prefix at its tip's final arity can emit no more matching extensions; its previously emitted longer prefixes remain separate. The finite projected state graph progresses by monotone degrees, arities, registration, or prefix length, with irreversible tail saturation. Unseen physical arrows that leave its state unchanged have zero drift on the compared value, while their influence on subsequent rates is covered by the conditional bounds at each age. The rectangle over rates yields Bellman sub-/supersolutions for the physical process even with predictable adaptive hidden pressures; no Markov assumption for the projected process is needed. Solving these inequalities at each state prices the full discounted lifetime, rather than extending the labelled fixture by simulation. Discount endpoints `LO,HI` are allowed inside the Bellman rectangle; the final product by the true `μ` uses both endpoints, also for signed bounds.
* **Physical Palm normalization.** The row starts at the real `A` promotion in its joint live environment. Condition on the mother's degree at that moment, not the stationary observation time. The physical stable-age formula integrates at independent `Exp(μ)` under that birth Palm law; it supplies the factor `μ`. This is the law from the physical birth compensation in `occupation-neon/renewal/next/README.md`, not a Perron-label law. The positive stationary mass also follows from the strictly positive certified contrast and the positive upper reward on every occurrence.

Parent degree is nondecreasing, so the upper gain bound holds at every subsequent age, for all birth environments in the class. Use nonnegative reward `b 1{D in j}` and omit rewards above degree 34, making the lower inequality valid all-time. Do not add a residual kill or use the cutoff to discard rewards in the P4 sign computation itself. Exact rational DP with downward `10^-16` rounding produces `A`.
