## Rare-event sandwich and late correction

On the graph-history space (collapse all winners attaching to the tag), the finite likelihood is exactly the product of `1−Δ/R` or `1+Δ/B`. All outside-arrow identities remain visible. The finite products are nonnegative martingales under the **unconditioned** reference. The subprobability with density `L∞` is dominated by the physical path measure on cylinders by Fatou, then on the full σ-field by the monotone class theorem. The endpoint is the *same graph-path functional* under both measures. This gives the lower bound even if a singular physical component exists. For the upper bound, conditional on a prefix, lock `A₀`: the red count reference increases at least as often as the physical count by a common-uniform monotone coupling. The upper bound therefore uses the exact prefix density `L_i` and the posterior `Beta(R_i,B_i)`; it does not use an independent future share. Both inequalities can be normalized by the rare beta survival **before** any limit. The resulting reference law is a beta mixture restricted to `θ>x_v`; conditional on `θ`, the schedule is iid, and `θ→x` for `x_v→x` interior. The outside-index envelope bounds finite-window moments uniformly also for the exponentially small part outside the working compact interval.

The late product cannot be bounded by summing the absolute sizes of the individual log factors. Its signed predictable drift is `Δ(z−θ)/(j z(1−z))`. Stop *before* applying a step whose current state fails the stated tests. With `h=1+i/v` and denominators bounded away from zero in units of `j`, use `Δ≤1+Hh^(1/8)` and `|z−θ|≤(v+H√v h^(3/4))/(v+i)`. The integrated tail of the resulting drift has terms `O(H M^(−7/8))` and `O(H² v^(−1/2) M^(−1/8))`; conditional squares have tail `O(H² v^(−1) M^(−3/4))`. The latter control both the martingale maximal error and the summed log-linearization remainder. The Bernoulli word and outside-index initiation envelope give a summable dyadic stopping bound `O(H⁻²)`, independent of the birth tree; logarithmic moment growth on level `k` is absorbed by `2^(k/8)`. This bounds *all* later log increments on the good event. Use truncated likelihoods for the lower bound, and finite-window uniform integrability for the upper bound. This never assumes uniform integrability at infinite time or equates the two infinite-path laws.

## Clock, returns, and uniform error

Here is a direct coupling that removes the need to justify one global collection of discrete spatial proposal slots. Fix the tilted Bernoulli word. Only the outside jumps get waits: before outside jump `f`, sample an exponential wait of rate `b+f+Δ` and select among the exact genuine outside arrows. The exponentials can be generated from fresh rate-one variates. Intervening tag choices have already prescribed counts and give the tag more leaf stock, without evolving its old or virgin branches. On this auxiliary clock, suppress each branch-root-to-tag channel as a **null internal move**, retaining its slot in the root's degree. Keep an independent rate-one tag clock, with its recipient chosen from its actual neighbours at that stage of the word. Superposing all active clocks gives exactly the outside total rate and conditional winner law. Couple to an independent inert old process and a rate-one clock always starting a fresh independent virgin until the first tag-to-core selection. Drivers of these ideal processes are independent *before* the coupling, not conditional on its success.

Up to total jump index `Mv`, the probability of any early tag initiation in the first `εv` steps is at most `εv/b`. Later, with high probability uniformly in `θ∈[p,q]`, the word has `D_tag≥p εv/2`. The number of core neighbours is at most `1+J_{⌊Mv⌋}`. Summing the conditional tag-to-core hazards `c_tag/[D_tag(B+Δ)]` over outside steps gives `O_{M,p}(1/(εv))` in expectation on that word event, by the envelope. The exceptional word probability vanishes uniformly at fixed `ε>0`. These bounds use only `b` and the schedule, not any initial degree in the old side.

For `f≤Mv`, the auxiliary time immediately before the next wait differs uniformly from `log(1+f/v)` by a term tending to zero in probability: the centered wait sum has maximal variance `O_M(1/v)`, and the difference in compensators from replacing `b+f+Δ` by `b+f` is `O_M((1+J_{⌊Mv⌋})/v)`. The harmonic sum differs from the log by `O_M(1/v)`. The outside count divided by `v` tends uniformly to `(1−θ)s` at total index `vs`. Hence the limiting time is `t(s)=log(1+(1−θ)s)`. It is **not** the whole-tree holding clock, which concentrates instead at `log(1+s)`; even conditioning on the physical endpoint leaves those size-only holding times independent of the physical jump path. Confusing the two clocks produces an apparent `x` dependence. At a fixed positive `ε`, replacing the auxiliary time by its limit in the weighted integral is harmless uniformly: the replica's deficit is monotone with total variation at most `1+P(T)` on a bounded time window (so translation by a small time error costs at most that variation times the error in an unweighted time integral, with bounded kernel away from zero). No smoothness of the old environment is needed for this comparison. In the first `εv` steps, while unchanged, the old root has degree two, its gain rate is ≤2, and the tag rate is one. Thus the chance of any early change to `Δ` is `O(ε)`. This handles the singular starting kernel in probability; finite-window likelihoods are uniformly integrable by the exponential moment of `J_{⌊Mv⌋}`.

The auxiliary construction compares directly to the inert process of the *same finite environment*, before taking any local limit. Thus the previous estimates give uniformly vanishing error in the ratio to the beta cap minus that finite environment's `g`, for leaf placements on compact thresholds, with iterated cutoffs as in the proof. For continuity of `g` in a locally converging sequence, use the old-cell cutoff in continuous time: the fixed old cell containing the degree-two boundary root has old core degree at most two. The reversed-walk bound for proposals reaching it in bounded time is at most `2 T^h/h!` for incoming paths of length `h`. This does not scale with the largest degree outside. The kernel tail is uniformly integrable by `δ≤1+P`; at zero `Eδ(t)≤2t` because the old contribution is ≤1/2 and its first gain intensity is ≤2. In particular the potential is a.s. finite and Jensen gives the stated uniform positive bound. For a ball of multiple observation cells the reversed-walk coefficient is the sum of their incident old core degrees, locally bounded on a convergent sequence; there is no need to claim a universal constant for arbitrary observation balls.

## Attempts to break the two claims

* **`κ` independent of `x`:** The two first-order increments have different signs and their combined weight is `(1−θ)/(1+(1−θ)s)−1/s`. Writing `y=(1−θ)s` gives exactly `−dy/[y(1+y)]` and `t=log(1+y)`, hence `−dt/(e^t−1)`. The old side and independent immigrant process in this clock contain no `θ`. The weight on old inputs is the *leaf-target birth flux* `L_o/D_o`, not the uniformly sampled parent or a leaf sampled by its stock alone. For a core target the birth weight is `r_o/(v−1)`, but the beta bound must retain `a=1/(D_o+1)`; then `a r_o≤1`. This gives the claimed `v^(−1/6)` relative loss after averaging. Only the beta factor supplies the remaining threshold dependence. Bounded `g` and the empirical local law give the score limit; the size normalization is taken using uniform integrability, not independence of the limiting global count and a selected local environment.
* **Geometric prefix:** For `k` fixed, the no-outside event for the first `k` decisions has reference weight `θ^k` and `L_k=1` exactly. Its schedule suffix is iid and only the red stock and total size were incremented by `k`; all old/virgin states, `B`, and `Δ` are unchanged. The full sandwich with this event inserted therefore multiplies the same limiting correction by `x^k`. This gives geometric **survival** for the number of tag choices before the first outside jump (mass `(1−x)x^k` at `k`), not a claim that the first outside *arrow type* is independent of all conditioning. For arbitrary `k`, the upper bound by `q^k+e^(−cv)` follows by splitting the conditional beta mixture at `q`; this upgrades to tightness uniformly on compacts. The overshoot quotient with the prefix inserted gives the stated independent exponential limit. No error is inferred from a finite-label geometric approximation at varying thresholds near 1.
* **Near-one and shared futures:** At fixed label and `ε↓0`, the perfect prefix reaches absolute size of order `1/ε`; the physical first-defect arrow law and the inverse moment of the postdefect amplitude govern the answer. Here `v→∞` at fixed `x`: absolute departure size is `v+O_P(1)` on the rare event and the single early outside choice affects the marked correction with probability tending to zero. The exponent `v−1/2` agrees in the leaf sector, but this does not identify the large-`v` limit of the fixed-label near-one constants or justify exchanging limits. The two regimes and scalings do not contradict one another. Shared futures are already included in the one-path likelihood and the conditional birth score. The adjacent inequality takes a quotient of marginal probabilities, never multiplies two joint rare-event estimates or assumes independent futures for adjacent or sibling labels. It gives no joint adjacent-label rare-event law.
