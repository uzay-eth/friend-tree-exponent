Inputs are the existing deterministic-size empirical uniform-core local limit and UI of the normalized core count. The proof below independently realizes the finite-window dynamics by a clock in the *outside-jump index*. This avoids any need to assume that the whole graph has a tight size or that all birth recipients have locally tight degrees.

## Exact change of measure

For birth at size `v` into a prebirth vertex of degree `d−1`, `a=1/d` and `b=v−a`. Define `R=D−1+a`, `B=j−R`, and `Δ=R−r`, where `D,r` are the tag degree and attachment rate. Collapse tag-target arrows to their one common resulting transition. Preserve the arrow origin and multiplicity for every outside target. With `w=1/D_sender`, the physical outside-arrow probability is `w/j`; the reference is `(B/j)w/(B+Δ)`. Their ratio is `1+Δ/B`, not `1+Δ/j`. For a tag birth the ratio is `1−Δ/R`. Products are exact on finite cylinders. In the reference, outside arrow choices can be generated from fresh randomization after the Bernoulli word is obtained from the beta mixture; they add no information about its parameter conditional on a finite word.

The finite likelihood is a nonnegative reference martingale (before disintegrating). Its limit defines a submeasure of the physical measure by conditional Fatou on cylinders and extension; equality or UI of that martingale is unnecessary. Conversely, from every finite state the physical tail is bounded above by the reference posterior `Beta(R,B)` using the *original* locked deficit `1−a`, since the physical reciprocal deficit is nondecreasing. Thus the endpoint probability is squeezed between the reference integral of `L_∞` on `Θ>x` and that of `L_i` there, for any deterministic `i`. Both bounds remain valid with a finite-prefix restriction measurable by `i`. The signed drift after conditioning on `Θ=θ` is essential: `E(ℓ−1 | state)=Δ(z−θ)/[(v+i)z(1−z)]`, not zero, with `z=R/(v+i)`.

## Finite-window verification via an auxiliary clock

Given fixed `θ` bounded away from both endpoints, sample the iid choice word as independent geometric runs of tag births before successive outside steps. Perform a run instantaneously after the preceding outside jump (or at the start). A tag gain changes neither the outside index `f`, nor `B=b+f`, nor `Δ`: the new leaf can send only to the tag. Between outside jumps run the directed **outside** physical rates in auxiliary time. In particular, ignore sends whose **target** is the tag, and retain the tag's own entire rate-one clock, including selection of its old parent and of previously promoted neighbours. The holding rate is `B+Δ`, and the winning arrow has exactly the required conditional weights. New tag leaves created by the runs have no outside clocks until promoted. All these statements apply with an arbitrary fixed finite choice-word prefix conditioned on in advance, with only a fixed initial shift.

If `T_f` denotes auxiliary time after `f` outside jumps, the compensated holding times have total variance `O(1/v)` on `f≤Mv` by `B≥b`. The difference of the integrated conditional means from `Σ_{g<f}1/(b+g)` is bounded in mean by `Σ_{g<Mv} EΔ_g/(b+g)^2=O_M(1/v)`: `Δ≤1+C`, where the tag-origin count `C` is bounded by the independent Bernoulli envelope of parameters `1/(b+g)`. Maximal variance and the deterministic harmonic approximation give `T_f=log(1+f/v)+o_P(1)` uniformly in that range. The Bernoulli law of large numbers therefore gives `t(s)=log(1+(1−θ)s)` for step `⌊vs⌋`.

Split the outside arrows into the old side (with the tag slot kept), internal arrows of every tag-promoted branch (same port convention), and the independent rate-one tag clock. At each tag ring launch an independent virgin branch in the candidate comparison; physically this agrees whenever the tag chooses a stock leaf. Before any mismatch, couple both systems with identical clocks in the old side and in every born branch, and use the same tag clock. At positive auxiliary time, the tag has accumulated order-`v` gains: the time/index inverse above and the uniform Bernoulli law of large numbers give this on any bounded interval bounded away from zero. It has at most `1+Pois(T)` core neighbours, using the tag clock up to `T`. Conditional choice of a core therefore fails with probability tending to zero on such an interval (stop on stock below a fixed positive multiple of `v` and on too many rings). The probability of any tag ring in `[0,η]` is at most `η`. No outside arrow from a branch can physically affect another branch: a send through the port is a tag birth already represented by the recorded choice word. These arguments give joint local convergence of the old-side root-degree history and all immigration branch histories on fixed time intervals. For locally converging old environments, first run a fixed initial-cell ball with exterior slots; the independent old-cell graphical construction bounds the probability of an exterior influence by a factorially decaying ordered-path estimate, with the endpoint's fixed local core degree. This is applied directly in auxiliary time, where the rates are *exact*, not to a globally coupled discrete reference denomin­ator. Remove the finite ball after comparing the agreeing finite neighbourhoods. The first possible change in the old degree costs at most rate two while it is two, and a first immigration costs rate one, giving `O(η)` for any change at small time. This handles the lower integration boundary where a Taylor expansion of the tag factor would fail.

On `ε≤i/v≤M`, stop at a fixed bound on `Δ` and a lower bound of order `v` on `R,B`. The quadratic log-expansion error is `O(1/v)`; the martingale part of the signed linear increments has the same variance order. The remaining Riemann integral is

```
∫ δ(t(s))[(1−θ)/(1+(1−θ)s)−1/s] ds = −∫ δ(t)/(e^t−1) dt.
```

The small-time event above removes the lower cutoff in probability. At fixed `M`, discarding all tag factors gives `L_⌊Mv⌋≤exp(O_M(1+J_⌊Mv⌋))`. The Bernoulli envelope has uniformly bounded exponential moments of every fixed order there (`Σp=O_M(1)`), hence finite-window UI; the bound even holds for parameters outside the interior compact. In the limiting process `δ≤1+P_t` and `Eδ(t)≤2t`: the first change of the old parent costs rate at most two before it occurs, the old term is at most `1/2`, and the immigrant expectation is at most `t`. This proves integrability of the potential and the claimed environment-uniform positive Jensen bound. Its independence of threshold follows from the time change, not from replacing the rare conditioned graph by the original graph.

## Tail localization, endpoint integral, and scope

For `i≥Mv`, take `H` fixed, stop when the centred Bernoulli count exceeds `H√v(1+i/v)^(3/4)`, or `C_i>H(1+i/v)^(1/8)`, or `z_i` leaves a fixed interior bracket. On dyadic blocks both count failures sum to `O(H^−2)` by maximal variance for the first and a moment bound (`EJ^k=O_k((1+log(1+i/v))^k)`) for the second; the `z_i` bracket is then automatic for large `v,M`. The absolute signed drift sum is `O(HM^(-7/8)+H²v^(-1/2)M^(-1/8))` and the conditional square sum is `O(H²v^(-1)M^(-3/4))`. Indeed the deterministic offset contributes a denominator of order `(v+i)^2` and the count fluctuations carry the additional `√v(1+i/v)^(3/4)`. Stopping lets the same square bound control the log correction and the maximal martingale increments; on good paths the limits exist with bounded-in-probability log perturbation after `Mv`. The order of cutoffs is important: choose `H` large for the exceptional probability, then `M` large at fixed `H`, then `v` large (equivalently apply the corresponding iterated limsups). This does **not** bound infinite-time expectations of the likelihood by exponentiating a moment estimate. For the lower bound use bounded truncations of the limiting likelihood (or bounded Lipschitz functions of its log) in the dominated submeasure; the positive limiting likelihood is at most one. For the upper bound use the finite-prefix posterior and finite-window UI. The tilted beta endpoint concentrates at `x` on `Θ>x`; for `x` in an interior compact the discarded mass above a larger fixed threshold is exponentially small, and the finite-window expectation bound is uniform even there. The two bounds give the same limit `g` after sending `M` to infinity. This proves the sequential local limit for every locally convergent sequence of leaf placements and `x_v→x`, including the compact-uniform conclusion after averaging. No global path UI assertion was used.

**High-degree and nonleaf sector.** The exact prebirth target weight is `r_o/(v−1)`, from *all* its neighbours. In particular, leaf senders can dominate nonleaf placements; a star of size `n` has core-placement probability `(n−1)/n`, but after multiplication by the core's **postbirth** reciprocal it is only `(n−1)/n²`. At a prebirth core of degree `D_o≥2`, `a=1/(D_o+1)≤1/3`. Uniformly down to `a=0` the locked beta upper tail on interior compacts is at most `C a v^(−2/3)(1−x)^(v−1/2)` by the gamma-ratio bound, `1/Γ(a)=O(a)`, and bounding `t^(a−1)` before integrating the remaining power. Thus `Σ_core r_o/(D_o+1)≤C_{v−1}` makes the sector `O(v^(μ−5/3)(1−x)^(v−1/2))`, relatively `O(v^(-1/6))`. Dropping either the reciprocal prefactor or the distinction between pre- and post-degree loses that proof; a leaf recipient has pre-degree one but post-degree two, not one. For a leaf recipient, a specific leaf can be chosen only by its neighboring core. Sum over those leaves to obtain `L_o/[(v−1)D_o]`, with **prebirth** denominator in this expression, and a common old-side law per core. The star example is adverse to neglect by raw birth probability, not to the theorem.

The normalized score is bounded by one and converges sequentially in the marked core topology, giving convergence on compact sets of environments by compactness. Use tightness of the empirical local law and UI of the normalized core count to average; no product independence with `X` is needed. In particular `κ=E[X] π((L_o/D_o)g)/√π_math>0` by `π(L_o/D_o)=μ`. The locked `a=1/2` beta tail contributes exactly `x^(-1/2)v^(-1/2)(1−x)^(v−1/2)/√π_math`, by the endpoint integral and the gamma ratio. Hence the stated equivalent and adjacent ratio `1−x` hold uniformly on each compact interior interval, with eventual strictness there. This is not one cutoff that works at all interior thresholds simultaneously. The same squeeze with a fixed prefix of `k` tag births contributes `x^k`; the compact-uniform equivalent at `x+y/v` gives the independent exponential overshoot with survival `exp(−y/(1−x))` and the geometric prefix survival `x^k`. This does not describe first promotion versus first outside attachment interchangeably.

The older fixed-threshold bounds (`open/age-order/fixed-threshold/README.md`) leave a polynomial gap and allow oscillating prefactors; the new signed physical likelihood closes that gap. In 89ae's fixed-label near-one proof (`open/age-order/near-one-constant/README.md`), the perfect prefix instead grows on the `1/ε` scale, the postdefect inverse moment gives a Gamma-normalized constant, and the conditional entrance size has an inverse-tilted Pareto law. Neither limit can be taken in place of the other; this result covers `v→∞` with threshold in an interior compact.
