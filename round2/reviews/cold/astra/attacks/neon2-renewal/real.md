## 2. Pathwise localization, countable events, fixed size and support

- Stop the discounted free/inert local martingales at finite ordinary sizes. The stopped bracket is bounded by the integral of the *unstopped* expected size before the stop, since the indicator of no stop only reduces the nonnegative integrand. The free size mean is `n_0 e^t`; the inert size mean is at most this value since the inert root-slot selection causes no state change. For each bounded horizon, the stopped integrals converge in `L²` by the vanishing expected bracket after the stop (dominated convergence and nonexplosion); the global tail integral is finite because `2mu>1`. This yields true martingales and preserves their means before the positivity argument. In the free restart argument `{M∞>0}` belongs to `σ(∪_j F_j)`; conditional probabilities are at least `δ` simultaneously at the countably many integer epochs, hence their limit is the indicator. The support or identification of any degree limit is not used in this step.
- For interpolation use the single event obtained by intersecting over `k≥2`, integer mesh denominators `m≥1`, and integer fixed lags `h≥1`, with the martingale convergence/positivity and Yule events. For each `k,m`, the residual converges for every fixed `h` on that event; send `j→∞` first, then `h→∞`. The error from (12) is bounded by `epsilon_k(h) K_4(e^{-mu(t-h)}+M_{t-h})`. Interpolate the *unscaled* cumulative count by monotonicity before sending `m→∞`. Exact-degree counts need not be monotone and are obtained by subtraction on the same event. Cesàro comparison is legitimate after the all-real-time limit; terminal coboundaries divided by the horizon vanish from `C≤K_4(1+Phi)`, and their own jump martingales converge by the same localization. If desired, `c_k/c_2` follows from (12) at `T3` and convolution with the initial rate-two wait to start at the edge: the same factor `2/(2+mu)` multiplies all continuous-time mean amplitudes.
- Transfer to fixed `n` is pathwise along the free Yule epochs, since `tau_n→∞` and `n e^{-tau_n}→W_size>0`. The Gamma shape parameter is **three** for the time origin at `T3`. The identity `M∞=W_size^mu Y∞` uses `a_n/n^mu→1`, not independence of `M∞` and `W_size`. The discrete conditional increment bound holds in the whole jump-chain filtration; by orthogonality it yields the stated deterministic tail-variance bound after conditioning on `T_n` as well. The `L²` transfer for counts uses Doob's maximal inequality and linear comparability, rather than dividing an `L²` continuous-time random variable by a possibly dependent clock factor.
- The support proof works at fixed size with the same variable `Psi⁰(J)Y_n=E[X|T_n]` at every step. Root the current shape at a fixed center and move a farthest leaf of distance at least two to that center; the sum of distances decreases strictly, so the procedure terminates at the star. Deletion leaves a genuine size `n−1≥3` tree in the jump-bound domain. No monotonicity of the martingale value along relocations is required: an endpoint crossing plus a bound on each step suffices. Every unlabelled crossing shape has positive probability by pruning down to a three-vertex path and reversing; no probability of an infinite prescribed history is needed. Conditional Chebyshev then yields positive mass in each open interval; closure adds zero, which is not an atom. Here `mu<1` is needed for the growing path endpoint, and `2mu>1` for the small conditional variance.

## 3. Summability and pathwise localization of hub mass

**Holds under the exact stated a.s. assumption.** No expectation integrability is needed. For each positive integer `K`, freeze `B_r` at the inclusive first crossing of the partial sum of `C_j/[j(j+1)]`. The pre-crossing sum costs at most `K`, and the frozen tail costs exactly `C_tau/tau≤1` if there is a crossing. If there is none, the entire sum is at most `K` directly. At a transition from size `r−1` to `r`, inclusion is the predictable event `tau≥r`; using `tau>r` instead could condition away the promotion causing the crossing. Tracked old degrees have nonpositive conditional drift when normalized, since the physical recipient rate is at most `D_v/(r−1)`. New promotions enter with mass exactly `2/r`; newborns enter as leaves and are not charged at birth. The exact Abel identity requires `n>m`; the `n=m` upper bound is immediate. A useful stronger *pathwise* form is

```math
\sum_{r>m}\frac{B_r-B_{r-1}}r
=\sum_{r>m}\frac{B_r}{r(r+1)}-\frac{B_m}{m+1},
```

since `B_n/n→0` in both stopping branches. Thus the dominating tail is a legitimate integrable random bound for every fixed `K`, even if the original unfrozen sum has infinite expectation. No assumption of integrability of the unfrozen tail enters Fatou. The deterministic implication to sublinearity follows also from `C_m/m≤Σ_{r≥m} C_r/[r(r+1)]` by monotonicity. On the no-stop event and the countable event of all fixed-label degree limits, the tracked normalized core degrees dominate the mass of leaves adjacent to cores with labels `>m`. The latter tends to the nonnegative defect of the first `m` labels. Fatou, bounded convergence for the stopped tail, and union over integer `K` close the proof. The final TV step can use the exact nonnegative decomposition `p_n=q_n+` (core-initiator subprobability of mass `C_n/n`) at `n≥3`; in this convention `||p_n−q_n||_1=C_n/n`. Replacement by `Z` in the recipient-degree law must be followed by dominated convergence against `Z`, not by uniform convergence over all labels.
