## Independent terminal-bound proof

**Mean.** At size at least three, attachment to the root adds a leaf, and a separate promotion loses one leaf. The probabilities are `a/n` and `L/(nD)`, respectively, where `L≤a≤D` and `L/D≤1`. These give supermartingale `D/n` and submartingale `(L−1)/n`; clipping the latter at zero preserves the inequality. Both are bounded, and the lower limiting variable is at most the upper one. Conditional expectation at the terminal tree gives exactly the bounds used in the certificate. In `T₃`, the centre pays at least `1/3`, while an end gains next with probability `1/6`, giving its conditional mean at most `7/24`. The seed symmetry multiplies the difference by `1/2`, proving `1/48`.

**Lower hub bound.** A tagged leaf sends at rate one and can lose its tag only on promotion, at reciprocal root degree. With `j` live tags, the root degree is at least `max(d,j+c)`, where `c=d−L` accounts for permanent initial untagged neighbours. A subset process can be maintained by extra deletions: with `j` selected tags, the aggregate deletion hazard can be made exactly `j/max(d,j+c)` while birth is at rate `j`. This construction uses the subset count, so does not require monotonicity of the actual rate in the true stock. The bounded-state hitting recursion telescopes by successive differences; its increments have ratio at step `j` equal to `1/max(d,j+c)`. On nonextinction the process cannot stay forever in a finite positive band (uniform positive probability of exit in bounded time there). The denominator tail after term 28 is bounded by the geometric sum with ratio `1/max(d,29+c)`. The constant `d` comparison gives the second lower bound `1−d^(−L)`.

To avoid taking any other project's limiting-mass result as input, from a first hit of stock `K≥3` couple a fresh one-black Pólya urn to the actual root gains. While the gap is ≥2 its up probability is at least gap divided by population, and down probability at most inverse population. The stopped power `2^(1−gap)` bounds failure by `2^(2−K)`. A successful urn has positive normalized limit almost surely. Hence survival of the tagged process implies hubhood: intersect its survival event with each stock hit and bound zero-share probability by `2^(2−K)`, then send `K` to infinity. There is no conditioning of urn marginals on the tagged process surviving.

**Upper hub bound.** Until the first arrow from neighbour `x` into the root, charge that success and track `j` of `x`'s tagged other leaves. If `c` is the number of other permanent untagged neighbours, births of tags have rate `j`, tag losses rate at most `j/(j+1+c)`, and the charged arrow rate at most `1/(j+1+c)`. For a decreasing test in `[0,1]` with success value 1, both loss and success increase the drift; thus taking both upper hazards simultaneously is conservative. At `j=0,1`, `min(1,2/(j+1))` is already 1. At `j≥2`, its drift is at most `−2j/((j+1)(j+2))+1/(j+1+c)≤0`; this justifies the upper boundary at 30. The first-step table from the pinned source has the correct orientation of all three hazards. `check_terminal.py` independently solves it by eliminating in the reverse order, checks the physical rate corners, and rechecks the stock bounds with a longer geometric-tail truncation. A hub must gain again; before its first gain the existing neighbour set is fixed, so a clipped union bound is valid. No independence of arrows from different neighbours is assumed.
