- **Upper bound:** An arrow to a core changes only its gain state; an arrow to a leaf must originate at its sole neighbor and both decreases that parent's leaf count and creates a core one level deeper. A far-tail core of degree at least 101 has constant own weight and only a positive offspring term, even at the cutoff. The finite state drifts are affine in the only unknown send rate. The deterministic normalization by `prod(1+lambda/j)` gives the supermartingale starting at the lone core of `T_3`; Markov gives the claimed prefactor without a summable bound on *all* n. The exponent is positive at any threshold strictly above `lambda/log(z)`; dyadic summation, nondecreasing core height and a countable limit of thresholds suffice. Leaves and the arbitrary founder root add only bounded offsets. Both reported upper constants are outward bounds.
- **Physical embedding:** The leaf-first uniform partition can be chosen predictably at each ring. Before ring j at an active core the physical core-neighbor count is at most j; only that core's own rings can change it. Removing one designated leaf per self ring, preferentially the selected one, preserves the subset even when an unrecorded real promotion occurs. At a recorded promotion the new core was a leaf with exactly one neighbor and gains one fresh leaf at the same event. Its own clock can previously have been used in the parent's pool; this is harmless by strong Markov at the promotion, not by independence of entire clock trajectories. Its new pool is disjoint from every live pool, and an ignored jump cannot promote a designated leaf or alter a projected rate unless it is one of the prescribed transitions. Full-filtration conditional rates therefore identify the same law as independent local chains with independent copies at recorded births. Treat death and birth on the same ring as one transition. Nonexplosion follows from the true Yule embedding (and for the abstract chains from rate-at-most-one births per live individual).
- **Biggins:** i.i.d. reproduction *point processes*, not independent siblings; offspring bounded by 50 and mean >1; displacements are real, negative birth ages, with finite expected exponential weight at all positive tilts. Ages are bounded by the **elapsed** 50th self ring, not absolute global time. Use the on-survival theorem. If using the no-extinction formulation of the review theorem, retain only infinite-descendant vertices, condition on root survival, and apply it to their tree: the offspring clusters there are i.i.d. by the branching property and the intensity is unchanged (thinning by survival probability rho, then dividing by rho). The exponential first-moment bound gives the matching upper bound on the full maximum. Positive tilts yield the speed `-beta_*`; negative tilts are harmless also at `beta_*` (smaller than `beta_hi` so they give a larger transform product). The reflected-cap path gives divergence at and below tilt -1. The first-ring contribution `1/(2(theta+2))` gives ratio tending to zero at infinity, while supercriticality gives minus infinity at zero; thus the strict comparison uses a finite maximizer, not merely pointwise positivity of h.
- **Diameter and a.s. bootstrap:** The pinned binomial bound is the unconditional mixture over generation size, with zero on size zero; it does not incorrectly condition on survival. Since `P(N=0)>=1/4`, repeated returns to a bounded positive generation size have a uniform chance of extinction; hence sizes tend to infinity on survival. Dominated convergence rules out the unique infinite line. Intersect speed events over countably many possible roots first, then select the pair; descendants are in distinct physical arms of their LCA, so both depth contributions count, even at a common arbitrary sufficiently large time. A failed trial has finite progeny and the last active chain stopping is observable at finite time. The next physical promotion has conditional hazard at least one (one lone core or two end cores), hence occurs a.s. The restarted law gives conditional success probability rho, which suffices for a geometric bound on consecutive failures without requiring independent start times. The positive Yule limit gives `tau_n/log(n)->1` for transfer of both lower bounds.
