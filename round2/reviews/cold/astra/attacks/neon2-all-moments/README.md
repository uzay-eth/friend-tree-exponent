## Probability comparison and the power basis

At size `N` the rate-`N` hold depends only on size and is independent of the two arrow marks. The complete holding sequence can be generated independently of the embedded tree, giving `W_N∼Gamma(N,1)` independent of `Z_v` from a deterministic-size completion. Scaled ordinary degree powers are uniformly integrable by domination with size and any larger integer Yule moment. The resulting amplitude `M̃_p` has denominator `Γ(p+1)` and a closed discounted Doob martingale. At a later stop the required identity is a conditional physical restart, not a size-amplitude factorization under future selection. The normalized signed two-birth pairing uses the same common size `n+2` for every completion. The opposite adjacent-target orders have difference `r_y/[D_y(D_y+1)]−r_x/[D_x(D_x+1)]`; nonadjacent orders cancel, twins cancel, and the serial path coefficient is `r_x/(D_x+1)`. Dropping the two nonnegative mixed terms gives the stated endpoint split, and its serial weights sum to at least one.

For the global lower use zero at stocks 0 and 1, and `q_p(L−1)` at `L≥2`. The worst birth/loss drift excess divided by this value is `p[1/(L−1)−1/(L+p−2)]≥0`, including `L=2`; the two zero states have nonnegative drift. For the upper use the positive affine size `a=(D+L)/2`: gain rate is at most `a`, a gain increases it by one, and a promotion decreases it by one half. Gamma monotonicity and the unit-gain ratio give discounted upper drift at most zero. Bounds of polynomial order in `D` justify localization and the lower limit in expectation. Both stock and affine size differ from degree by at most the core-neighbour count, which grows at most by the focal vertex's own number of rings; this is `O(t)` almost surely. Hence both power tests have the claimed physical amplitude limit.

For the `1/3` lower, reserve the focal clock separately and assign independent genealogical clocks to the initially selected leaf and its descendants. Until the focal vertex's first ring `σ`, no tracked leaf can promote, and the selected family agrees literally with the virtual Yule family. The latter can continue with its own clocks and is independent of `σ`; outside events only add untracked leaves. The first focal ring spends at most one leaf. With `q=e^(−t)`, the geometric law of `Y(t)` and the rising-factorial series give `E[q_p(Y(t)−2)1_{Y(t)≥3}]=(1−q)^2 q^(−p)`. Discount cancels the last factor, and `e^(−σ)` is uniform, giving `1/3`. No independence of the physical tree at the ring is needed. For a leaf on degree `d`, promotion hazard is at most `1/d` until promotion. The Laplace bound `E[e^(−pτ);τ<∞]≤1/(1+pd)` follows by integrating survival, including possible mass at infinity; the real promotion state is `(2,1)`. Gamma log-convexity at the midpoint then gives the strict claimed square-root upper.

## Nineteen intervals and executable scope

The basis `q_p(D)=∏_(i=1)^(D−1)(1+p/i)` is exact at every real `p` here. Dividing the discounted drift by `q_p(D_a)` removes every nonlinear power term from the interior: only focal gains multiply by `1+p/D_a`. The remaining drift is multiaffine in the displayed rates and `p`, so checking all corners works for every admissible physical rate vector, even when rates are correlated. On a degree-cap face the lower Gamma ratio is the inverse product starting at `L−1`, minimized at the upper exponent; the upper first rounds its affine seed **up** to an integer, then maximizes the resulting inverse product at the lower exponent. In the coupled cutoffs the ratio `q_p(D_b)/q_p(D_a)` is bounded at the correct endpoint depending on degree order. Since the subtracted `beta` and `gamma` values are nonnegative, the upper ratio gives a safe lower difference boundary.

The comparison order is `alpha/beta → gamma`, then `vx` or `psi2 → eta1 → eta1_x` as applicable. Before a marked leaf promotes, its parent gains at rate at least one; the nonincreasing `gamma` makes one the worst upper-drift rate. With two adjacent cores a single birth cannot gain at both or promote both sets of leaves. The reciprocal of the tracked core neighbor remains present in the appropriate gain interval. With a distinguished leaf, its promotion at `1/D_a` lands at `(D_a,L_a−1;2,1)` in `psi2`, distinct from the ordinary other-leaf loss rate `(L_a−1)/D_a`. The outer parent-degree cap in `eta1_x` uses the `eta1` handoff; the focal degree cap uses the separate `alpha−gamma` lower.
