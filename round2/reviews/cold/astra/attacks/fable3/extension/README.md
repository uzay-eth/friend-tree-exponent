## Classifiers

1. **Leaf lower.** Suppress the current parent clock until an independent rate-one exponential time. The tag stays a leaf, and the degree of the suppressed parent begins at `d` and has gain rate at most its degree. Thus its stopped mean is at most `d e^t`, and integrating Jensen's lower bound on the reciprocal against the first-ring time gives `1/(2d)`. A successful selection produces a core with `(k,c)=(1,1)`; the strong Markov property and baseline escape give the stated `1/[2d(e−1)]`. Both implementations use the certified rational lower surrogate `B(1,1)/(2d)`.
2. **Core lower.** At fixed `(k,c)` until the next relevant change, `β≥k` and promotion hazard `k/(k+c)` imply that the integrated conditional probability of a birth is at least `d/(d+1)` (`d=k+c`), even if exterior histories change `β`. For `k≥1` the jump occurs almost surely. The successor payoffs at birth and promotion are distinct: `(k+1,c)` and `(k−1,c+1)`. Minimize the two-payoff average over the entire probability interval and then maximize with the independently valid baseline. The degree-24 boundary uses baseline except the internal all-leaf star seam where the code deliberately uses 0. The dependency order is decreasing total degree, then increasing `k`; each recursion is finite. This is a hazard comparison, not a Markov assumption on `(k,c)`.
3. **Leaf upper.** Keep the marked leaf in `k`; it has its own success rate `1/(k+c)`. A promotion of an unmarked leaf moves to `(k−1,c+1)`. The rate-`k` Bellman inequality and monotonicity in `k` are checked **after** taking the minimum with baseline. They imply a supersolution for the actual birth rate `β≥k`, stopped at success or the degree-24 baseline boundary. Tone of the source comment aside, the minimum alone is not used as evidence for the particular post-minimum row checks.
4. **Bare-core upper.** Before its first gain, all candidate senders of the marked core are its current neighbours. For a given neighbour, fix its starting core degree `c≥1`. It may increase along the physical history, so the live total degree satisfies `D≥k+c`, with the marked core occupying one slot. The nonnegative combined loss/success term `k(H(k−1)−H(k))+1−H(k)` divided by `D` is bounded above by the frozen-`c` term, while monotonicity of `H` pays for extra leaf births. At `k=0` use 1; at `k=41` use `1/40`, justified by the baseline supersolution `1/max(1,k−1)`. At `k=3`, dropping the marked slot would make its drift check read `4≤3`, so the slot is essential. Take the minimum with the old per-neighbour bound, then the union bound, clipped at 1. The edge lower bound uses the complement of a valid current neighbour upper after these checks.

The independent Fraction implementation solves the 49 `F/H` tridiagonal tables by reverse elimination (opposite to both submissions), checks each original equation and ranges, and tests all 276 improved leaf rows as well as the core lower recursion and boundary cases. ## Independent algorithms and fixtures

- **Multiplicity/orbit DP (`ext-dp.cpp`).** A one- or two-centre key (the latter with a virtual root) specifies the free tree. Equal branches can be permuted; folding them position by position after canonical ordering gives exactly the automorphism classes. Integer rate to an attachment target is `J_n Σ_{x~w}1/D_x`, where `J_n=lcm(1,…,n−1)` and the integer rates sum to `n J_n`. Grouping attachment sites on a free-tree orbit is legitimate only for the unmarked transfer. After a marked distribution is present the code instead visits all target vertices for each mark representative, then reclassifies that representative in the new tree. The missing factor from naïvely visiting only one target of each orbit is detected in the oracle. Summing a single label's masses over all mark orbits gives the free-tree mass used to initialize the newborn orbit; one must not multiply by the mark-orbit size again. Terminal sharding is by free-tree key, so the signed subtraction for each rooted shape stays together. Rounding in the cached comparison rank is only a fast path; ties are decided as exact rationals. Relevant allocation/carry and mass assertions are active.
- **Signed rooted DP (`lead-signed_orbit_dp.cpp`).** Old/new laws are subtracted on identical rooted keys before selecting the classifier endpoints. Reroot the newborn law at its new leaf and propagate the preceding label retaining its root. Linear transition then preserves the exact signed difference for later sizes. Signs may have cancelled at any intermediate vertex, but this does not lose future mass because the transition depends only on the current rooted shape. Terminal splitting by hash of the rooted key never separates like terms; sharded use excludes extra propagation. At size 23 the normalizer has 365 bits, versus a 383-bit signed magnitude limit. Partial mass per rooted key or bucket is bounded in absolute value by the normalizer because the positive and negative input masses separately do not exceed it and the transition multiplies the normalizer by the full row sum. The centred carrier may use an extra virtual vertex; the pinned runtime range through 23 leaves space for it and both 64-bit canonical words fit.
- **Directed grid (`ext-grid.cpp`).** Before the terminal subtraction floors act only on nonnegative masses. A newborn assigned from a sum of previously floored orbit masses is still below the true newborn mass. With the two marginals missing respectively `E_old,E_new` on scale `2^62`, the signed interval is safely widened by `−E_new/2^62` and `+E_old/2^62`. Terms of the signed accumulation remain bounded by the one-marginal totals. The wide multiply precedes division and the classifier reductions remain rational. This is the same multiplicity/orbit design in a second engine, not a third independent physical encoding. A further claim about the individual `p_24` is outside this board audit.

The unmerged physical parent-history oracle checks every adjacent pair under both classifier settings through size 8. It includes controls that lose multiplicity and that classify the two laws before subtracting; both change the expected mass or bound. Both pinned exact engines ran with UBSan through size 11 for both modes, covering older labels propagated past the moment of birth. The signed engine's four-way terminal split and the orbit engine's three separate terminal shards recombine to the same exact endpoints; the latter's shard masses sum to the exact denominator.
