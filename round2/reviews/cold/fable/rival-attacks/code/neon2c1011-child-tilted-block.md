# Supplementary lower-bound proof for Claim 11 (independent of Biggins)

Let ν be the finite intensity measure of one embedded CMJ individual's offspring ages, with Laplace transform φ. Write β=5067/5000 and h(θ)=log φ(θ)+βθ. The claimant's exact derivative bracket gives θ₀∈[θ₁,θ₂] with h′(θ₀)=0. Under the probability law dνθ(t)=e^(−θt)dν(t)/φ(θ), the mean m(θ)=−φ′(θ)/φ(θ) satisfies m′(θ)=−Varνθ(t)<0; strictness follows because even the first-ring-before-first-leaf-ring emission gives a nondegenerate absolutely continuous component. At θ₀, m(θ₀)=β and log φ(θ₀)+θ₀m(θ₀)=h(θ₀)>0. By continuity increase θ slightly, and take δ>0 with m(θ)+δ<β and log φ(θ)+θ[m(θ)−δ]>0.

The expected age counting measure in generation k is ν^{*k}, by independence between reproduction processes. Tilting this convolution and applying the LLN to k independent νθ ages shows

    ν^{*k}([0,kβ]) ≥ exp(k[log φ(θ) + θ(m(θ)−δ)]) × (1−o(1)).

Indeed restrict the tilted sum to the interval k[m(θ)−δ,m(θ)+δ]; its probability tends to one and the exponential tilt-back factor is bounded below there. Thus for a sufficiently large fixed k, the process that keeps just generation-k descendants born within kβ of each kept vertex is a supercritical Galton–Watson process, with offspring bounded by 50^k. Siblings can have dependent ages; only reproduction across individuals and unexplored block subtrees uses independence. The retained genealogical steps have exact physical graph distance k, and their interbirth times are bounded by kβ. On survival one gets height growth at least 1/β for all large times. The usual binomial count of surviving subtrees gives two distinct surviving same-generation block roots almost surely on survival (if extinction probability were zero, the same conclusion follows because the population tends to infinity when the offspring law has mean >1). Applying the same depth estimate in each branch and adding the two path lengths gives diameter growth at least 2/β.

At each fresh full CMJ trial this block event has a fixed positive probability. It suffices to restart at extinction of the full CMJ, without detecting block extinction: on full CMJ survival there are Z_g→∞ individuals in its generations, so the probability of full survival with no successful block subtree anywhere is at most P(1≤Z_g<K)+(1−q_block)^K for all g,K and vanishes by letting g→∞ then K→∞. Hence the original extinction-restart argument applies unchanged. Finally τ_n/log n→1 converts both bounds. This is a supplementary proof of the claimed lower endpoints; it does not certify the exact BRW speed.
