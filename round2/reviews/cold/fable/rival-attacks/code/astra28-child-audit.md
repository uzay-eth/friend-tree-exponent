## Stationary bridge (all floors)

The actual oriented law `pi` is the normalized mean-rooted limit, not a stationary law of an invented finite Markov chain in `(D,c)`. For a bounded local reward, `exp(-mu*t) E[sum_core reward] -> c_E pi(reward)`. One may use almost-sure local total variation and uniform integrability from the `L^2` bound for the normalized core count. FY's unbounded donor degree is not an unbounded score: at most `17*16` terms per host, with finitely many affine splines uniformly Lipschitz in inverse donor degree; replacing `1/D` by zero only beyond M changes each term by <=Lip/M. Keeping the absorption predicate `D>=12` directly keeps a fixed-radius bounded local score. Matched prefix roles or terminal rows rooted within fixed distance need no moment of ordinary degree. Removing seed exceptions costs bounded score per ignored founder or is covered by the oriented-limit construction / core-arity uniform integrability.

For any sum of bounded discounted prefix values, an actual promotion retains all existing terms at successors and seeds the matching higher prefix once, with a new `D=2,c=1` role. Thus the Bellman checks apply conditional on a real newborn's future physical environment (no independence of siblings assumed). Their summed birth source is precisely the first role value per real promotion, or the marked value for `f`; the other prefix sources are internal. The positive stationary birth normalization is `pi(b)=mu` (differentiate `C` by counting promotions, or use age identity). With physical newborn law `beta`, `pi(f)=mu E_beta int_0^infty exp(-mu*a) f(a) da`. For nonnegative rewards replacing the discount by original Gold2 upper and the multiplier by its lower endpoint is conservative, also if the path is adapted. Alternatively sum the pointwise Bellman inequalities before integration and separately use stationarity. This applies to actual finite masks and ignores only nonnegative contributions at cutoffs.
