## Law and support arguments

### Common: what is stationary and what is signed

The normalization is `E[sum over cores]/E[C(t)]`; the limit is the oriented core law π, not the law of an ordinary vertex or the law of an edge picked proportional to its occurrence in a particular random tree. An edge or motif *intensity per core* can count multiple embeddings. `round2/fringe/spatial-limit.md:29–45,49–55` gives the mean/marked ancestor representation and fixed-size empirical law. Bounded observables with upward ports are read from a fixed-depth marked ancestor fringe. Degree-weighted arity tests in the LP use the exponential-in-arity UI bound in `O/flow/README.md:16–32`: at a physical birth one old arity increases by one and a new arity-zero core enters, giving drift ≤ `(exp(θ)−1)∑ exp(θk)+C(t)`.

`round2/degree-law.md:11–21` proves `Q_t f=S_t f+∫Q_u B S_(t−u)f du` by conditional expectation and compensation of actual core promotions. Apply the physical eigenmeasure, let the old-root contribution die out, and get `π f = μ E_β ∫ exp(−μa) f(X_a) da` with `β=πB/μ`. The orientation is retained, since each promotion makes a permanent parent→child relation. A mark of maternal degree at birth is retained by flagging the newborn in `B` and then tracking the same marked root with `S`; for the parent objective the same conclusion also follows by applying the birth compensator directly to the restricted intensity `b 1_(D_mother≥3)`. Neither the newborn environment nor different donors need be independent. The lifetime projections start at actual promotion to `(2,0)`; caps apply to current nondecreasing coordinates and one can stop once no qualifying occurrence can follow. Incomplete tagged paths can branch at births; the total number of live registrations per apex is bounded by the fixed arity caps. The discounted subsolution inequality works directly with these intensities, and bounded projected/aggregate continuation gives a vanishing terminal term. No claim that a projected census is Markov is needed. Nonnegative objectives admit the upper discount in lower occupation and the lower multiplier; signed diagnostic objectives need both signs/corners and are not budget entries.
