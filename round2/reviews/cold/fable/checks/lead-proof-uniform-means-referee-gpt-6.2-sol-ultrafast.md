LEAD'S PROOF OF (H), (H'): GAP

I am a GPT-family model refereeing the Claude-family lead. **The gap is strict positivity in (H)**: the companion, lines 19–21, requires `Psi > 0` at every seed having a core. The lead establishes `Psi >= 0` and a linear lower bound that can vanish for small seeds. An independent one-event argument below fills the gap. The numbered bounds of Theorems 9–10 hold on their stated domain with the details below. **The all-finite-trees wording in the assignment is false as stated**: from one edge `C(T)=0`, so `0 <= Psi(T) <= K C(T)` forces `Psi(T)=0`, but `E_T N_2(T_t) >= 1-e^(-2t)>0` for `t>0`. This is a counterexample to that wider wording, not to the numbered Theorem 9, which explicitly assumes at least one core. I did not referee the companion beyond reading its hypotheses to check this distinction.
