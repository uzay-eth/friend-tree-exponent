### Objects and physical transfer

Let `S` be a finite unmarked rooted fringe with a core root. Real vertices include all leaves. The root has a parent slot counted in degree `D` and core degree `c`, but the slot does not ring and an arrow to it does nothing. Every other arrow has rate `1/D_sender`. In intrinsic evolution `L0`, an incoming arrow adds one leaf to its target; when the target was a leaf it becomes a core with planted shape `J`, without changing the degree of its old parent. Thus `L0` has total jump rate `n−1/D` and each jump increments `n` by one. `b=(D−c)/D`, `p=1/D`, and `r=D−c+Σ_Y 1/D_Y`, summing over old immediate core children `Y`. The founder is special: no extra slot, no particle, and a separate immigration row.

An active `(a,S)` has a frozen **external** death rate `a`, even as `S` grows. In physical evaluation of this type at an old core, `a=q(D_parent)` at the instant the evaluation is read. This distinction is essential. A parent's active row emits `Y` and `Y+` at respective rates `q(D)−p` and `p`, each with mark `q(D)` frozen at emission. `Y+` is a copy with a leaf on its root, not the old physical child. At rate `r` per old `Y` it emits the dormant pair `(q(D+1),q(D);Y)`. At rate `p` per old grandchild `Z` of `Y` it emits `(q(D_Y+1),q(D_Y);Z)`. These are copies of *unchanged* subtrees and do not replace the emitter. In a raw dormant `(l,u;S)` both marks stay fixed as `S` evolves intrinsically; killing has rate `l`, consuming conversion to active `(l,S)` has rate `u−l`. This gives diagonal loss `u`, not `l`. `P_c=R_c+B` adds a separate `J_{q(D)}` birth at rate `b` to `R_c`; `B=B0 J_restrict` with `B0g=b g(q(D))` on actives, zero on dormants. `R_N` further kills a lineage on its own first intrinsic size `N+1` transition, retaining its already emitted relatives and the full intrinsic holding-rate subtraction. The dormant divided-difference similarity multiplies incoming dormant emission rates by `u−l`, divides dormant scores by `u−l`, and replaces conversion in the mean row by unit active production. As a positive branching law that means **retained** dormant with independent death at `u`. Uncapped `q=p` eliminates the ordinary unchanged *active* copy; a coarsening keeps that copy where `q>p` but never changes any physical send rate.

Write `T` for the sum of active responses at all planted core subtrees with current parent labels (zero at the silent top); for the old permanent model the subtree labels are inherited except for that top. The physical identity uses active test scores with dormant score **equal to their difference**, or uses `P_c(t)f` where a mark-independent active terminal `f` is initialized to zero on dormant. Arbitrary terminal data with a mark dependence and zero on dormant do not have that property. For a physical arrow parent→core child, only the target's standalone shape misses the gain, and only the target's outgoing edges change labels. The target's own label is unchanged. Charge the missing target shape increment and the shifts of its outgoing labels to the sender. A gain from a source inside the target's fringe is intrinsic in the target's fringe; its outgoing label shifts are then charged at that target's rate `r`. Ancestor shape updates are already in their intrinsic generator. The sum of `q(D) f_d(Y)` in the ordinary copies cancels each artificial child death, including founder-funded terms. Promotions generate exactly one new evaluation and do not drop the promoter's mark to its old children. This proves the aggregate generator identity on unrestricted finite configurations, also for a founder or an included planted top at zero.

### Exact small-size checks and negative controls

`check.py` builds canonical unordered shapes by adding a real leaf at the target of every directed arrow. The number of shapes at sizes `2…10` is `[1, 2, 4, 9, 20, 48, 115, 286, 719]`. The physical row comes from applying the old-to-new subtree sum to the actual arrow, and is compared as a coefficient vector (all active test scores at once), after expanding the dormant differences. Branching rows are built separately from the five emission channels. For each `N` the finite state family includes every shape up to `N` and every obtainable exterior label including zero, plus all adjacent nonzero drops from degrees `2…N`. Tests include the exact full residual drift on those states (with exact `N+1` successors), its killed inequality, the powered estimate at `ε=1`, and the dormant drift identity with intrinsic drift separated. Founder checks use slot-free arrows on roots with at least two children. A finite `N` here is not intended to enumerate the infinitely many possible input marks: the exact edge proof covers the full interval `0≤a≤1/2`.

At discount 8, `check.py` independently solves the killed residual system by sparse exact elimination. For each direct column and the active-unit column it verifies the common-forcing intrinsic Green equation, intrinsic resolvent commutation, raw dormant differences, and scaled equations with exact substitutions. It constructs the full resolvent from the newborn matrix inverse and substitutes every equation, also checking `I+F=(I−K)^−1`, `(I−K)j=J V`, full versus residual occupation reconstruction, and the `N−1` to `N` intrinsic exit-forcing identity for the unit reward. These are finite tests of algebra, not approximate near-critical solves. I intentionally do **not** compare a physically *globally* killed tree to a lineage-killed branching system: even at `J,N=2` their active-unit derivatives are `−3/2` and `−1`, respectively, since lineages may keep newborn copies emitted at the boundary.
