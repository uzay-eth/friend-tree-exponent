## Scope and gaps

- The gold2 verdict is for the authenticated `b435b4c` payloads under the stated model. The physical sweep is an adversarial check, not a proof over all trees; the all-degree certificate supplies that step. Two requested snapshot files (`T4` and `T6`) were absent.
- The Astra verdicts are for the two released fixed trials derived from gold1, with the cited mean stable-age, oriented neighborhood and exponent identification results as inputs. A scalar almost-sure exponent alone would not establish the stationary repricing. No optimization or claim of optimality beyond the stored trials is needed.
- The old FY stdout is not byte-identical to its historical receipt, although every exact component agrees. The signed LP can pass on an overstated objective; column feasibility alone cannot certify physical ownership. Check receipts and the physical degree and ordering restrictions when changing the objective.
- The gold2 perturbations test loss of the published endpoint; they are not a proof that arbitrary binary corruption is caught by the cap logic. Payload authentication and the private-buffer controls are separate checks.
