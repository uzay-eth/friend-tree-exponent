## Fable comparison, gaps, and scope

Fable's nonasymptotic bounds imply `EX/[32(e−1)]≤c≤2EX`, or `.01315≤c≤1.44618` at the supplied `EX`. Equivalently `1/[32(e−1)]≤π(F)≤2`. The independent numerical `π(F)≈.82–.84` is safely inside. Combining valid Astra and Fable bounds strengthens the lower endpoint to `μ EX/2≈.2045` and the upper endpoint remains `2EX`. These are theoretical constant multipliers of `EX`; the numerical `EX` itself is not a certified value. This is a refinement of **the same weighted placement and promotion mechanism**: Fable's Jensen calculation uses the first parent ring to get a *lower bound* `1/(2d)` on promotion and then a uniform survival lower bound. Astra integrates the actual all-time promotion hazard in the evolving parent, with the tag's own rings preserved, and keeps the actual hub endpoint as a function of the full local environment. It then averages that bounded continuous flux under the uniform-core limit. Neither proof says that the typical parental promotion probability is asymptotic to `1/(2d)`.

**Gaps list for (A)–(C): none found** under the earlier inputs. The only wrong coefficient I found was in the Fable comparison prose, outside the bounds and outside Astra's proofs. Astra's prose `1+o(1)` on the marked side can be justified as above; the proof only needs the fixed `1+η` inequality. The positivity and rate statements are genuinely one-sided in scope: no effective threshold label `N(K)`, quantitative convergence or error term for the equivalents, numerical certification of `π(F)`, global order across all missing intermediate labels, order at fixed positive `x`, or order for arbitrary growing `y=nx`. In particular the compact log limit is about individual tails, and differences between two nearly equal logarithms are not controlled. Conditional birth-tree reversals at the hub atom are not ruled out by unconditional eventual ordering.
