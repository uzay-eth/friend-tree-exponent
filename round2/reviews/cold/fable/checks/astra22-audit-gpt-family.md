### Core-only simulation, mass proxy and errors

`sim.cpp` draws the initiator uniformly. Each core has weight one; the aggregate of its leaves has weight equal to their count and uses a Fenwick tree. A core initiator chooses uniformly from its core-neighbour and leaf slots. Early leaf identities are only stored while needed for a label (through label 262144); anonymous slots cannot change the transition probabilities. Promotion deletes a leaf slot and yields a new core with one new leaf. The path at size 3 has a fair choice of seed center. The snapshots check leaf count, core-edge count and total degree. Every vertex with degree ≥8 is represented in the output whether or not its label is known; the scored x/r rectangles have cutoffs strictly above this degree, and the script verifies that no unknown label passes any scored cutoff. Simulation uses discrete jumps, which is exact because all individual ring rates are one; no holding times are required for these checks.

For this core the remaining normalized degree martingale has variance at most `d` and the absolute expected integrated drift is at most `c+1` (at most rate-one additional core-neighbour increments). ## Fable comparison: zero threshold and early labels

Write `H_n=# {v≤n:Z_v>0}`. At `x=1/n`, `a=0`, `b=1`, the iterated limit (first `n→∞`, then `r↓0`) of the normalized count is `X π(F)/μ`. Tonelli / monotone convergence applies to the deterministic integral against the finite mark measure. This gives an almost-sure **liminf** for `n^(-μ)H_n`. It does **not** on its own give the almost-sure upper bound: “simultaneously for all `r>0`” cannot justify exchanging a supremum over `r` with the `n` limit. This is a **GAP in the proposed zero-threshold inference**, not a gap in Claim 22 as stated. The omitted band consists of `0<Z_v≤r/n`, not the discarded labels with `xv` outside a compact range at a fixed positive cutoff.

There is an **L¹ completion** using Claim 18, independent of any uniform-in-`r` assertion. The equivalent `p_v∼c v^(μ−1)` yields `n^(−μ) E H_n→c/μ` by summation. If `A_n(r)` is the positive-threshold count in the first `n` labels, the nonnegative residual satisfies `n^(−μ)E[H_n−A_n(r)] → E[X]∫_0^1 y^(μ−1)[π(F)−ν((ry,∞))]dy`, which tends to zero. Triangle inequality with the L¹ rectangle limit gives `n^(−μ)H_n→X π(F)/μ` in L¹. No summable rate for this approximation is provided by the displayed claim.

## Verdict and limitations

**HOLDS**, conditional on the cited inputs. No gap found for fixed positive `r`, including `a=0` and `b=∞`. The most vulnerable technical points were the common-future forecast, the marked dirty-core perturbation, and the clock normalization. All survived; centering separately at birth fails even on the smallest fixtures. The endpoint proof is based on a matching independent total count, not an unproved uniform extension of the compact-window LLN. The paper's rate for the *centered finite-window error* is not a rate for the marked limit.
