### What the shared notation does and does not identify

- **Long versus Astra 2/5/13.** Long’s $`\mathcal K_s f(x)=\mathbb E_x\sum_{v:\mathrm{direct\ child}}e^{-s\sigma_v}f(X_v)`$ retags the **whole** growing tree, keeping the exterior, all real vertices and stubs. $`A_m^\infty=(\mathcal K_s^m\mathbf1)(x_\infty)`$; its scalar pressure is $`r_\infty`$, while $`r(\mathcal K_s)`$ is the full operator radius (separately characterized in C). Astra 2’s compact positive $`K_s`$ acts on $`C(\{1/2,1/3, \ldots\}\cup\{0\})`$ using a residual **marked fringe** and permanent labels from birth; Astra 5 groups capped labels in a 19-dimensional renewal equation, keeping actual reciprocal sends. Astra 13 instead uses a current-parent gauge and dormant copies. Same physical critical parameter; no equality of kernels, pressure functions off criticality, or genealogical environments is established. C’s nilpotent killed truncations and essential radius one are fully compatible with A5’s nonzero determinant root and A2’s compactness.
- **Long versus Astra 14.** The isolated unmarked planted dynamics with a counted silent parent slot is shared, so Astra’s $`J`$ is Long’s inert newborn and $`M_s(J)=V_s^\infty`$ for $`s>\mu`$. A14 builds $`H_s=(I-\Delta V_s C)^{-1}\Delta V_sb`$, $`U_s=V_s(b+CH_s)`$ with $`V_s`$ acting on bounded scores and $`Cf=D_o^{-1}\sum_Y f(Y)`$; its physical identity is $`sM_s(J)(1-U_s(J))=1`$ **above** $`\mu`$, with a separate sign proof across it. Long’s $`B_s(z)=1-1/F_s(z)`$ uses a rank-one subtraction on the whole-environment birth kernel, $`D=\mathcal K_s-(\mathcal K_s\mathbf1)\otimes\ell_{x_\infty}`$, and a generation variable $`z`$; it does not bound $`\|\Delta V_s C\|`$ or provide A14’s signed evaluator. There is an exact shared scalar consequence: by Tonelli $`F_s(1)=sV_s^\infty`$, so $`B_s(1)=U_s(J)`$ for real $`s>\mu`$ when $`B_s`$ is continued along the positive real axis. This equality of values does not identify the two feedback operators, their domains of convergence, or an off-critical sign scheme. Sources: A p. 12 (22–24), C p. 48 (58–59), Long `docs/signed_renewal_scope.md`, and Astra `influence/zero/critical.md` (1)–(2).
- **Leaf insertion.** A p. 34 (77) bounds the absolute increment of the unnormalized discounted **total core count**, uniformly over old retained states relative to a newborn supremum $`W_T(s)`$. C pp. 44–45 (53–54) localizes the effect along a core path. A10 supplies the limiting spatial cell score used by Astra’s separate `influence/gradient/README.md` (§§1–4, first file A14): its explicit uniform one-leaf bound is for $`\Delta\Psi^0`$, where $`\Psi^0=\lim_{t\to\infty}e^{-\mu t}\mathbb E^0 C_t`$; it uses parity infections on ordinary vertices and critical cell rewards. Long’s exploration uses aggregate core channels and discounted count rewards. A14’s additional off-critical contraction is for the bounded child-occupation operator $`\Delta V_s C`$ in total variation; its constant is <1, whereas $`40W_T(s)/s`$ is not a TV contraction. A fact 14 gives a first-hit bound with multiplier 0.605 at 0.565; this is closely analogous to C’s directional step but applies to a different coupled first-hit variable at a different discount. Astra then bounds the intrinsic root leaf-fraction integral, not C’s relative tagged total-core reward error.
