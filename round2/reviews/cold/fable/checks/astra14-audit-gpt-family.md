## Objects and conventions

* A planted fringe has an ordinary nonleaf root, all its ordinary descendants, and one inert exterior parent slot. Its real leaves and real cores count toward its size, the slot does not. Every core has degree `D=1+L+#core children`; the 1 is the true parent for strict descendants and the slot at the top. The slot counts as a core neighbour in `c=D−L`. Each ordinary vertex has a rate-one source clock; a source chooses each neighbour at its actual `1/D`. The top-to-slot choice does nothing, and the slot never sends. A leaf clock sends to its parent. An arrow targeting a core adds a leaf there; one targeting a leaf promotes it, keeping the tree and attaching a leaf at the promoted vertex. `L⁰` changes one whole fringe in this way, with total intrinsic rate `n−1/D_o`.
* The signed states forget all marks. `E` sums scores over the old immediate core children; `T` sums over all core descendant fringes, with their slots, and includes the top. `Δ` inserts a leaf at the top before taking the difference, and `C` is `E` multiplied by the **actual** `p=1/D_o`. `b=L_o/D_o`. The signed operator `L⁰+CΔ` is not a Markov generator. Its `+p` and `−p` copies have no particle interpretation. No extra killing or copies occur in `V_z` itself. Its derivative resolvent is a bounded-operator construction on all finite unmarked shapes, not a finite-size limit assumed a priori.
* In the positive comparison, a newly promoted core receives the **frozen** mark `1/min(D_promoter,20)` (or `1/D_promoter` in the older uncapped construction). This affects representation but never an intrinsic arrow rate. The current `p` of its parent is no larger than this mark. The residual `R` runs the intrinsic whole fringe, kills the original particle at its current fringe root's frozen mark, and retains it while independently emitting a *whole copy* of `Y⁺` at rate `p` and a *whole copy* of `Y` at rate `a_Y−p` for each core child. Copies carry all inherited marks. `B₀` adds a marked virgin at rate `b`, also leaving the source in place, in addition to the actual intrinsic promotion. `B_nt` omits only the degree-two direct production: it does not suppress the actual intrinsic promotion or any old-child copies. The trigger is `h ev_j`, where `j=J_(1/2)`. The auxiliary zero-mark positive root has no root killing, but *all* its strict descendants and direct newborns still carry the nineteen original permanent labels. This is not the signed zero gauge applied to every vertex.
* In a size-killed Green solve the intrinsic transition to `N+1` pays zero for that particle, but its full diagonal rate remains. Smaller independent copies (including same-size `Y⁺` when the parent is bare with one child) are not killed. Killing the entire physical tree at `N+1` has a different boundary, quantified below. The cap-48 TV certificate has **positive continuation** at the degree exit and no kill in the theorem.
