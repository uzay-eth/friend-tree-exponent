## Comparison with Fable and Neon-2

- **Fable claim 5 / `COUPLING.md` §5:** for fixed `α>0`, `E Beta(1/2,v−1/2)^α = Γ(α+1/2)Γ(v)/(Γ(1/2)Γ(v+α)) ∼ [Γ(α+1/2)/Γ(1/2)] v^{-α}`. Astra's moment is smaller by the rare-event factor `v^{μ−1}→0`; the two estimates agree. The Fable tail is `ε↓0` at **fixed label** with label-dependent constants, not the birth-scale mass `vZ_v` regime. The lower-bound history puts `v` on a leaf, then sends all subsequent births through size `m` to `v`, so at size `j` its recipient rate is `j−v+1/2`; the product of the normalized rates is of order `m^{-(v−1/2)}`. The expected remaining deficit ≤`(v+1)/m` gives the lower endpoint-tail bound by Markov with `m` of order `1/ε`. Neither this endpoint law nor the stochastic cap by a continuous beta variable would alone prohibit atoms at interior positive values. They are consistent with the independent Gamma-split absolute continuity argument. The positive atom statement allows an atom **at zero**. Fable's finite-label stochastic order and late near-one order are separate from Astra's eventual moment comparisons.
- **Neon-2 claim 5:** its a.s. total `M_n∼ AX K_n log N` and uniform logarithmic-volume CDF are consistent with `B_R/K_n→X L_R(1)` in probability. The relative frequency of a fixed shape is asymptotic in probability to `L_R(1)/(A log N)` (pair `2/(3 log N)`), hence any finite shape collection has asymptotically zero mass in the macroscopic census. At each fixed volume, a `1/log N` term is below the precision of its uniform CDF or TV estimates. Its a.s. control at the larger scale cannot upgrade the fine-scale count to a.s.; the slow diagonal loses all pair occurrences along random indices without affecting the macroscopic scale. Ordinary per-shape convergence does not give a tight limiting distribution on all shapes or locate a TV phase boundary.

## Gaps and limits of the verdict

No unfilled gap found at the stated scope. **I(a)** is per deterministic schedule, with an explicit extremely small sufficient a.s. range and a counterexample to a universal a.s. extension; no maximal a.s. range, path-adaptive schedules or rates near the proportional boundary are proved. **I(b)** is for each fixed deterministic threshold and a finite shape list, with a stable kernel and eventual finite core-singleton ledger. It does not give eventual equality simultaneously at thresholds selected from the realized masses, finite-time conditional independence, or a total-variation claim conditioned on the full history. **II** proves fixed-power late-label asymptotics, not a uniform-in-`α` estimate, a crossover label, or global stochastic order. Finite experiments test identities and plausibility; the limits use the analytic arguments above.
