# Claim 7 — Astra's proof map

Locations below are in `ASTRA_REPO/round2/hub-mass.md`. This claim has two routes to the same mass conclusion. Neither requires Claims 2, 3, 5 or 6.

## Expectation route and conditional laws

1. **Inputs and one-vertex supermartingale (lines 5, 14–16).** Inputs: Addario-Berry et al. Proposition 3.1 as cited here gives the fixed-label limits $`D_v(n)/n, L_v(n)/n\to Z_v`$ almost surely; `proofs/bracket-motifs.md` Theorem 1 gives $`a_n=\mathbb E C_n\le K n^\lambda`$ for some $`K<\infty`$, with the available $`\lambda=1133/2000<1`$. Quote: “a particular core of degree $`D`$ gains a leaf with probability at most $`D/j`$” (line 16). Therefore $`\mathbb E[D_v(j+1)/(j+1)\mid\mathcal F_j]\le D_v(j)/j`$ after its promotion; at promotion size $`r`$ it starts at $`2/r`$. **Elementary.**

2. **Tail mass in expectation (lines 16–24, (2)).** Inputs: step 1, at most one promotion per step, and conditional promotion probability $`j^{-1}\sum_{x\ {\rm core}}L_x/D_x\le C_j/j`$. All late-labelled cores present at $`n`$ were promoted at $`r>m`$; bounding by all promotions there and conditioning just after each promotion gives the leading sum in the quote:
   $`\displaystyle \mathbb E\sum_{v>m, \ D_v(n)\ge2} D_v(n)/n\le 2\sum_{r=m+1}^n(a_r-a_{r-1})/r\le 2K\sum_{j=m}^\infty j^{\lambda-1}/(j+1)\le 2K\left(m^{\lambda-2}+\frac{m^{\lambda-1}}{1-\lambda}\right).`$
   The last bound uses $`j+1\ge j`$ and the decreasing integral comparison for $`j^{\lambda-2}`$. **Standard.**

3. **Total limiting mass (line 26 and (1)).** Inputs: steps 1–2, the degree sum over a tree, dominated convergence for each of the first $`m`$ terms bounded by one, and monotone convergence in $`m`$. Quote: “the degree sum over cores is $`2(n-1)-(n-C_n)=n+C_n-2`$”; its normalized expectation tends to 1. Thus “$`\mathbb E\sum_{v\le m} Z_v\ge1-O(m^{\lambda-1})`$” with deficit bounded by (2). For the pointwise upper bound use $`\sum_v L_v(n)/n\le1`$ for $`n\ge3`$ and Fatou for the countable label sum (or the core degree sum plus $`C_n/n\to0`$ a.s.). Hence $`\sum_v Z_v=1`$ a.s. Absent the cited leaf limits, the available mean power bound also implies $`C_n/n\to0`$ a.s. by dyadic Markov/Borel–Cantelli and monotonicity. **Standard.**

4. **Pointwise probabilities upgrade to total variation, then weak convergence (line 28).** Inputs: step 3 and the fixed-label leaf-neighbour limits. Quote: “$`\mathbb P(W_n=v\mid T_n)=n^{-1}(L_v(n)+\sum_{w\sim v, \ D_w(n)\ge2}1/D_w(n))`$”. Its non-leaf term is bounded by $`C_n/n`$; this tends to zero on the same full-probability event. Alternatively $`C_n/n\to0`$ follows pathwise from mass one and leaf limits, since each finite sum of $`L_v(n)/n`$ is bounded by $`(n-C_n)/n`$. On a finite label prefix the weights converge, and the remaining mass is arbitrarily small because the limit vector sums to one; this is total variation convergence. For a bounded continuous test replace each degree argument on that prefix by its limit $`Z_v`$ and then send the prefix length to infinity. The image law is $`\sum_v Z_v\delta_{Z_v}`$. **Elementary.**

## Localized route using a pathwise power bound

5. **Stop promotion immigration, include the crossing increment (lines 30–35).** Inputs: step 1 supermartingale, $`C_n=O(n^\lambda)`$ a.s. for deterministic $`\lambda<1`$ from the cited preprint, and $`\tau=\inf\{n\ge3:C_n>K n^\lambda\}`$. Start a nonnegative sum at zero at size $`m`$, activate on $`\{\tau>m\}`$, add all cores promoted after $`m`$ through the stopping step, and freeze thereafter. Quote: its expected stopped sum is at most “$`2\mathbb E\sum_{r=m+1}^n (C_{r\wedge\tau}-C_{(r-1)\wedge\tau})/r`$” (line 34). **Delicate:** check the update indicator is $`\mathbf1_{\{\tau\ge r\}}=\mathbf1_{\{\tau>r-1\}}`$, measurable before the jump; old entries use their normalized degrees at the actual crossing step and then remain frozen, while a new entry adds $`2/r`$. The sum has a positive immigration term: it is the sum less accumulated immigration, or each existing stopped entry, that has the supermartingale inequality.

6. **Uniform bound by summation by parts (line 36).** Inputs: step 5; $`C_{r\wedge\tau}\le K r^\lambda+1`$ (unit jump, including $`\tau=3`$ when the initial count is one, and $`\lambda\ge0`$ in any nonvacuous power bound). Quote: “$`O_\lambda((K+1)m^{\lambda-1})`$ uniformly in $`n`$”. With $`B_r=C_{r\wedge\tau}`$, the sum without the factor 2 equals $`B_n/n-B_m/(m+1)+\sum_{r=m+1}^{n-1}B_r/[r(r+1)]`$, so a sufficient upper bound including factor 2 is $`2(K+1)(1+1/(1-\lambda))m^{\lambda-1}`$. **Elementary.**

7. **Localize the missing mass, exhaust the stopping events (lines 36–41).** Inputs: steps 5–6, fixed-label limits, total normalized core degree $`1+(C_n-2)/n`$, pointwise mass upper bound from step 3, and Fatou. On $`\{\tau=\infty\}`$ every core with label $`>m`$ was promoted after $`m`$, so the actual late-labelled mass is bounded by the tracked sum. Its limit there is $`1-\sum_{v\le m}Z_v`$. Quote: “$`\mathbb E[\mathbf1_{\{\tau=\infty\}}(1-\sum_{v\le m}Z_v)]\le O_\lambda((K+1)m^{\lambda-1})`$” (lines 38–39). First send $`m\to\infty`$ at fixed $`K`$; then send $`K\to\infty`$, since the pathwise $`O`$ bound yields a finite random supremum of $`C_n/n^\lambda`$. Step 4 transfers unchanged, now with $`C_n/n\to0`$ directly from the pathwise upper bound. **Standard.**
