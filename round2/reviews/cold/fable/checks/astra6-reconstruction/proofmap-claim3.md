# Claim 3 — Astra's proof map

Sources are relative to `ASTRA_REPO`. Part A follows `round2/fringe/asymptotics.md` §§1–3, part B follows the dependency `round2/operator.md` §§1–5 (including the spatial characterization, although not all of it is needed for Claim 3), and part C follows `round2/degree-law.md` before returning to the bridge in `asymptotics.md` §4. Numbering below is continuous. Exact birth marks are used in A; the marks in B and C are leaf counts.

## A. Sharp mean: `round2/fringe/asymptotics.md`

1. **Analytic weighted transforms and compactness (lines 19–25).** Inputs: Claim 2, `renewal.md` §§3–4: $`R_tW\le e^{\rho t}W`$ with $`\rho=51/100`$, $`I_tW\le1/d+2e^{qt}`$ with $`d=3/20, q=9/25`$, and the first-original-particle decomposition (8). Quote: “$`G_z f(S)=\int_0^\infty e^{-zt}R_t f(S)\,dt, \quad\widehat I_z(F)=\int_0^\infty e^{-zt}I_t F\,dt, \quad jF(a)=F(J_a)`$” (lines 21–23); $`U_zg=G_z[b_Sg(p_S)]`$, $`K_z=jU_z`$. In $`\|\cdot\|_W`$, $`\|G_z\|\le1/(\Re z-\rho)`$ and $`\|\widehat I_z\|\le1/(d\Re z)+2/(\Re z-q)`$ on their respective half-planes; Taylor series converge in norm there by exponential domination. Root mark affects the original particle only by the constant death rate, so the first-particle decomposition gives continuity of $`jG_z f`$ for $`f=1, f_k`$ and row total-variation equicontinuity for the bounded-input $`K_z`$; hence compactness by Arzelà–Ascoli and operator holomorphy. **Delicate:** check dependence on the root mark (including the limit mark zero), that the integrand before its survival factor is independent of this mark for these scores, and that the child-weight bound controls the row even with arbitrarily large intrinsic degrees. No norm-continuity of $`R_t`$ in time on the entire weighted space is needed.

2. **Transform of the physical mean (lines 27–33, (3)).** Inputs: step 1; Claim 2, `renewal.md` (3), immigration convolution, (7) next generation, (9) nonnegative generation series, $`r(K_s)<1`$ for $`s>\mu`$. Quote: “$`\int_0^\infty e^{-zt} M_tf\,dt=\widehat I_z(G_z f+U_z(I-K_z)^{-1}jG_z f)`$” (3). At complex $`z`$ with $`\Re z>\mu`$ the generation terms are dominated absolutely by their real-discount counterparts for $`|f|`$; Neumann series sums in norm by spectral radius $`<1`$ at the real argument and $`|K_z^n g|\le K_{\Re z}^n|g|`$. Use Fubini/Tonelli with the immigration bound. **Standard.**

3. **Critical residue (lines 37–41).** Inputs: step 1, Claim 2's simple strongly positive compact Perron eigenvalue, analytic spectral projection on a small circle, dual eigenmeasure and the positive reproduction-time measure. Normalize $`\ell(g)=1`$. Strong positivity implies $`\ell(v)=\ell(K_\mu v)>0`$ for nonzero $`v\ge0`$. Quote: “$`\lambda'(\mu)=\ell(K'_\mu g)<0`$” (line 39); the inverse on the complementary spectral projection is analytic and the residue is $`g\otimes\ell/[-\ell(K'_\mu g)]`$ (line 41). **Standard:** the $`t`$-weighted Laplace integral is finite because $`\mu>\rho`$; strict negativity follows from positive reward at positive times.

4. **No other poles on the entire boundary line (line 43).** Inputs: steps 1 and 3 and compact Fredholm alternative. For $`\tau\ne0`$, assume $`K_{\mu+i\tau}v=v\ne0`$. Quote: “$`|v|\le K_\mu |v|`$”; full support of $`\ell`$ turns this into equality everywhere and simplicity gives $`|v|`$ a positive multiple of $`g`$. Each row has a density component for mark $`1/2`$ through the original newborn alive and unchanged at time $`t`$ (lower bound $`\tfrac12 e^{-(\mu+a+2)t}`$ suffices by excluding both vertex rings). On that component $`e^{-i\tau t}v(1/2)`$ cannot have a constant phase almost everywhere on any nontrivial time interval. Triangle equality fails, so $`I-K_{\mu+i\tau}`$ has bounded inverse, analytic locally. **Delicate:** equality must hold for the integrated complex reward in each row, not merely for the absolute values of aggregated mark masses; a time interval with positive density at the same discrete mark rules it out. Only local continuation along the line is used in this proof; no high-frequency estimate or uniform strip is asserted.

5. **Pole-subtracted scalar transform and positive coefficients (lines 45–50, (4)).** Inputs: steps 2–4 and Claim 2's positive founder immigration (initial star promotes at rate one before a first promotion). Quote: “$`d_f=\widehat I_\mu(U_\mu g)\ell(jG_\mu f)/[-\ell(K'_\mu g)]`$” (4). The scalar transform minus $`d_f/(z-\mu)`$ is locally analytic at every point of $`\Re z=\mu`$. The reward in $`jG_\mu f`$ is positive for $`f_k`$ and for $`f_k-f_{k+1}`$: the original residual newborn can survive, gain enough root leaves, then hold the specified degree for an interval, all with positive probability. **Standard.**

6. **Time asymptotic by Wiener–Ikehara and edge convolution (lines 54–60, (5)).** Inputs: steps 2, 5 and Debruyne–Vindas Theorem 1.1 and subsequent paragraph (cited in line 60), with nondecreasing $`M_t f_k`$. Under $`x=\mu t`$ the transform of $`M_{x/\mu}f_k`$ is $`\mu\mathcal L[M_\cdot f_k](\mu z)`$, residue $`d_{f_k}`$ at 1. Quote: “$`M_t f_k\sim d_{f_k} e^{\mu t}`$” (line 54). The founder adds at most one, and the first wait from the edge is independent rate 2; convolving against its density gives “$`\mathbb E_{T_2}N_{\ge k}(t)\sim d_k^*e^{\mu t}, \quad d_k^*=2d_{f_k}/(2+\mu)`$” (5). **Delicate:** check the exact Laplace-transform Tauberian formulation, monotonicity implying its one-sided condition, local analytic continuation implying the required boundary condition, and the residue normalization under rescaling (no extra $`\mu`$). The independent wait uses dominated convergence with the normalized $`T_3`$ mean bounded.

7. **Fixed-size transform convergence (lines 62–70).** Inputs: step 6, independence of holding times and jump chain, and Yule distribution from two: for $`q=e^{-t}`$ its size weights are $`q^2(n-1)(1-q)^{n-2}`$. Quote: “$`\nu_x=x^{-(\mu+2)}\sum_{n\ge2}(n-1)a_{n, k}\delta_{n/x}`$” (line 68), where $`a_{n, k}=\mathbb E N_{\ge k}(n)`$. Set $`1-q=e^{-s/x}`$, so $`xq\to s`$; the difference between powers $`n`$ and $`n-2`$ gives a factor $`e^{-2s/x}\to1`$. Then $`\int e^{-sy}d\nu_x(y)\to d_k^*s^{-(\mu+2)}`$. Tilt by $`e^{-y}`$ and normalize using the transform at 1; the tilted probability transforms tend to $`(1+s)^{-(\mu+2)}`$. Laplace continuity yields a gamma law of rate one and shape $`\mu+2`$; undo tilting on compact intervals at continuity endpoints for the locally finite limit with density $`d_k^*y^{\mu+1}/\Gamma(\mu+2)`$. **Delicate:** the raw measures have infinite total mass; the continuity theorem is used only after the tilt, and the untilting multiplier is bounded on each bounded interval. Check $`q^2`$ and the start-from-two shape to keep the gamma coefficient correct.

8. **Recover individual means and form a probability (lines 72–76, (1)–(2)).** Inputs: steps 5, 7 and monotonicity of $`a_{n, k}`$ in $`n`$. Weighted averages over $`[(1-h)n, n]`$ and $`[n,(1+h)n]`$ bound $`a_{n, k}`$ below and above respectively, with denominator divided by $`n^2`$ tending to the integral of $`y`$ over the corresponding interval. First let $`n\to\infty`$, then $`h\downarrow0`$. Quote: “$`c_k=d_k^*/\Gamma(\mu+2)`$” (line 74) and “$`p_k=(c_k-c_{k+1})/c_2`$” (line 10). Positivity of differences uses step 5. Dominated convergence in the nonexplosive residual family gives $`jG_\mu f_k(a)\downarrow0`$ for each $`a`$ with bound $`W(J_a)/(\mu-\rho)`$ uniformly in $`a, k`$; integrate against $`\ell`$ to get $`c_k\downarrow0`$ and telescoping total mass 1. **Standard.**

## B. Spatial-law dependency: `round2/operator.md`

9. **Cells and mean semigroup (lines 5, 15–19, (2)).** Inputs: physical friend-tree generator rewritten on the leaf-marked core, where $`r_x=L_x+\sum_{y\sim x}1/D_y`$, $`b_x=L_x/D_x`$, and a promotion adds a core with $`(L, c)=(1,1)`$. Assign old-root labels by inheritance; only old edges connect different cells. Quote: “$`(Q_s f)(G, o)=\mathbb E_{G, o}\sum_{v\in H_o(s)}f(G_s, v)`$” (2). Promotions are at rate at most one per member, so $`Q_s1\le e^s`$ and $`\mathbb E|H_o(s)|^2\le2 e^{2s}`$ by Yule comparison. Relabeling at time $`s`$ gives $`Q_sQ_t=Q_{s+t}`$ by conditional expectation, without assuming cell independence. **Elementary.** Infinite-tree justification follows below.

10. **Infinite tree via old-edge proposals and local stabilization (lines 21–37, (3)).** Inputs: step 9, independent Poisson marked clocks, time ordering, and degree reversibility of simple random walk on the old core. Each oriented old edge $`x\to y`$ proposes at rate $`1/c_x^0`$ and accepts with probability $`c_x^0/D_x`$. Retain old boundary slots and suppress inbound arrows across truncated ball. A discrepancy at $`w`$ requires an ordered chain of proposals; erase repeats to find an increasing sequence on the simple old path. Quote: expected tuples on a specified path of length $`j`$ are “$`s^j/j!\prod_{x\text{ sending}}1/c_x^0`$” (line 25); sum of products over simple paths ending at $`w`$ is at most $`c_w^0`$ by reversal. With $`j=R-k+1`$ and $`p=\min(1,(s^j/j!)\sum_{w\in B_k(o)}c_w^0)`$, quote “$`|Q_s f-Q_s^{[R]}f|(G, o)\le 2\sqrt2 e^s\|f\|_\infty\sqrt p`$” (3) by two Cauchy–Schwarz bounds. Each finite collection of cells is nonexplosive: finite total intensity of inbound proposals and endogenous rates bounded by physical vertex counts. A summable, common discrepancy event controls all larger truncations; defines the infinite law with the Markov property. The same local coupling yields continuity on bounded cylinders and, with Yule uniform integrability, on $`C_b(\mathcal X)`$. **Delicate:** systematically check path erasure/reversal at arbitrary unbounded old degrees, that all required final balls of root-cell members stay in old $`B_k(o)`$, that truncation keeps boundary degrees for sends, and that the discrepancy event controls all larger balls simultaneously rather than only each pair separately.

11. **Time-tilted approximate eigenmeasure (lines 41–45, (4)).** Inputs: step 9, Claim 2's input exponent from `proofs/existence.md` Theorem 1, and the independent rate-two start wait. With $`F`$ denoting the edge mean, $`F(t)\le F_3(t)\le F(t+1)/(1-e^{-2})`$ gives the $`T_3`$ exponent. For $`m_{t+h}=m_tQ_h`$, take $`w(t)=e^{-\mu t-\delta|t-T|}`$, $`Z=\int_0^\infty wF_3`$, and $`\pi_{\delta, T}=Z^{-1}\int_0^\infty w m_t`$. At fixed $`\delta`$, $`Z\ge e^{-\mu(T+1)-\delta}F_3(T)`$ whereas $`w(0)=e^{-\delta T}`$; arrange $`w(0)/Z\to0`$ as $`\delta\downarrow0`$. On $`u\ge h`$, $`w(u-h)/w(u)\in[e^{(\mu-\delta)h}, e^{(\mu+\delta)h}]`$. The missing initial interval contributes $`O_h(w(0)/Z)`$ since $`F_3(t)\le e^t`$. Quote: “$`\|\pi_{\delta, T}Q_h-e^{\mu h}\pi_{\delta, T}\|_{\rm TV}\to0`$” (4). **Standard.**

12. **Moment bounds for the tilts (lines 47–49, (5)).** Inputs: step 11, conditional birth-age moment estimates; degree is initially two, evolves with gain rate at most degree, and core degree is stochastically bounded by $`1+\mathrm{Pois}(a)`$ at age $`a`$ by its own clock. These also dominate the founder. For $`\alpha<\min(1, \mu)`$ and $`\theta<\log(1+\mu)`$ with both positive, conditional moments are $`\mathbb E D^\alpha\le2^\alpha e^{\alpha a}`$ and $`\mathbb E e^{\theta c}\le e^\theta e^{(e^\theta-1)a}`$. Compensation over promotions of total mean rate at most $`F_3(t)`$, with $`w(t+a)\le w(t)e^{-(\mu-\delta)a}`$, gives quote “$`\pi_{\delta, T}(\text{moment})\le B(1+w(0)/Z)/(\mu-\delta-\kappa)`$” when the corresponding age bound is $`Be^{\kappa a}`$ and $`\delta<\mu-\kappa`$ (5). **Standard.**

13. **Ball tightness and passage to an eigenlaw (lines 51–55).** Inputs: steps 10–12, mass transport on finite root-uniform laws, Cauchy–Schwarz, Prokhorov on the Polish marked-ball space. Put $`E_J=\{D_o>J\}`$; the moment bound implies uniform $`\pi_{\delta, T}(c_o\mathbf1_{E_J})\to0`$. Choose degree cutoffs $`J_i`$ successively by distance. Path reversal via mass transport bounds the expected number of simple offending paths of length $`i`$ by quote “$`(J_1\cdots J_{i-1})\pi_{\delta, T}(c_o\mathbf1_{D_o>J_i})`$” (line 53). Choose summable failure probabilities over radii and extract a weak limit of the tilts; bounded cylinders and step 10 yield $`\pi Q_h=e^{\mu h}\pi`$ for each $`h`$, with the same subsequence. Retain the degree moments by lower semicontinuity. **Delicate:** root degree tightness alone does not control neighbouring degrees; check path reversal rooted at the bad endpoint gives exactly the displayed count and hence finite marked-ball cutoffs on a set of large mass.

14. **Pointwise lower exponent and minimality (lines 59–67, (6)).** Inputs: step 10's infinite construction; `proofs/existence.md` Corollary 5.3 and Lemmas 3.1, 5.1, 6.1. Given $`M\ge2`$, $`\epsilon\in(0,1)`$, fix $`k`$ from that comparison; for all sufficiently large $`s`$ some rooted $`k`$-vertex seed across a parent of degree at least $`M`$ contributes quote “$`e^{-\eta s}[F(s)-k-\theta_k(s)]/k`$” (6), where $`\eta=7\log2/\log(1/\epsilon)+9/M`$ and $`\theta_k(s)=\sqrt{k(k+1)(2k+1)}e^{-s}`$ in existence Corollary 5.3. The rooted seed may depend on $`s`$. If $`o`$ lacks a leaf, silence all neighbours of a chosen neighbour $`w`$ (including $`o`$) for one unit and require $`w`$'s first ring to select $`o`$; if already leafy silence $`o`$. Next silence $`o`$ and let a tagged leaf ring at least $`M`$ times in one unit. In the third unit script a root ring choosing a leaf with probability at least $`(M+1)/(M+1+c_o)`$ per such ring; protect the selected leaf and newborn until the unit ends. Script the remaining $`k-2`$ births, one per unit under exterior-parent silence, with per-unit lower bound $`e^{-(k+1)}/k`$ in Lemma 3.1. This gives fixed delay $`k+1`$ and $`p(G, o, M, k)>0`$ uniform over the finitely many seeds. Multiply (6) by $`p`$, send $`s\to\infty`$, then $`\epsilon\downarrow0`$ and $`M\to\infty`$. Thus $`\liminf s^{-1}\log Q_s1(G, o)\ge\mu`$. For $`\sigma Q_1=r\sigma`$, Fatou applied to $`e^{-(\mu-a)n}Q_n1`$ for arbitrary $`a>0`$ gives $`r\ge e^\mu`$; step 13 attains equality. **Delicate:** the weighted cut-edge lower comparison must survive the locally finite exterior, including the conditional law of the external parent clock; check the planted branch remains in the root cell and the finite-clock event covers the entire finite (but not degree-uniform) required set.

15. **Drift tests and edge transfers (lines 71–77, (7)).** Inputs: steps 9–10, 12–13, finite-truncation Dynkin formula, Yule bounds, and mass transport. Quote: “$`Af(G, o)=\sum_x[r_x\Delta_x^+f+b_x\Delta_x^bf]+b_of(G^{b_o}, \mathrm{new})`$” (7). For cylinder scores supported on bounded-degree balls, and for the constant score, this is a bounded local drift. Dynkin gives $`Q_tf-f=\int_0^t Q_sAf\,ds`$ for these tests; apply the eigenlaw for $`\pi Af=\mu\pi f`$, in particular $`\pi b_o=\mu`$. Mass transport cancels the two stated transfer divergences, with arc values “$`r_wg(w, u)`$” and “$`\mathbf1_{D_w\ge T}a(D_u, c_u)/D_w`$” (line 77): pass bounded degree-local transports to the weak limit, then exhaust for nonnegative transports; the signed finite-support tables have integrability through (5). **Standard:** the transfer conclusion is auxiliary; Claim 3 needs the eigenlaw, exponential core-degree moment and $`\pi b_o=\mu`$.

16. **Full spectral radius on $`C_b`$ (lines 81–88, (8)).** Inputs: steps 10, 13–14, `round2/finite/one-port-upper.md` (1)–(2), spectral-radius formula. The cited bound is $`\mathbb E C_B(t)\le A_j(t)e^{\Lambda_jt}F(t+1)`$ uniformly in the physical exterior at direct birth, with $`\Lambda_j\le6/j+2/j^2`$ for $`j\ge4`$ and $`\log A_j(t)=O_j(\log(2+t))`$. Conditional compensation of the founder's direct promotions at rate at most one bounds its cell by the quote “$`Q_t1(G, o)\le 1+t A_j(t)e^{\Lambda_j t}F(t+1)`$” (8). Extend to infinite inputs by finite completions preserving degrees on each old ball (append a core with one leaf per missing boundary slot), using (3). Then $`\|Q_1^n\|=\|Q_n1\|_\infty`$, the upper exponential rate is $`\mu+\Lambda_j`$ for every $`j`$, and the eigenlaw gives the lower bound $`e^{\mu n}`$. Thus $`r(Q_1)=e^\mu`$; together with minimality this is the only positive probability eigenvalue. **Standard:** this step imports rather than proves the exterior-uniform one-port estimate; it is not needed to identify the degree marginal.

## C. Degree tail: `round2/degree-law.md` and the bridge

17. **Age mixture and the newborn Palm law (`degree-law.md` lines 13–21, (2)).** Inputs: steps 9, 13 and compensation over births in the old root's cell. With $`S_t`$ following the same old root and $`Bf=b_of(G^{b_o}, v)`$ at the newborn, quote: “$`Q_tf=S_tf+\int_0^t Q_s B S_{t-s}f\,ds`$” (line 15). Each new root is counted once at its own birth; integrability is bounded by the cell Yule first moment. Apply $`\pi`$, divide by $`e^{\mu t}`$, then take the limit for bounded nonnegative $`f`$. Quote: “$`\pi f=\int_0^\infty e^{-\mu a}\pi B S_a f\,da`$” (2). In particular $`\pi B1=\mu`$ (also step 15) and $`\beta=\pi B/\mu`$ is the newborn law; the mixture age has exponential law of rate $`\mu`$, independent of future evolution given the mixture's initial sampling. **Standard.**

18. **Normalized newborn degree, uniform domination, and positive moment (`degree-law.md` lines 25–27).** Inputs: step 17, degree gain rate $`L+\rho_o\le D`$ where $`\rho_o=\sum_{w\sim o\,{\rm core}}1/D_w`$, Yule comparison, nonnegative supermartingale convergence, and Doob $`L^2`$. Quote: “$`e^{-t}D(t)`$ is a nonnegative supermartingale and converges almost surely to a random $`W`$” (line 25). A Yule process from two has $`\mathbb E(e^{-t}Y(t))^2=6-2e^{-t}`$, giving uniform integrability and an integrable bound for $`\sup_t(e^{-t}D(t))^\mu`$, uniformly in newborn exterior. Quote: “$`A=\mathbb E_\beta W^\mu`$ is positive” (line 27). To show positivity, require one unit of root silence and at least two rings of its tagged leaf (a fixed positive probability). From any state with $`\ell\ge3`$ leaves the leaf drift is $`L+\rho_o-b\ge L-1`$, giving $`\mathbb E[L(t)-1]\ge e^t(\ell-1)`$ from that state. Uniform integrability, with Yule started at its fixed total degree, makes the limiting normalized degree have positive conditional mean. **Delicate:** ensure the degree and leaf jump comparisons are valid for all (including infinite) sampled environments, and use uniform integrability at the right starting state; lower bounds on prelimit means alone do not give positive limit.

19. **Threshold tail via hitting times (`degree-law.md` line 29, (1)).** Inputs: steps 17–18, monotone one-step degree gains and nonexplosion. Quote: “$`\pi(D_o\ge k)=\mathbb E_\beta[e^{-\mu T_k};T_k<\infty]`$” (line 29). On unbounded growth, $`D(T_k)=k`$, $`T_k\to\infty`$, and $`k e^{-T_k}\to W`$; on bounded growth put the reward equal to zero for unreachable levels, and $`W=0`$. Its $`\mu`$-power is dominated by the integrable supremum in step 18. Hence $`k^\mu\pi(D_o\ge k)\to A`$. **Standard.**

20. **Single-degree tail via flux (`degree-law.md` lines 33–37, (1)).** Inputs: step 19, eigenlaw step 13, exponential $`c_o`$ moment from steps 12–13, compensated threshold crossings inside the old-root cell. Quote: “$`\mu\pi(D_o\ge k+1)=\pi[(k-c_o+\rho_o)\mathbf1_{D_o=k}]`$” (line 35); newborns have degree two and so enter no threshold $`k+1`$ for $`k\ge2`$. Though the test need not have bounded degrees throughout a ball, the crossing intensity per member is at most $`k`$ and $`Q_t1\le e^t`$, making compensation integrable. With $`0\le\rho_o\le c_o/2`$, the flux is at most $`k\pi(D_o=k)`$ and at least $`(1-\delta)k[\pi(D_o=k)-\pi(c_o>\delta k)]`$ for any $`0<\delta<1`$. The error is exponentially small; use step 19 at $`k+1`$ then $`\delta\downarrow0`$. **Delicate:** justify the balance for the actual threshold score despite the narrower generator domain stated in `operator.md` §4; mere differencing of equivalent tail asymptotics would not give the point law.

21. **Identify the degree marginal of the spatial law (`asymptotics.md` §4, line 80).** Inputs: steps 5–8, 11–13 and 20. Quote: “$`m_t(f_k)/m_t(1)\to d_{f_k}/d_1`$” and “$`\pi(D_o\ge k)=c_k/c_2`$” (line 80). Under the tilts, the fraction of weighted mean mass in any fixed bounded time interval goes to zero by $`w(0)/Z\to0`$ and boundedness of $`w(t)/w(0)`$ there. Thus each tilted expectation for the degree threshold tends to $`d_{f_k}/d_1`$; the threshold is bounded continuous on the marked core space and weak convergence transfers it. Consequently $`p_k=\pi(D_o=k)\sim\mu A k^{-1-\mu}`$ with $`A`$ the moment in step 18, after the fixed-size limit at each fixed $`k`$. **Standard.**
