**Mechanism, and a literal interpretation to reject.** A long delay before promotion costs a geometric factor `q^k` in the upper bound. Indeed conditional on the rare endpoint, delay at least `k≥1` has probability bounded by `min(1,C_x sqrt(v) q^k)` using the stronger bound below, so delays beyond a sufficiently large fixed multiple of `log v` are negligible. That is not a claim that almost all *immediate* births hit the tag at a fixed `x`. There is a precise sense in which successful histories need a macroscopic early influx: at size `n=⌈Lv⌉` for fixed `L>1`, the shifted urn can be restarted with red `D_v(n)−1/2`. Beta can be bounded by Beta with integer red rounded up and white rounded down, using gamma monotonicity; its upper tail then equals a binomial lower-tail probability. Uniformly on `D_v(n)≤z n` with `0<z<x` this is at most `exp[-n(D_KL(z||x)+o(1))]`, where `D_KL(z||x)=z log(z/x)+(1−z)log((1−z)/(1−x))`. Choose `L` with `L D_KL(z||x)>−log q`; after division by the lower bound the conditional probability of that low-degree event vanishes. This forces order `v` of the first order `v` births to hit the tag, and by taking `z` close to `x` forces any prescribed fraction strictly below `x` on a sufficiently long such window. There is no requirement for a majority when `x` is small. In the lower construction what produces the rate is a marked Yule family with atypically large amplitude and an exponentially tilted exterior; the dominant tilted exterior size is near `(1−x)(v−1)` and the marked amplitude needed is near `x(v−1)`.

**Actual gap in the printed marked-census proof, and repair.** In line 55 the alphabet bound is `T_n ≤ B exp(O(U log(U+N)))` (with an extra factor `U` for pointed survivors). Modulo its automorphism group of order at most `2^(L−1)`, this gives at least `2^(L+1)` distinct admitted types. All are physically reachable, since any prescribed leaf insertion has positive conditional probability. ### Importance sampling and independent constant approximation (B, C)

Compiled with `taskset -c 12 nice -n 5 timeout 30 env TMPDIR=. g++ -std=c++17 -O3 -Wall -Wextra rare.cpp -o rare`. An initial debugging run aborted at the first-defect roulette because the tag interval's length 1 was not subtracted before scanning the old directed intervals. All results below use the corrected source. A trial run `./rare finite 40000 16384` completed only `v=3` within the imposed 90-second timeout; that partial run is not used for comparison below.

Method: simulate the true birth tree, then importance-sample its **forced** all-tag prefix through pre-size `K`. Proposal is a half-half mixture of discretized Lomax(shape 2) delays `K−v` at scales `(v−.5)/ε` and one eighth of that scale. Weight a success by the exact physical prefix product times `(v−1/d)/K` divided by the proposal bin mass. Add the exact censored survival mass to size `n` if its degree satisfies the threshold. At `K` draw the true outside arrow by directed weights, then simulate the exact continuation to size `n` or until a too-large deficit. Many ordinary leaves of the tag can be compressed into one integer stock: with `a` explicit vertices and `s` anonymous leaf donors, the physical number of consecutive anonymous-initiator births before an explicit initiator has the geometric distribution mixed over `Beta(a,s)` (the all-anonymous stretch is a two-colour urn). After that wait the explicit initiator is uniform over the `a` and its neighbour is drawn using the actual degree. This avoids approximate time stepping and does not force late births to feed the tag.

`taskset -c 12 nice -n 5 timeout 150 ./rare ideal 16000 7` independently simulates the limiting first-defect model directly from the birth tree, skipping runs with `d>2` (their contributions are zero). For each remaining tree use the exact limiting Palm mixture and simulate the whole independent inert branch/immigration system up to `H`. Its stopped deficit `N_H` gives the naive estimate using `e^-H N_H`. Also compute deterministic *conditional expectation* bounds for the inverse moment given the whole ideal state at `H`. With `c_H` current branch count and `A_H=Σ_B(1−1/D_root)`, the drift gives `E[Y | F_H]≤e^-H (N_H+c_H+1)` since the future surplus is bounded by `c_H+Pois_clock(s)` and `∫_0∞e^-s(c_H+s)ds=c_H+1`. Jensen gives the lower estimator. For the upper estimator use the Yule lower comparison from shifted mass `N_H+A_H`, valid because `A` is nondecreasing even in the ideal system and its deficit rate is `N+A`. Then `Y` stochastically dominates `e^-H Gamma(N_H+A_H,1)` conditionally and the inverse moment is bounded by its gamma quotient. These are inequalities for **expectations** of the estimators; their sampled means are not deterministic intervals. The lower and upper estimator means are close relative to sampling error:
