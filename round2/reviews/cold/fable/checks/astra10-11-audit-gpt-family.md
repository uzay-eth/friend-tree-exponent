## 4. Exact checks (independent code)

`check_exact.py` enumerates the jump chain from the size-three founder, merging trees by unordered rooted shape and carrying exact rational state probabilities. The state includes the ordinary founder orientation, without birth marks (which are irrelevant for these identities). It enumerates every directed source–recipient arrow and explicitly recomputes the ordinary successor's branch, core degree, degree, and leaf stock. For each nonfounder core it compares the resulting infinitesimal rates with the four formulas in `other-marginals.md` and its degree-law input; it checks the pure-birth bounding rates and the upper degree rate. It also checks the finite-time **aggregate** crossing formula for both branch size and core degree at each level 2–8. Newborns have sizes/degrees below the tested crossing thresholds. The continuous-time series uses $`q=1-e^{-t}`$ and weights $`\binom{n-1}{2}(1-q)^3q^{n-3}`$, independently differentiates the threshold occupancy, and checks the finite-horizon discounted crossing equation coefficient by coefficient (at rational discount 2). This tests the physical flux, not stationarity of a finite-size distribution or the limiting age law.
