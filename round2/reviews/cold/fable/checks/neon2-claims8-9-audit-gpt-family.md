## Independent exact tests

`python3 -B exact_trees.py` generated all 47 free trees of sizes 2–8 by leaf addition and canonical encodings. For every tree it compared the two-birth identity **coefficient by coefficient** on marked rooted shapes against direct caller–neighbour enumeration, and checked that the coefficient vector discarded in the endpoint split was nonnegative. It then enumerated the two births plus one and plus two further births, checking exact probability mass and grouping the terminal signed measure by rooted shape.

The exact-test upper bounds did not use Neon-2’s tables. For integer $`k`$, with $`q_k(d)=d^{\overline k}/k!`$, I used
```math
\frac{q_k((L-1)_+)}{q_k(N)}
\ \le\ \mathbb E[Z^k\mid T']\ \le\ \frac{q_k(D)}{q_k(N)}.
```
The lower bound follows from the independently checked leaf-count drift with discount $`k`$, and the upper from $`r\le D`$ and $`q_k(D+1)-q_k(D)=kq_k(D)/D`$; the audited Yule moment domination justifies the limits. For noninteger rational $`p\ge1`$ I used Jensen’s lower bound $`((L-1)_+/N)^p`$ and the $`k=\lceil p\rceil`$ moment upper bound raised to $`p/k`$ by Jensen. Roots were rounded outward using integer comparisons. Positive signed masses use upper endpoints and negative masses lower endpoints.
