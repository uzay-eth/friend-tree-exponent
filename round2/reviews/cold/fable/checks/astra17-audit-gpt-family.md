### Exact input and dependency ledger

* S6.3 and S6.4 apply to **every finite free tree with a core**, rooted at a core and compatibly marked for the renewal calculation. Such marks can always be assigned (e.g. `1/2` on old nonfounder cores); `Φ=d₂Ψ` is physical, independent of the marks/orientation. S6.3's errors `O(C(T)e^(σt))` are uniform in tree and admissible score; S6.4 gives the eigenfunction, upper comparison, additive-error reverse comparison and nonnegative martingale. All uses of the reverse linear comparison pure `aC` are for large `C`, uniformly. S6.7 is prior L² closure (the sharper bound in L45 is new); S6.8 identifies the continuous limit from `T₃` and its discrete counterpart; S6.9 gives the exact conditional means and L² discrete identification. For L42–L80 one only needs the normalized eigenmartingale from arbitrary finite core seeds, not an unquoted generalized S6.8 theorem about count convergence. Alternatively discrete a.s. existence and positivity transfer to any finite seed by pruning to an edge, reversing to a positive-probability prefix and conditioning. This does not transfer universal tail constants.
* The live-cell bound used at the random promotion time does **not** follow from the existence of a spatial eigenmeasure or almost-sure local convergence alone. It is the uniform estimate in the linked Claim 10 proof `round2/fringe/spatial-limit.md`, §1, eqs. (2)–(3); it also follows directly there from S6.2 (`P_u1≤B e^(μu)W` on every planted start) and S2/S5's capped physical cancellation. Start from an arbitrary whole finite free environment and keep the selected old core's cell: only its **new** children appear in its immigration term. Its old neighbours stay active. The S2.2 path-weight drift bound (unchanged under S5 clamping) gives the cell weight expectation ≤`2e^(qu)` since it starts with only the root. Then `I_uW≤1/d+2e^(qu)` (`d=3/20`, `q=.36`), uniformly even if the old root has high degree, and integration gives `C_Q≤1+B(1/(dμ)+2/(μ−q))`. Conditional elapsed horizons are permitted because the bound is uniform in the stopped current environment and every elapsed time; the random horizon `t−β` is then known. Neither one-endedness nor spatial uniqueness is used. No silent-slot limit is needed.
* The linked physical infection lemma supplies the old-edge bound as reconstructed above. S6.5 only supplies `3e^(2u)` and cannot replace that contraction or the critical `d^(−μ)` modulus. S2/S5 (particularly S5.1 and the proved bracket for `μ`) enter upstream in S6.1–3 and the cell estimate; no need to reevaluate the renewal matrix or the exponent. Claim 3's fixed-size mean, degree law and order of limits in degree are not independent inputs to 17. Its mean agrees with `E X=c₂`: S6.4 and S6.9 give L² boundedness of `C_n/n^μ`, hence uniform integrability. Claim 7 is unused.

## Gaps, scope and conclusion

No unrepaired gap in the two statements on the board. The R13 wording must not extend `Φ≤KC` literally to the edge.
