## Derivation and attacks on the ledger

Below, `σ=+1` lower and `−1` upper. Orient toward the first core, starting at the three-vertex path (the first birth from the edge produces it). For a nonfounder `x=(D,k)` set `L=D−k−1`, `b=L/D`, `ρ=Σ_core neighbours 1/D_sender`. Each leaf sends to its core at rate 1, and the core sends to its leaves at total rate `b`, so the old-core count changes only on promotions. Within the projected census the two moves are gain at `L+ρ` and promotion at `b`. For a fixed fringe all intrinsic changes add one ordinary vertex and have total rate `n−1/D_root`; the apex's outgoing arrow to its parent is the excluded term. The exterior parent can change any time, but its current reciprocal belongs to `[0,1/2]`. All Bellman proofs are rate inequalities conditional on the complete *current* physical history, not Markov claims about hidden neighbours or birth-degree marks. A lower Bellman value at discount HI gives a lower expected discounted reward by integrating `(HI−G)V<=reward`, with killed values and/or bounded discounted boundary terms.

For normalized Laplace means write `I_a(F)=∫_0^∞e^(−at)E F(T_t)dt` and divide by `I_a(C)` as `a↓μ` from above. The mean theorem gives `I_a(C)→∞`; the normalized discounted birth count is `a−C(T3)/I_a(C)→μ`. For each physical newborn, condition on its real birth environment and use the uniform discounted value. The discrepancy from replacing `a` by `μ` is uniformly `o(1)` for these bounded masked rewards, by dominated convergence of the scalar discount kernel. Thus nonnegative rewards can be priced at discount `HI>=μ` and prefactor `LO<=μ`; the argument even works if μ=HI. For older tags, the running completed count with positive reward is uniformly bounded per apex by its arity cut and each tip cap; at finite ages prefixes have expected counts bounded by fixed-degree polynomials by rate-one promotions. This permits the terminal-limit argument for the branching registration sums. On bounded intervals Dynkin holds by localization (Yule vertex count); integration by parts at `a>μ` and comparison `c0 C<=Φ<=C0 C` give `I_a(σ(G−λ)Φ)=σ(a−λ)I_a(Φ)−σΦ(T3)`. Absolute integrability of the pointwise signed generator follows here from the one-sided charge, the potential comparison and this integration by parts. The ratio proof need not assume convergence of an unbounded local drift in the spatial law. With ceiling `B`, a certified positive paid numerator `R` yields `μ>=λ+R/B` or `μ<=λ−R/B`.
