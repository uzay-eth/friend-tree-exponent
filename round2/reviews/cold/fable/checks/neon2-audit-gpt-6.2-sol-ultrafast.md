## Comparison with the independent Fable work

**(a) Dichotomy.** Neon is discrete and uses only infinitely many leaf changes ⇒ repeated chances at a stock of at least three ⇒ some urn survives. D1 is continuous time: a frozen-core-degree birth–death comparison and a large-stock moment argument imply the nonhub leaf stock stays bounded. A no-ring window at the core keeps a tagged leaf unpromoted while that leaf’s rate-one clock can ring repeatedly. These windows can be taken at successive (locally nonexplosive) stopping times separated by at least one, with a uniform strictly positive conditional success probability at each available start. Thus the nonhub cannot have a leaf at arbitrarily late times and eventually has no more gains. The frozen `c` comparison is sound (`c` only increases); the second comparison’s mean bound is `E[e^-t Y_1(t)]≥k-1+e^-t` since the compensated test `e^-t(Y_1-1)` has nonnegative drift even at the absorbing boundary. Its moments pass to the limit by Yule `L²` domination/uniform integrability. The extinction comparison and the clock-window proof do not use the edge theorem. D1 gives useful state-dependent bounds; Neon’s proof is shorter and explicitly constructs positive probability of freezing *after* promotion. The two arguments are independent in their main steps.

**(b) Means.** My 5040 histories give exactly Neon's two posted endpoints; its difference is the reduced `6182603/746496000`, equal to `43278221/5225472000`. The size-eight endpoints are approximately `[.2150498,.4187296]` for label 1 and `[.0336205,.2067677]` for label 3. The independently recomputed size-eleven intervals agree with d1’s summary (roughly `[.2304,.3940]` and `[.0439,.1780]`; those particular four-decimal endpoints are rounded for display, not all rounded outward). D1’s signed grouping explains the additional reach: for each rooted class `s`, exchangeability makes the conditional mean a single number in `[l(s),h(s)]`. For the coefficient `q_i(s)−q_j(s)` with negative sign multiply by `h`, otherwise by `l`; add after cancellation. My three strict size-eleven lower bounds are `.1499926,.0106010,.0021528` when rounded to the nearest seven places (`.1499925,.0106009,.0021528` rounded down, as in d1), matching the strict ordering through label 5. This is stronger than subtracting the separate intervals, and it is distinct from the ordering of the zero atom.

**(c) Finite-degree neighbours.** D1 cites the edge theorem and the normalized leaf limit in the *classifier consistency* part, and also uses the edge theorem as an optional finite-state lower bound. ## Gaps and limits

No substantive gap in the numbered claim. Three places where an incautious reading would leave a gap:

- Line 85, “Lévy's conditional Borel–Cantelli lemma forces infinitely many such sends on this event”: name the arrow filtration, or use recipient-only events in the tree filtration. A labelled extension does not generally determine which donor rang.
- Line 27, “For any finite cylinder event” and “On the positive-probability event of linear neighbour growth”: condition the likelihood on the prepared history and apply it to the tree-measurable `liminf` event, not to the auxiliary event naming the successful urns. The latter event need not belong to the tree sigma field.
- Line 27, “The $`1/n`$ threshold estimates promotion by time $`n`$”: this requires a **strict** threshold. The exact script’s “cross-time brackets” use the very same size on both sides.

The optional uniform-ordinary-vertex paragraph does invoke sublinear core growth; it is not part of the self-contained fixed-label conclusion. Even exact small-size marginal stochastic order does not establish general adjacent order of limiting means or hub probabilities, first-order stochastic domination of the limiting shares, their distribution, or a sharp truncation error for the hub-probability formula with nested limits. Fixed-label escape of nontrivial components does not identify the law seen from a uniformly sampled surviving core or a uniformly counted component, nor give uniform component-size bounds for a growing cutoff.
