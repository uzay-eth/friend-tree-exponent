## 2. The repair (found by the child; the two main steps checked by the lead)

* For (13) no uniform-in-lag gradient is needed, because the argument takes t to infinity along a mesh at a *fixed* lag h and only afterwards lets h grow. On [t - h, t] the martingale r -> E[N_{>=k}(T_t) | F_r] jumps at rate |T_r|, by at most the influence of one added leaf over the remaining time t - r, which the elementary coupling bounds by a constant times e^(2(t - r)) (one discrepancy vertex, each discrepancy creates new ones at rate at most 2; this is the coupling lemma of my own proof `round2/proofs-by-lead/almost-sure-limit-from-uniform-means.md`, step 2). Hence Var(e^(-mu t) N_{>=k}(T_t) | F_{t-h}) <= const e^(-2 mu t) |T_{t-h}| integral_0^h e^(4(h - v) + v) dv, with expectation O_h(e^(-(2 mu - 1) t)), summable along every mesh since mu > 1/2. This replaces the cited gradient and uses nothing but the Yule bound.
* For lower comparability no finite-time inequality is needed: for each fixed planted state the real key renewal theorem already proved in step 3.3(1) gives e^(-mu t) P_t 1(S) -> (trigger amplitude of S) times a positive constant, the trigger amplitude is H(S)/g_2 (so it inherits H >= c_1), Fatou gives liminf e^(-mu t) m_t(T) >= c (C(T) - C_*), and the Abelian limit along real s gives the same bound for Phi. Both steps are real-variable.
* For the claim-14 facts: move the scalar renewal argument to the front, get the sharp upper mean for the inert newborn from it, integrate the transfer identity for s > mu to obtain s M_s(J)(1 - U_s(J)) = 1, and take the real Abelian limit. (Checked by the child only.)

With these changes part 3 would be free of the contour estimate, at the price of losing any rate: the key renewal theorem gives o(1), not O(e^(-(mu - sigma) t)).

## 3. What holds

(ii) L2 convergence at fixed size through the Gamma-normalised martingale Y_n = Phi(T_n) Gamma(n)/Gamma(n + mu) with bounded jumps and Doob's inequality. (iii) Full support by leaf relocation at fixed size. (iv) A scalar key renewal theorem for the mean, with no rate. Child: all follow, with details supplied in its table.
* **Part 2 (re-rooting gradient), checked by the lead given root-independence.** If Phi(T; o) = f(T, o) + sum over non-root cores of U(S_v) does not depend on the root o, then for a leaf added at a core w, root at w: no non-root fringe changes, so |Phi(T+) - Phi(T)| <= 2 sup|f|; for a leaf added at a leaf, root at its parent: the old fringes are unchanged and one newborn fringe J appears with U_mu(J) = 1, so the difference is at most 2 sup|f| + 1. With l = 0.565658 and q = 0.8600511260: 2/(l (1 - q)) = 25.2643, sup|f| <= (1 + 25.2643)/l = 46.4314, bound 93.8627 < 94. (The child notes the same argument with the real forcing gives 49.1992.) * **Part 1 (stationary fringe law).** Holds after supplying the transfer from the inert mean to the spatial marginal; the citation given for "Route A" (a projected generator identity on the whole bounded domain) is not what the cited files state, and the child writes out a complete substitute. The child's exact computations show that a naive killed truncation does *not* preserve the occupation ratio (at cutoff 2 and s = 2 the truncated functional of the constant 1 is 4/7, not 1): the identities are statements about the unlimited operator and cannot be checked or used through finite truncations without boundary terms. This limits what the "explicit" law gives numerically.
* **Part 4 (hub mass under pathwise summability).** Compared by the lead with its own proof of the hub-mass theorem (`round2/proofs-by-lead/hub-mass-theorem.md`), which assumes E C_n <= K n^lambda and bounds the expected late-promotion mass by 2 sum_{r > m} E C_r/(r(r + 1)). **Objection 1, the conditional variance (13). Re-derived by the lead.**
- The repair replaces the cited gradient with an elementary fixed-lag coupling. It couples T_u and T_u^+ on one label space, with discrepancy set A(u), where |A(0)| = 2.
- Clean ring. A vertex outside A has identical neighbourhoods in both trees. It uses one clock, one neighbour choice and one fresh label, so A does not grow. If the chosen neighbour lies in A, it stays in A, and the new leaf is not in A.
- Discrepant ring. A vertex in A rings at rate 1. It adds at most 2 vertices to A in each tree, so A grows by at most 4, at total rate at most |A|.
- Hence E|A(u)| ≤ 2 e^{4u}. Vertices outside A have equal degrees in both trees, which gives (13a): |E_{T+} N_{≥k}(T_u) − E_T N_{≥k}(T_u)| ≤ 2 e^{4u}, for every tree and every k.
- Variance on [t − h, t]:
  - the Doob martingale r ↦ E[N_{≥k}(T_t) | F_r] jumps at total rate |T_r|;
  - each jump is at most 2 e^{4(t−r)}, by (13a);
  - and E[|T_r| | F_{t−h}] = |T_{t−h}| e^{r−t+h}.
  So the conditional variance is at most 4 e^{−2μt} |T_{t−h}| e^{8h} (1 − e^{−7h})/7 ≤ (4/7) e^{8h} e^{−2μt} |T_{t−h}|. With E|T_{t−h}| = 3 e^{t−h}, the second moment is at most (12/7) e^{7h} e^{−(2μ−1)t}. This is summable along every mesh, since μ > 1/2.
