## 1. Line-by-Line Audit of the Total-Variation Proof

1. **Boundary measure & birth-time identity (eqs. (3)–(4)):**
   Counting each core-containing component at its unique highest surviving core $`v`$ (parent $`o`$ has $`D_o > N`$; founder exception $`\le 1/C_n \to 0`$) and transferring to $`o`$ gives $`M_N(f) = \mathbb{E}_\pi[\mathbf{1}_{D_o > N}\sum_{v \in \mathrm{ch}(o)} \mathbf{1}_{D_v \le N} f(F_N(v))]`$. Applying the birth-time representation of $`\pi`$ (independent $`\mathrm{Exp}(\mu)`$ age $`t = L + y`$, $`L = \log N`$, $`\mu e^{-\mu t} dt = \mu N^{-\mu} e^{-\mu y} dy`$) yields eq. (4) cleanly by monotone convergence on bounded nonnegative truncations. **Verified.**

2. **Degree second moment & promotion count domination:**
   Under $`\beta`$, a newborn starts at $`D(0) = 2`$ and gains leaves at rate $`L + \sum_{w \sim o} 1/D_w \le L + c/2 \le D`$, so $`D(t)`$ is dominated by a rate-$`1`$ Yule process from $`2`$ ($`\mathrm{NegBin}(2, e^{-t})`$), whose exact second moment is $`6e^{2t} - 2e^t \le 6e^{2t}`$. Its promotion count $`K_t`$ has predictable rate $`1 - (1+K_{t-})/D(t-) \le 1`$, dominated by $`\mathrm{Pois}(t)`$ ($`\mathbb{E}[K_t^2] \le t + t^2`$). **Verified.**

3. **Early measurability replacement ($`b = \sqrt{L}`$, $`E_N(y) = \{e^{-b}D(b) > e^{-y}\}`$):**
   For every $`y`$ with $`\mathbb{P}_\beta(W = e^{-y}) = 0`$ (all but countably many $`y`$), once $`L`$ is large enough that $`0 < b < t = L + y`$, both $`\mathbf{1}_{E_N(y)}`$ and $`\mathbf{1}_{D(t) > N} = \mathbf{1}_{e^{-t}D(t) > e^{-y}}`$ converge a.s. to $`\mathbf{1}_{W > e^{-y}}`$, so $`\mathbb{P}_\beta(E_N(y) \triangle \{D(t) > N\}) \to 0`$. For every $`|f| \le 1`$, Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the replacement error uniformly in $`f`$ by $`\frac{\sqrt{t+t^2}}{L}\mathbb{P}_\beta(E_N(y)\triangle\{D(t)>N\})^{1/2} \to 0`$, and discarding promotions $`\tau \le b`$ costs at most $`\mathbb{E}[K_b]/L \le b/L \to 0`$ uniformly in $`f`$. **Verified.**

4. **Physical-to-inert branch coupling & internal deletion bound (the key TV step):**
   - On $`E_N(y) \in \mathcal{F}_b`$, $`D(s) > e^{b-y}`$ for all $`s \ge b`$. For any promotion $`\tau \in (b, t]`$, couple the child branch to an inert-parent tree $`J`$ via identical internal arrows. Only an incoming parent arrow $`o \to v_\tau`$ (predictable rate $`1/D(s-) < e^{y-b}`$) can separate them, costing probability $`\le (t-\tau)e^{y-b} \le t e^{y-b}`$. Outgoing arrows $`v_\tau \to o`$ increase $`D(s)`$ (preserving $`D(s) > e^{b-y}`$) and have identical null internal effect as $`J`$'s inert slot; because $`E_N(y)`$ is already $`\mathcal{F}_b`$-measurable and eq. (4) is a linear sum over births $`\tau`$, only each birth's marginal conditional law given $`\mathcal{F}_\tau`$ is needed (no independence across siblings is required).
   - For the physical branch of ordinary size $`m`$ ($`m(0)=2`$), its total size increase rate is $`m - 1/D_{\mathrm{top}} + 1/D_{\mathrm{parent}} \le m + 1/2`$, solving $`g'(u) = g(u) + 1/2, g(0)=2 \implies \mathbb{E}[m(u) \mid \mathcal{F}_\tau] \le \frac{5}{2}e^u - \frac{1}{2} \le \frac{5}{2}e^u`$. Since $`u = t - \tau < L + y - b`$, Markov's inequality gives $`\mathbb{P}(m(t-\tau) > N \mid \mathcal{F}_\tau) \le \frac{5}{2}e^{y-b}`$. Whenever $`m \le N`$, every vertex in the branch (including the top vertex with its $`1`$ exterior edge to $`o`$) has original degree $`\le m \le N`$, so $`D_{v_\tau}(t) \le N`$ and $`F_N(v_\tau; t)`$ equals the entire branch with zero internal deletions.
   - Hence on the complement of an event of conditional probability $`\le (t + 5/2)e^{y-b}`$, $`\mathbf{1}_{D_{v_\tau}(t)\le N} f(F_N(v_\tau; t)) = f(J(t-\tau))`$; on the bad event the difference is $`\le 2`$ for $`|f| \le 1`$. Compensating over $`\tau \in (b, t]`$ gives total error $`\le 2(t/L)(t + 5/2)e^{y-b} \to 0`$ uniformly in $`f`$. **Verified.**

5. **Rate replacement, interval shift, and uniform-in-$`f`$ Dominated Convergence:**
   - Replacing promotion rate $`1 - (1+K_{r-})/D(r-)`$ by $`1`$ on $`E_N(y)`$ costs $`\le \frac{e^{y-b}}{L}\int_0^t (1+r)\,dr = \frac{e^{y-b}}{L}(t + t^2/2) \to 0`$ uniformly in $`f`$; shifting age integration from $`[0, t-b]`$ to $`[0, L]`$ costs $`\le (b + |y|)/L \to 0`$ uniformly in $`f`$.
   - **Uniform-in-$`f`$ DCT check:** Define $`g_N(y) := \sup_{|f|\le 1} \big|\mathbb{E}_\beta[\mathbf{1}_{D(t)>N}\frac{1}{L}\sum_{\tau \le t}\mathbf{1}_{D_{v_\tau}(t)\le N} f(F_N(v_\tau; t))] - \mathbb{P}_\beta(W > e^{-y})\frac{1}{L}\int_0^L P_u^0 f(J)\,du\big|`$ (for $`y \ge -L`$; $`\mathbb{P}_\beta(W > e^{-y})`$ for $`y < -L`$). All error bounds above are independent of $`f`$, so $`g_N(y) \to 0`$ for Lebesgue-a.e. $`y \in \mathbb{R}`$. For $`y \le 0`$ and $`L \ge 1`$ ($`t = L + y \le L`$), Cauchy–Schwarz + Markov on $`D(t)^2`$ gives $`\frac{\sqrt{t+t^2}}{L}\sqrt{\frac{6e^{2t}}{N^2}} \le \sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{6}e^y = \sqrt{12}\,e^y`$, which multiplied by $`\mu e^{-\mu y}`$ is integrable on $`(-\infty, 0]`$ because $`1 - \mu > 0`$. For $`y > 0`$, $`\mathbb{E}[K_t]/L \le 1 + y/L \le 1 + y`$, integrable against $`\mu e^{-\mu y}`$ on $`[0, \infty)`$ because $`\mu > 0`$. Scalar DCT on $`g_N(y)`$ gives $`\sup_{|f|\le 1} |\frac{N^\mu}{L} M_N(f) - A \frac{1}{L}\int_0^L P_u^0 f(J)\,du| \le \int_{\mathbb{R}} \mu e^{-\mu y} g_N(y)\,dy \to 0`$, where $`\int_{\mathbb{R}} \mu e^{-\mu y} \mathbb{P}_\beta(W > e^{-y})\,dy = \mathbb{E}_\beta[W^\mu] = A > 0`$. Setting $`f \equiv 1`$ gives $`d_N \sim A N^{-\mu}\log N`$, and dividing gives total-variation convergence of $`\nu_N = M_N/d_N`$. **Verified.**

6. **Log-volume limit (eq. (2)):**
   In $`J`$, ordinary size $`m(u)`$ has exact birth rate $`m - 1/D_{\mathrm{top}} \in [m - 1/2, m) \subset [m-1, m]`$, sandwiched between $`1 + Y_1(u)`$ and $`Y_2(u)`$ (rate-$`1`$ Yule processes from $`1`$ and $`2`$). Both $`e^{-u} Y_1(u)`$ and $`e^{-u} Y_2(u)`$ have a.s. finite positive limits, so $`\frac{\log m(u)}{u} \to 1`$ a.s., hence $`\frac{\log m(LV)}{L} \to V \sim \mathrm{Unif}[0,1]`$ a.s., transferred to $`\nu_N`$ by TV convergence. **Verified.**
