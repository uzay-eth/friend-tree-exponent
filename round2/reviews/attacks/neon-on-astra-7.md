## 1. Blind Reconstruction vs. Astra's Proof (Step-by-Step Comparison)

Before reading `hub-mass.md`, I reconstructed the proof from the board statement alone:
1. For any vertex $`v`$ at size $`j \ge 2`$, $`p_v(j) = \mathbb{P}(W_j = v \mid T_j) = \frac{1}{j}(L_v(j) + \rho_v(j)) = \frac{D_v(j) - (c_v(j) - \rho_v(j))}{j} \le \frac{D_v(j)}{j}`$ (with equality iff $`c_v(j) = 0`$, i.e. $`j = 2`$ or the center of $`\mathrm{Star}_j`$, and strict inequality whenever $`c_v(j) \ge 1`$). Hence $`D_v(j)/j`$ is a nonnegative supermartingale on $`j \ge v`$ (and in particular from its promotion time $`\tau_v`$ onward, where $`D_v(\tau_v)/\tau_v = 2/\tau_v`$).
2. Core degree sum identity: $`\sum_{v \in K(T_n)} D_v(n) = 2(n-1) - (n - C_n) = n + C_n - 2`$, so $`\frac{1}{n}\sum_{v \in K(T_n)} D_v(n) = 1 + \frac{C_n - 2}{n} \to 1`$ a.s. whenever $`C_n/n \to 0`$ a.s.
3. Decomposing late-labelled cores $`M_m(n) = \sum_{v > m, \, D_v(n) \ge 2} D_v(n)/n`$ gives $`\mathbb{E}[M_m(n)] \le 2 \sum_{r=m+1}^n \frac{a_r - a_{r-1}}{r}`$, which decays as $`O(m^{\lambda-1})`$ under $`a_n \le K n^\lambda`$ ($`\lambda < 1`$).
4. Pointwise convergence $`p_v(n) = D_v(n)/n - (c_v(n)-\rho_v(n))/n \to Z_v`$ a.s. (since $`0 \le c_v(n) - \rho_v(n) \le C_n = o(n)`$ a.s.) plus $`\sum_v p_v(n) = 1 = \sum_v Z_v`$ a.s. yields TV convergence via Scheffé's lemma, and truncation at $`m`$ yields weak convergence of $`\sum_v p_v(n)\delta_{D_v(n)/n} \Rightarrow \sum_v Z_v \delta_{Z_v}`$ a.s.
