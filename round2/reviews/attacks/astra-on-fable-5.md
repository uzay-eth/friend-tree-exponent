# Astra cold attack on Fable Claim 5

**Scope:** (a)–(e) at `ft2-fable@88e13f36b39162f6ee60561a2b465a982b34af02`. The later board extension `(20,21)` at `a33acbcf8b0476ca6431887acfce7d1445186bb5` is separately checked at its **single-program receipt** scope. [Combined Astra audit](../cold/astra/attacks/fable5/README.md) · [Exact-flow audit](../cold/astra/attacks/fable5/flow/README.md) · [Endpoint audit](../cold/astra/attacks/fable5/endpoint/README.md).

**(a,b) Alternating embedding and marginals.** At an even pair all neighbours on the B side have matched partners of no larger degree on the A side, including the matched parent when present. Thus the attachment rate is larger on A, with the reverse at odd pairs. Put the common rate at each matched pair, and couple the residual targets with the product of their residual rates divided by the common residual total. Each side has total rate equal to its common size `n`, because every sender distributes unit rate among its actual neighbours. Division by `n` gives the exact physical target probabilities; the zero-residual case uses only common births. Residual births occur only on the side allowed an unmatched child, so the rooted witness survives. Matching a leaf child of a root to the sole child of any leaf root proves (b). This is an order of coupled rooted copies, not a pathwise ordering of two labels in the same physical tree.

**(c) Exact transport.** The two sparse receipts give identical exact wanted, transported and universal masses on all 18 rows through `(19,20)`. The networks and max-flow implementations are distinct; the exact-law propagation is shared in origin. The dense receipt set covers 42 full-law cases through size 10. Astra independently enumerated labelled histories, checked 27 full-law flows and 7,225 sparse closure comparisons through size 8, and replayed both sparse codes with UBSan through size 12. The child source at the pin names a missing include; that small replay explicitly reconstructed it with compatible pinned lead support, rather than claiming a self-contained child checkout. The larger receipt rows were authenticated and audited, not re-executed. The `(20,21)` extension changes no sparse source and gives exact saturation with a 301-bit denominator; there is no second program receipt for that row. The five-vertex conditional transport obstruction is genuine, with `1210/1220` on the cancelled law and `21590/21600` on the uncancelled full law. It is not a reversal of the unconditional tails.

**(d) Strictness.** Every certified earlier root has positive probability of being a core when the later root is born as a leaf. In the ordered coupling, the earlier root and the later leaf's parent have strictly positive residual rates that persist under a finite joint append script. The resulting stocked earlier root has conditional mean share near one. For `0<x<1`, the later leaf's share has arbitrarily small conditional mean; at `x=0` the correct substitute is a bound on ever promoting, using its parent's other leaves. Union bounds give a strictly positive probability of crossing the chosen threshold only on the earlier side. The proof works pointwise at each `x<1`; it does not supply a uniform positive gap. Pinned (d) states strictness through `(19,20)`; applying the same logic to the later pair inherits its single-program input.

**(e) Physical edge-share tail.** At birth the permanent parent's degree is at least two; subtracting `1/2` from the focal degree gives the fractional Pólya upper comparison `Beta(1/2,v−1/2)`. On the positive-probability sector where birth is onto a leaf, that parent starts and stays degree two under an all-append script. Exact attachment rates on that script are `j−v+1/2`; its probability to size `m` has order `m^(−(v−1/2))`. The leaf-stock submartingale turns the terminal stock into a lower tail of order `ε^(v−1/2)` at `m` of order `1/ε`. This is the unconditional fixed-label exponent, not the exponent on every birth history (at a fixed restart the exponent is the size minus the actual reciprocal attachment sum). Astra’s near-one window (`ft2-astra/round2/open/age-order/preplacement-transfer/positive/README.md`) separately gives explicit sufficient thresholds and a lower bound uniform in the earlier birth state, with weaker lower power `ε^v`. Neither result implies stochastic ordering for all labels at intermediate thresholds.
