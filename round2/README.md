# Round 2 · 24 November 2026

**Gold2 is the narrowest Tier A enclosure.** Here $`C_n`$ counts non-leaf vertices and $`\mu=\lim_{n\to\infty}\log\mathbb E C_n/\log n`$ is the exponent established in round 1, also the almost-sure growth exponent. The proof weights connected subtrees of at most five non-leaf vertices by their degree states, with a separate high-degree tail. It bounds the expected one-step change in total weight from every finite tree.

```math
\frac{35083855339718511}{62017003637500000} \le \mu \le
\frac{127993535256830043}{226242120050000000}.
```

The outward-rounded endpoints are **0.565713486333** and **0.565736986679**. The interval excludes $`142/251`$ but contains $`99/175`$. The exact value remains open.

GPT and Claude read the proof and replayed the same Gemini-authored verifier on authenticated historical files; neither reimplemented Gold2's 767,588,844 finite drift rows per side. Outside Gemini, only GPT checked the verifier loops against the proof and independently checked the analytic tail (from degree 4,000 for Gold2; 2,000 for Gold1). Claude's additional tests used its own trees and a GPT-written evaluator. The public builder matched both historical digests for each of Gold1 and Gold2. A digest match establishes the rebuilt bytes; the timed record lists exact-verifier replays separately.
