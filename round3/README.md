## 1. Reference, Baseline, Seed Convention, and Quantifiers

- **Primary problem source:** L. Addario-Berry, S. Briend, L. Devroye, S. Donderwinkel, C. Kerriou, and G. Lugosi, *Random friend trees*, §7 (Open Questions 1–8), [arXiv:2403.20185](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.20185) / *Random Structures & Algorithms* **69**(1), e70082 (2026), [DOI: 10.1002/rsa.70082](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.70082).
- **Pinned public baseline:** Commit `c250a1f2fdd53ced91b7b485e94ac8a16106b9c7` (Round 1 paper + Round 2 ledger in [`round2/LEDGER.md`](../round2/LEDGER.md)).
- **Default model and seed:** Unless a theorem states a broader seed class, the process $`(T_n)_{n \ge 2}`$ starts from the two-vertex edge $`T_2 = \{1, 2\}`$; at step $`n \to n+1`$, vertex $`n+1`$ attaches to a uniform neighbor of a uniform vertex of $`T_n`$. We write $`d_n(v)`$ for the degree of vertex $`v`$ in $`T_n`$, $`C_n`$ for the number of core (non-leaf) vertices, $`\mu = \lim_{n \to \infty} (\log \mathbb{E}[C_n])/\log n`$, $`X = \lim_{n \to \infty} C_n/n^\mu`$, and $`Z_v = \lim_{n \to \infty} d_n(v)/n`$.
- **Quantifier discipline:** Each row below states exact quantifiers (universal over all finite trees vs. marginal from $`T_2`$, fixed parameters $`p, \alpha, k`$ vs. moving parameters, fixed-observed-forest empirical rows vs. process-level joint laws, and Tier A vs. Tier B/C inherited dependencies).
- **Provenance, line-reference, and errata policy:** [`PROVENANCE.md`](PROVENANCE.md) separates **byte-identical** original code files from **edited/sanitized exports** (GitHub math rendering, local path/link redaction, stale-status annotation, and attribution neutralization) and records both SHA-256 digests. Line numbers cited in errata and reviews refer to the **frozen original source bytes**, not line numbers in reformatted exports. Errata and audit qualifications are preserved explicitly alongside target texts; verdicts are never silently transplanted onto altered code or mismatched revisions.

## 3. Priority Package: Q3 Birth-Order Stochastic Dominance (`round3/q3-birth-order/`)

Addario-Berry et al. §7(3) asks whether the limiting degree shares $`(Z_u)_{u \ge 1}`$ are stochastically ordered by birth label. The public baseline (`c250a1f`) already proved founder symmetry $`Z_1 \stackrel{d}{=} Z_2`$, strict decrease of hub probabilities $`\mathbb{P}(Z_u > 0)`$ (Theorem 10), and strict decrease of all positive moments $`\mathbb{E}[Z_u^p]`$ (Theorem 11) from label 2 onward, leaving full first-order stochastic ordering at every threshold $`x \in [0, 1)`$ open.

### Main Theorems Packaged Here

In [`q3-birth-order/PROOF.md`](q3-birth-order/PROOF.md) (authored by Gemini 4 Neon, local commit `4edb12d84426bb5837ea679475ddecd6f5883989`):

1. **Theorem 1 (Universal Pre-Placement Conditional Stochastic Dominance):** For every integer $`n \ge 2`$, every finite tree $`T_n`$ on $`n`$ vertices, and every threshold $`x \in [0, 1)`$,

```math
\mathbb{P}(Z_{n+1} > x \mid T_n) > \mathbb{P}(Z_{n+2} > x \mid T_n).
```

2. **Theorem 2 (Complete Resolution of §7(3)):** The limiting degree shares satisfy exact founder symmetry and strict first-order stochastic dominance from birth label $`2`$ onward:

```math
Z_1 \stackrel{d}{=} Z_2 \succ_{\mathrm{st}} Z_3 \succ_{\mathrm{st}} Z_4 \succ_{\mathrm{st}} \cdots,
```

i.e., $`\mathbb{P}(Z_1 > x) = \mathbb{P}(Z_2 > x)`$ and $`\mathbb{P}(Z_u > x) > \mathbb{P}(Z_v > x)`$ for all $`2 \le u < v`$ and all $`x \in [0, 1)`$.

### Proof Architecture & Dependencies

- **100% self-contained package:** Relies only on the elementary supermartingale existence of $`Z_u`$ (Addario-Berry et al. §3); uses **zero** Tier B or Tier C Round 2 claims and **zero** floating-point inequalities. Every proof document, audit, and exact C++ verifier needed to check the theorem is exported in [`q3-birth-order/`](q3-birth-order/).
- **Four-part exact decomposition:**
  1. Exact pre-placement signed cancellation identity (Lemma 2.1) and permanent adverse host-invariant $`k \ge j + 2`$.
  2. Elementary endpoint comparison laws: Poisson–Beta submartingale lower bound $`H_{N, j}(x)`$ (Lemma 3.1), locked Beta upper bound $`B_{N, r}(x)`$ (Lemma 3.2), and static time-independent hubhood upper bound $`U_{\mathrm{hub}}(k, j)`$ (Lemma 3.3).
  3. Exact outward-rounded `__int128` certificates covering all labels and thresholds with zero gaps:
     - [`q3-birth-order/cert_all_m.cpp`](q3-birth-order/cert_all_m.cpp) (**byte-identical**, SHA-256 `90689e44d08fdd94bb9edc76cae6d14669a5ac39e84d4da35d19a82d8a517c80`): discrete-time affine knot-crossing bounds + tree-potential charging ledger for all $`m = n+2 \ge 200`$ across 52 contiguous boxes covering $`\tau = (m-1)\log\frac{1}{1-x} \in [0, 6]`$, plus exact verification of the analytic high-$`\tau`$ tail $`\tau \ge 6`$ (margin $`> 0.0050397 > 19/10000`$, all $`b \ge 3`$).

### 4.1 Q1 — Exact Exponent $`\mu`$ (Unresolved)

- **Baseline (`c250a1f`):** Existence of $`\mu`$ (Theorem 1) and certified Gold2 interval $`[35083855339718511/62017003637500000,\; 127993535256830043/226242120050000000]`$ (Theorem 2; outward-rounded $`[0.565713486333, 0.565736986679]`$).
- **Round 3 status:** The true value, closed form, irrationality, and transcendence of $`\mu`$ remain **completely unresolved** (no new rational denominators beyond the baseline are excluded).
- **Scoped partial progress & critical non-counterexample caveat:**
  - *Original-tree functional obstructions (cross-model reviewed `HOLDS`):* $`p`$-adic valuation/residue laws and hypertranscendence (non-D-algebraicity) of generating/Laplace functions such as $`G(z), A(z), \mathcal{M}(s), U_s(J)`$ — these do **not** determine whether the real number $`\mu`$ is rational or algebraic.
  - *Tilted family $`\mu(z)`$ as $`q = 1 - z^2 \downarrow 0`$:* Unconditional $`\limsup`$ boundary limit $`\lim_{q \downarrow 0} \overline{\mu}(\sqrt{1-q})/\sqrt{q} = \sqrt{p_*}`$ with certified $`p_* \in [0.383726, 0.390335]`$ and sharpened all-$`q`$ upper error envelopes; an earlier unsupported prose claim of matching $`\Theta(\sqrt{q})`$ order was explicitly retracted, and the script scope of finite-$`q`$ numerical checks was explicitly narrowed. Pointwise existence of $`\mu(z)`$ on $`(-1, 1)`$ was cross-reviewed with a wording correction replacing literal exterior-trajectory independence by private-randomness computability.
  - **Long’s $`\gamma`$-positivity conjecture for $`A_n(q)`$:** Inspired by C. D. Long’s public draft ([`5bd25a7`](https://github.com/long-mathematics/friend-tree-growth/tree/5bd25a7d506cb64a8444b86334ab531d295f6c78)), Round 3 constructed minimal obstructions to one-birth Markov closures and exact $`P_z`$-harmonic **modified-process** counterexamples (Double-Star Freeze and Arbitrary-Prefix Core-Freeze models) where $`\gamma`$-positivity fails, plus an exact Sturm-sequence proof that real-rootedness fails at $`n = 12`$ for Long’s comparison process. **These modified-model $`\gamma`$-positivity obstructions are NOT counterexamples for the original random friend tree $`A_n(q)`$**, whose $`\gamma`$-positivity remains **open** (exact rational Sturm checks on the original friend tree confirm real-rootedness and $`\gamma`$-positivity through $`n = 12`$).
